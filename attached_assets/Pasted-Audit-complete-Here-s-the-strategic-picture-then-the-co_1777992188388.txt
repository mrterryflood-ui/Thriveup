PHASE 1 — Foundation (unlocks everything else)
Build:

Multilingual app shell: every UI label translated into top 12 languages. Browser-locale auto-detect. "What language feels most like home?" picker on first launch.
Newcomer Onboarding journey: 5-screen warm welcome. "How long have you been in the US?" / "What do you need most right now?" / "Who else needs this with you?" — outputs a personalized starter pack (phrase boards, language prefs, suggested resources).
ThriveUp wiring:

Sends newcomer_onboarded event to hub with anonymized demographics (language, county, primary need) → feeds into ecosystem demand-signal RAG AI.
Pulls regional resource recommendations from RAG AI based on the user's region.
Stakeholders served: Every newcomer. Every language. Every age.

Grant alignment: ✅ Foster belonging + reduce barriers to participation.

PHASE 2 — Access (critical-services expansion)
Build:

Immigrant Phrase Board Pack (6 boards, all 12 languages preloaded): ER & Medical · School & Parents · Housing & Landlord · Workplace & Wages · Police/ICE Encounter · Government Forms (DMV/SSA/USCIS).
Document Explainer: photo or upload a letter → translation + plain-language explanation + action items + draft response.
Know Your Rights cards (printable + offline): ICE encounters, traffic stops, workplace, tenant. Sourced from ACLU/NILC/IDP.
Offline-first critical phrases cached in service worker per language.
ThriveUp wiring:

Document explainer fires translation_requests events with anonymized topic (medical/legal/school) → ecosystem analytics show what immigrants are actually navigating.
Crisis-flagged documents (eviction, deportation notice) trigger optional warm_handoff to legal aid partners.
Stakeholders served: Adults navigating institutions. Anyone who's just received scary mail.

Grant alignment: ✅ Expand access to critical services. ✅ Reduce barriers.

PHASE 3 — Learning + Gamification: "Belonging Path" (NEW capability, foundational for the grant story)
This is where you become a language-access AND learning platform.

Build:

Belonging Path: a gamified journey, not an ESL curriculum. The framing matters — "You don't need to fix your English. You need the world to meet you halfway, and you need to know how the world works here."
Three Tracks:
🏥 Health & Safety (ER vocabulary, calling 911, prescription pickup, mental health resources)
🏫 Family & School (parent-teacher meetings, IEP basics, US grading, lunch programs, discipline rights)
💼 Work & Money (job interviews, wage rights, taxes, banking, tenant rights)
Mechanics (built on existing learned_expressions table):
XP — earned for using the interpreter, completing a phrase board scenario, helping a community vocabulary submission
Streaks — "5 days bridging" doesn't shame you for missing days; says "Welcome back, ready when you are"
Belonging Badges — Newcomer · Connector · Parent · Voice · Bridge-Builder · Community Anchor
Confidence levels instead of fluency levels — you're not graded on English, you're celebrated for showing up
Cultural exchange units — 2-way: a unit on Texas culture for newcomers, AND a unit on the user's culture that they can share with neighbors
ThriveUp wiring:

New table learning_progress (userId, track, unit, completedAt, xpEarned, badgeId)
Sends learning_milestone events to hub → feeds Outcomes dashboard → shows up in grant evidence reports (e.g., "847 ESL-equivalent learning hours delivered to immigrant communities in Travis County in Q3").
Hub can push "community challenges" (e.g., during Citizenship Day: 2x XP for the Civics unit).
Stakeholders served: Adults learning, kids tracking parents' progress with pride, schools/employers viewing aggregate (consent-based) outcomes.

Grant alignment: ✅ Language access programs. ✅ Workforce readiness. ✅ Long-term stability + economic mobility.

PHASE 4 — Family Circles (every stakeholder in the home)
Build:

Family Circle: link 2-6 accounts. Roles: Adult, Helper (older teen/young adult), Child, Elder.
Caregiver Mode 2.0: An adult child can review and add context to messages BEFORE they go to a doctor/landlord/teacher — but parent stays in the driver's seat. Explicit framing: "Helper, not translator. You support — they decide."
Child-safe Parent Assist: kids under 13 see ONLY a "Help Mom/Dad" button — no exposure to crisis content, eviction notices, medical details. They get to feel useful without being parentified.
Elder voice preservation: record grandma's voice once → AI TTS uses her voice for messages she sends. Dignity for elders who feel diminished by typing.
Family belonging timeline: shared (consent-based) view of "what we've navigated together" — appointments, school events, accomplishments.
ThriveUp wiring:

New tables family_circles, family_circle_members, family_circle_consents.
Family events (parent completed a school meeting, elder sent a doctor message) optionally show in shared timeline.
Routes to ThriveUp if a family member wants to learn the platform itself.
Stakeholders served: Parents. Kids (now seen as helpers, not interpreters). Elders (voice preserved). Caregivers.

Grant alignment: ✅ Foster belonging + trust. ✅ Long-term stability. (And it's the storytelling spine for the grant narrative.)

PHASE 5 — Community Weave (the ecosystem really kicks in)
Build:

Org & Agency Directory: legal aid, refugee resettlement (IRC, USCRI, Catholic Charities, HIAS), schools (Manor ISD, AISD, Pflugerville ISD), free clinics, food banks, mutual aid, sanctuary congregations. Verified, multilingual, with hours/eligibility.
Mentor Match: opt-in pairing — a Year-3 immigrant from Honduras can mentor a newly-arrived family from Honduras. Async, light-touch, consent-driven, language-matched.
Voices of Austin v2: extend the existing storytelling page with audio/video story circles, dialect-preserved transcripts, and curated themes ("First job in Austin," "Getting my kids in school," "Becoming a citizen").
Org-side mini-portal: verified orgs can view consent-based aggregate demand signals ("47 families in 78753 need school enrollment help this week") and post resources.
ThriveUp wiring:

Uses existing Warm Handoff protocol to send a user's Communication Profile (dialect, accessibility needs, primary language, current situation) to the receiving org — no re-explaining.
Uses existing Evidence Registry to track org partnerships (verifiable proof for grant reports).
Org directory feeds the RAG AI so it can recommend specific resources mid-conversation ("Sounds like you're being evicted. Texas RioGrande Legal Aid has a free hotline in Spanish at...").
Stakeholders served: Orgs (visibility + warm referrals + outcome data). Mentors (purpose). Mentees (a real person, not just an app). Storytellers (cultural preservation).

Grant alignment: ✅ All four priorities, plus directly meets "navigation support for housing, health, education, employment."

PHASE 6 — Ecosystem Amplify (make the impact legible to funders)
Build:

Immigrant Outcomes Dashboard (extends existing Outcomes page): tracks belonging metrics, learning hours, services accessed, families connected, stories preserved, mentor pairs, warm handoffs completed, crisis interventions — all RPLICE-compliant.
Grant Evidence Reports: one-click generation of verifiable evidence packets for the FY26 Equity Mini Grant + WIOA + St. David's + future grants.
Public Impact Page (/our-impact): non-PII aggregated impact for community trust.
Hub-driven program campaigns: ThriveUp Hub can push "Citizenship Day Challenge" / "Back-to-School Family Drive" / "Tax Season Support Sprint" — Talk Your Talk participates as a node.
ThriveUp wiring:

Outcomes dashboard pulls from heartbeat metrics + new learning_progress + family_circles tables.
Each new outcome category becomes a queryable evidence slug (/api/ecosystem/evidence/immigrant-belonging, /family-navigation, /learning-hours).
Hub-issued directives (e.g., "Increase Spanish-language onboarding by 40% by Q1") flow into the Work Queue and back as evidence.
Stakeholders served: Funders, City of Austin Equity Office, partner orgs, and most importantly — the community, who can see their own collective impact reflected back.

Grant alignment: ✅ Reporting backbone for the grant itself + future grants.
