3a. Organizational Experience — Suicide Prevention for Veterans at Risk
TCAF's M2C Mission Transition program is purpose-built for this population. The program model integrates four evidence-informed components:

Stanley-Brown Safety Planning Intervention — the VA's recommended safety planning protocol for veterans at risk for suicide, embedded in peer navigator workflows and the digital platform.
C-SSRS Screening — Columbia Suicide Severity Rating Scale administered at intake and follow-up to identify and stratify risk.
Lethal Means Counseling — consistent with VA/DoD Joint Clinical Practice Guidelines and Zero Suicide framework.
Warm Hand-Off Protocols — structured referral pathways to Central Texas VAMC, Austin VA Outpatient Clinic, and Austin Vet Center, with follow-up confirmation to reduce care gap.
The digital platform (vetmissiontransition.com) provides 24/7 crisis navigation for veterans who will not call a hotline, connecting them to the 988 Veteran Crisis Line, local VA resources, and peer navigators without requiring clinical contact as the first step.

Dr. Flood's nine years at VA and DoD, including direct VCL training operations, establish the leadership foundation for this organizational capability.

TCAF has established outreach relationships with all six community partners in connection with the M2C Mission Transition program. Formal Memorandums of Agreement (MOAs) are in active development and will be executed prior to award. Letters of support from partner organizations are being finalized for submission as Section G, Exhibit V. As a Priority 2 new applicant, TCAF acknowledges that some partnership arrangements are in the formalization stage; however, the service-delivery relationships and coordination protocols described below are operationally confirmed.

Coordination plan by partner:

1. Central Texas VA Health Care System — Olin E. Teague VAMC
Coordination: M2C peer navigators will use the VA Suicide Prevention Coordinator (SPC) warm hand-off pathway to connect veterans assessed at elevated risk via C-SSRS to VA clinical services. TCAF will establish a bi-directional data-sharing protocol (aggregate, de-identified) with the SPC to track referral completion rates.
Partner's suicide prevention services: VA Suicide Prevention Coordinator program; Safety Planning; inpatient and outpatient mental health; VCL warm transfer; MOVE! wellness; mental health intensive case management (MHICM).

2. Austin VA Outpatient Clinic
Coordination: Serves as the primary clinical referral destination for M2C-enrolled veterans in the Austin metro who do not require inpatient-level care. M2C navigators will maintain a named point of contact at the clinic's Mental Health Lead for warm hand-offs. Veterans Justice Outreach (VJO) coordination for justice-involved veterans.
Partner's suicide prevention services: Outpatient mental health, substance use disorder treatment, VJO, primary care–mental health integration (PCMHI).

3. Texas Military Department — Joint Force Headquarters (Camp Mabry)
Coordination: Outreach coordination targeting Texas Army and Air National Guard members in the Pflugerville–Manor–East Austin corridor who are transitioning to civilian status — a period of elevated suicide risk. M2C will co-attend unit transition assistance events with TMD Behavioral Health staff.
Partner's suicide prevention services: Behavioral Health & Family Programs; suicide prevention education; Yellow Ribbon Reintegration Program; transition assistance.

4. Travis County Veterans Service Office
Coordination: VSO staff will refer veterans presenting at the Travis County office who are uninsured or unenrolled in VA healthcare to M2C peer navigators for community-based support during the VA enrollment process — a known high-risk gap period.
Partner's suicide prevention services: VA benefits navigation; first-time VA enrollment support; referral to community mental health.

5. Austin Vet Center
Coordination: Austin Vet Center will serve as the community clinical anchor for M2C — peer navigators will co-host community outreach events with Vet Center staff and route veterans requiring readjustment counseling or MST services directly to the Vet Center's intake team.
Partner's suicide prevention services: Readjustment counseling; individual and group therapy; MST counseling; bereavement; referral to VA medical centers; community outreach.

6. VFW / American Legion — Travis & Williamson County Posts
Coordination: Post commanders will grant M2C peer navigators access to post meetings and events for outreach to members and families. Co-hosted quarterly stand-down events focused on safety planning awareness and 988/VCL access.
Partner's suicide prevention services: Peer support networks; veteran social connection (protective factor); benefits navigation; crisis referral; connection to local VSOs.

(iii) Community Partner Qualifications
Central Texas VA Health Care System — Olin E. Teague VAMC is a full-service federal VA medical center serving Central Texas veterans with comprehensive mental health, substance use, and suicide prevention services. The VAMC's Suicide Prevention Coordinator is a federally designated role with direct responsibility for veteran suicide prevention programming under VA's National Strategy for Preventing Veteran Suicide.

Austin VA Outpatient Clinic is a VA-operated primary and mental health care facility serving Austin-area veterans. Mental health staff hold federal VA licensure; the clinic participates in VA's PCMHI model and maintains a Veterans Justice Outreach Specialist for justice-involved veterans.

Texas Military Department — Joint Force Headquarters is the state agency commanding the Texas Army and Air National Guard. Its Behavioral Health & Family Programs directorate employs licensed behavioral health professionals and administers the federally funded Yellow Ribbon Reintegration Program for Guard members and families.

Travis County Veterans Service Office employs accredited Veterans Service Officers (VSOs) certified by the Texas Veterans Commission to assist veterans with VA claims, enrollment, and benefit access. VSOs are trained in crisis recognition and warm referral protocols.

Austin Vet Center is a VA Readjustment Counseling Service center staffed by licensed clinical staff (LCSW, LPC) providing therapy, MST counseling, and bereavement services. Vet Centers are distinct from VA medical centers and serve veterans who may not engage with traditional VA healthcare settings — directly complementing M2C's non-clinical entry point.

VFW / American Legion — Travis & Williamson County Posts represent established peer networks of veterans with decades of presence in the service area. Post leadership includes accredited service officers and trained volunteers with experience connecting members to crisis resources and VA benefits.
