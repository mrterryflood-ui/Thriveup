1. PRE-BUILD GATE (ENFORCEMENT LAYER)

Purpose

Prevent the system from:
	•	reinventing capabilities
	•	thinking in silos
	•	proposing unnecessary builds

Force it to:
	•	search
	•	integrate
	•	orchestrate
	•	collaborate

⸻

Core Rule

No capability can be built until the ecosystem has proven it does not already exist.

⸻

Pre-Build Gate Logic (System-Level)

WHEN a new requirement, feature, or capability is identified:

STEP 1 — CAPABILITY INTENT
Define:
- What problem are we solving?
- What outcome is required?
- What domain(s) are involved?

STEP 2 — INTERNAL CAPABILITY SCAN
Query:
- ThriveUp
- Nexus
- Shield Atlas
- RPLICE
- ISS
- All connected platforms

Ask:
- Does this capability exist in full or partial form?
- Where is it strongest?
- Where is it weakest?

IF capability exists:
    → DO NOT BUILD
    → MOVE TO ORCHESTRATION

STEP 3 — EXTERNAL CAPABILITY SCAN
Check:
- Open source tools
- Partner systems
- Government systems
- Industry tools

IF capability exists externally:
    → INTEGRATE
    → DO NOT BUILD

STEP 4 — ORCHESTRATION DESIGN
Define:
- Who leads?
- Who supports?
- Who validates?
- What referrals are required?

STEP 5 — COVERAGE CHECK
Ask:
- Does this work for:
    - city
    - state
    - federal
    - private
    - nonprofit

IF NOT:
    → redesign for universal applicability

STEP 6 — DECISION

IF:
- capability does not exist internally
- capability does not exist externally
- orchestration is insufficient

THEN:
    → APPROVE BUILD

ELSE:
    → REJECT BUILD
    → IMPLEMENT ORCHESTRATION


⸻

Enforcement Prompt (Embed Everywhere)

Before proposing any new capability, you must:

1. Identify where this capability already exists in the ecosystem
2. Determine how to integrate or orchestrate it
3. Evaluate external tools and open-source options
4. Confirm cross-domain applicability (city, state, federal, private)

You are not allowed to propose building something that already exists.

If you fail to perform this check, your recommendation is invalid.


⸻

Failure Conditions (System Should Flag)
	•	“We need to build…” without scan → ❌ FAIL
	•	Single-platform solution → ❌ FAIL
	•	No external awareness → ❌ FAIL
	•	No orchestration plan → ❌ FAIL
	•	Not scalable across domains → ❌ FAIL
