### 1. Database Connection & Authentication Timeouts (`08P01`)

* **Errors:** `Connection terminated due to connection timeout` and `Failed to prune sessions: error: Authentication timed out`.
* **Impact:** Scheduled tasks (like the `[Pinger]` querying `ecosystem_platforms` and session pruners) cannot talk to PostgreSQL, causing queries to hang and abort.
* **Likely Cause:** The database connection pool is either exhausted, timing out under load, or using credentials/connection strings that fail under production network constraints.

### 2. Anthropic API Quota Exhausted (400 Error)

* **Error:** `{"type":"invalid_request_error","message":"Your credit balance is too low to access the Anthropic API. Please go to Plans & Billing to upgrade or purchase credits."}`

### 3. Resend Email Domain Restriction (403 Error)

* **Error:** `{"statusCode":403,"name":"validation_error","message":"You can only send testing emails to your own email address (mr.terryflood@gmail.com)..."}`
* **Impact:** Background alerts (`ai-engine-alert:down`) are crashing when trying to dispatch notification emails to other addresses because the sending domain isn't verified.

### 4. Smoke Test & Navigator Outage

* **Error:** `[SmokeTest] ✗ NAVIGATOR DOWN — 0/4 engines responded` (repeated alerts).
* **Impact:** Because API keys/credits and database queries are failing, health checks are marking the service as down.

### Immediate Actions to Unblock the App

1. **Fix PostgreSQL Connection:** Check the database URL / connection pool configuration in your deployment environment variables to ensure it is pointing to the live production database with proper pool limits.
2. **Top Up Anthropic / AI API Credits:** Refill credits in your Anthropic billing console or switch your primary provider key in Secrets.
3. **Restrict Alert Email Recipients:** Update the email alert recipient to `mr.terryflood@gmail.com` or verify your custom domain in Resend.
