More FY 2026 STEM Ed Funding Opportunities and Resources

This listing of current NSF STEM Ed funding is only slightly redundant with my recent post. Check the guidelines for details and due dates. Look within the guidelines for how to contact program staff and submit questions or concept papers. You will find a listing of NSF proposal development resources at the end of this post.

Fostering Interdisciplinary Networks to Develop Emergent and Responsive Solutions: Supports interdisciplinary partnerships among educators, technologists and researchers to co-design and scale evidence-based, technology-driven solutions — particularly those involving AI — that strengthen K–12 learning and help build a skilled workforce.

https://lnkd.in/e62-VqKN: Planning Proposals (May 27, 2026); Development Proposals (Nov. 18, 2026)

NSF STEM K-12: Supports research and development to advance STEM teaching and learning in K-12 formal and informal settings, leveraging AI and emerging technologies to build knowledge, create tools and strengthen the U.S. STEM education and workforce.
https://lnkd.in/eHg7QTWp 
No deadlines: Submit any time.

Advanced Technological Education: Supports partnerships between two-year institutions of higher education, other academic institutions, industry and other entities to improve the education of technicians in science and engineering.
https://lnkd.in/e9af3uzB
Deadline: Oct. 1, 2026

IUSE: Professional Formation of Engineers, Revolutionizing Engineering Departments: Supports fundamental changes to the training of undergraduate engineering students that equip them with the technical and professional skills needed to solve complex societal problems.
https://lnkd.in/eaR4g9tY
Deadline: April 14, 2026

IUSE: EDU: Improving Undergraduate STEM Ed: For research on improving STEM teaching and learning for undergraduate students.
https://lnkd.in/euHEJFUq

Full Proposal Deadline: July 15, 2026

Advancing Quantum Education and Workforce Development: Supports students in education pathways/careers in quantum information science and engineering. For this Dear Colleague Letter, apply through existing funding opportunities. Consult the DCL and relevant funding programs.
https://lnkd.in/eymyEJpy
