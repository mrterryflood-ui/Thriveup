🚨 Federal Grant Opportunity – Youth Reentry & Workforce Development


PLEASE READ EVERYTHING!

Is your organization ready to help justice-involved youth successfully transition back into their communities and build pathways toward stable employment and long-term success?

The U.S. Department of Justice – Office of Juvenile Justice and Delinquency Prevention (OJJDP) has released funding through the FY25 Second Chance Act Youth Reentry Program to support programs helping youth leaving correctional facilities reintegrate into their communities.

• $13,650,000 available nationally
• Awards up to $750,000
• 36-month performance period
• Grants.gov Deadline: March 30, 2026
• JustGrants Deadline: April 6, 2026

This funding supports comprehensive programs that prepare justice-involved youth for education, employment, and stable community reintegration.

AREAS OF FOCUS
Programs funded under this opportunity should provide coordinated reentry services that include:

• Individualized reentry planning and case management
• Education, job training, and employment pathways
• Mentoring and supportive services
• Partnerships with community-based organizations and employers
• Programs designed to reduce recidivism and promote long-term success
• Services addressing housing, behavioral health, and family stability

Strong proposals will demonstrate evidence-based programming, measurable outcomes, and strong local partnerships.

🏷️ ELIGIBLE APPLICANTS
• State governments
• County and city governments
• Federally recognized tribal governments
• Community-based organizations partnering with government entities
• Organizations working with juvenile justice agencies

⚠️ IMPORTANT REQUIREMENTS
Before applying, organizations must have:

✔️ Active SAM.gov registration
✔️ Unique Entity Identifier (UEI)
✔️ Ability to submit through Grants.gov and JustGrants
✔️ Capacity to manage federal reporting and compliance

⚠️ Cost Match Requirement:
This funding opportunity requires a 50% cost match from the applicant.

This is a competitive federal award focused on improving outcomes for justice-involved youth and strengthening reentry systems nationwide.
