1) Wellcome × Nature — One-Page Submission Outline (recruiting + internal use)
Title (working)
Community-Sited Collaborative Care for Perinatal Depression and Anxiety: An Implementation-Science Adaptation with Multi-Vantage Fidelity, Lived-Experience Co-Design, and Sustainment Capability as a Funded Deliverable

The intervention
We are adopting Collaborative Care — the most-replicated depression-care model in psychiatry (Unützer et al. 2012 IMPACT; AIMS Center extensions 2022–2025; >90 RCTs across populations) — and adapting it for perinatal depression and anxiety delivered in community-based organizations rather than primary-care clinics. The content payload (registry-based caseload management, measurement-based stepped care, psychiatric consultation, behavioral activation / problem-solving therapy) is preserved. What changes is the implementation infrastructure: who delivers it, where, with what fidelity discipline, and what survives the funding cliff.

This is exactly the "novel intervention at an early stage of implementation" Wellcome's call describes — established content, novel delivery context, novel measurement and sustainment architecture, not an incremental advance.

Why this clears Gate 1 (RCT-grade evidence, last 5 years)
Collaborative Care core: Unützer 2012 IMPACT + continuous AIMS Center replication portfolio through 2025; multiple in-window pragmatic trials and meta-analyses.
Perinatal extension content: Mothers and Babies (Tandon group, Northwestern) — in-window RCTs of group, individual, mobile, and CHW-delivered variants; public-domain curriculum.
Endpoints already validated: PHQ-9, GAD-7, EPDS — all on Wellcome's clinical-relevance criterion (symptoms + functioning + QoL).
We are not claiming a new RCT. We are citing two converging in-window RCT lineages whose intersection is currently under-deployed.

What is novel — the TCAF Implementation Stack
The novelty Wellcome is scoring lives here, not in the content:

8-Dimensional Adaptable Treatment Fidelity (PI-authored, live in /measurement-studio). Multi-vantage — observer + recipient + community + system + mechanism-preservation under adaptation. Solves the field's monocular-fidelity problem.
Mechanism-Preservation Registry (in build). Adaptations logged against a prospectively declared mechanism map. Tells reviewers exactly which Collaborative Care core travels into the community-organization perinatal context and which does not.
Sustainment Capability Tracker (in build). The measurement capability becomes a funded deliverable at T0 — the host community organization owns the instruments by month 12 regardless of program continuation. This is the field's chronic blind spot named in Birken 2020 and operationalized for the first time as a scored gate.
Lived-experience by design. The Women's Health Canon and Voices of Austin are co-investigators on instrument selection, content adaptation, and fidelity vantages — not advisors consulted after design. Directly addresses Wellcome's lived-experience key criterion.
Community-organization siting rather than clinic-bound delivery. Closes the access gap IMPACT has historically depended on integrated health systems to bridge.
Clinically relevant endpoints
Primary: PHQ-9 reduction at 6 months (Collaborative Care's standard endpoint).
Co-primary: EPDS reduction across pregnancy + 12-month postpartum.
Secondary: GAD-7 reduction; WHO-5 well-being; functional milestones (return-to-work, breastfeeding initiation/continuation, infant well-child visit attendance).
Implementation: 8-Dim fidelity scores per vantage; Sustainment Capability Tracker at month 12 and month 24.

What we are asking Wellcome to fund
A two-year early-stage implementation evaluation across 3–5 community organizations in two states. Block-randomized rollout. Primary outcome at 6 months per site. Pre-registered mechanism map and pre-registered multi-vantage outcome lens slate. Sustainment Capability Tracker scored at month 12 (in-grant) and month 24 (post-grant), the latter funded as a deliverable, not deferred.
