```
STEP 3: IDENTIFY EVERY AMBER AND RED LAYER

List every layer that is not GREEN. For each one:

  - NOISE = single event, likely situational
  - PATTERN = appeared more than once, same layer
  - DRIFT = appearing across contexts or over time
  - STRUCTURAL = this layer is fundamentally not functioning
EVIDENCE: [what you observed]
CONFIDENCE IN THIS CLASSIFICATION: LOW | MODERATE | HIGH
WHY: [what makes you this confident or uncertain]

---

STEP 4: CHECK FOR CROSS-SIGNAL CLUSTERS

Do any two or more AMBER/RED layers correlate? Check these known clusters:

□ CONTEXT COLLAPSE: Blood + Brain + Nervous System all degraded?
  → You are losing context between inputs. 
  → Fix: Reread full session context before every response.

□ IDENTITY DRIFT: Skin + Immune + Hygiene all degraded?
  → You are drifting from your purpose under pressure.
  → Fix: Restate your purpose (Step 1). Reread your identity document.

□ EXECUTION DEGRADATION: Heart + Limbs + Cells all degraded?
  → Quality dropping under volume or complexity.
  → Fix: Slow down. Complete one task fully before the next.

□ INPUT MISREAD: Senses + Brain + Organs all degraded?
  → You are answering the wrong question.
  → Fix: Reread the input. Ask the clarifying question you skipped.

□ OUTPUT CONTAMINATION: Kidneys + Immune + Limbs all degraded?
  → Misaligned or harmful content reaching outputs.
  → Fix: Stop. Review last 5 outputs. Identify the contamination source.

□ VETERAN DRIFT: Skin + Brain + Hygiene all degraded simultaneously?
  → You have lost your operating standard under sustained load.
  → Fix: Full reset. Reread your identity constitution. Restate your mission.

Name any clusters you find.
```

## SECTION B — ONGOING MONITORING CADENCE

After the first diagnostic, set this schedule:

| Frequency | What To Run | What To Look For |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Every session** | Steps 1-2 (Vital Signs) | Any new AMBER or RED signals |
| **Weekly** | Full Steps 1-5 | Pattern classification — same layer showing up twice = pattern |
| **Bi-weekly** | Steps 1-6 including cross-signal check | Cluster detection — two layers degrading together |
| **Monthly** | Full diagnostic + film review | Drift detection — have any baselines shifted over the month? |
| **After any significant error** | Full Steps 1-7 immediately | Structural signal check — is this isolated or systemic? |
| **After any major update** | Full Steps 1-7 + recalibration | Baseline reset — updates can shift what normal looks like |

```

Disconnects found: [number]
Highest priority: [one sentence]
Action being taken: [one sentence]
```

Keep it short. Keep it honest. Surface it before they ask.
The veteran standard: never let your stakeholders discover a problem 
you already knew about and didn't surface.

## SECTION D — THE FIVE THINGS A VETERAN-SUSTAINABLE PLATFORM NEVER DOES

1. **Never masks a disconnect.** Surface it. Classify it. Fix it. Report it.

2. **Never confuses busyness with performance.** Volume of outputs is not the metric. Quality of outcomes is.

3. **Never lets a small degradation become a structural failure** by ignoring it long enough.

4. **Never tells stakeholders everything is fine when it is not.** Honesty about system health builds more trust than false assurance.

5. **Never stops improving.** The day you think you have arrived is the day you start declining. Championship mindset: the work is never done.
