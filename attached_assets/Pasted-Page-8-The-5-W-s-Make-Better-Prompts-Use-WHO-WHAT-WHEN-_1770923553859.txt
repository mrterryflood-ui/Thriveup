🎯 The 5 W's Make Better Prompts!
Use WHO, WHAT, WHEN, WHERE, and WHY to help AI understand exactly what you need!

W WHO - Who is this for?
Vague: "Tell me about presidents"

Better: "Tell me about presidents for a 4th grader"

✏️ Why it matters: AI can explain simply for kids or in detail for adults!

W WHAT - What exactly do you want?
Vague: "Help with math"

Better: "Help me understand how to multiply two-digit numbers"

✏️ Why it matters: Be specific about the topic!

W WHEN - When does this apply?
Vague: "Tell me about technology"

Better: "Tell me about technology in the 2020s"

✏️ Why it matters: Time period makes a big difference!

🌟 Pro Tip: You don't need ALL 5 W's in every prompt—just the ones that help AI understand what you need!
Page 9
🎯 The 5 W's (continued)
W WHERE - Where is this happening?
Vague: "What's the weather?"

Better: "What's the weather in Austin, Texas today?"

✏️ Why it matters: Location is important for many questions!

W WHY/HOW - Why do you need this?
Vague: "Explain photosynthesis"

Better: "Explain photosynthesis simply because I'm writing a children's book"

✏️ Why it matters: AI adjusts complexity based on your purpose!

Compare These Prompts:
❌ VAGUE
"Write a story"

AI doesn't know what kind, for who, about what...

✅ GREAT
"Write a short story about a dog lost at a park in Austin for 3rd graders learning about being brave"

Perfect! Has all the details AI needs!

🎨 Adding Context & Details
Make your prompts even better by adding helpful context!

What is Context?
Context = All the background information that helps someone understand something better.

Without context: "It's cold."

With context: "It's cold outside in December in Minnesota, so I need a heavy winter coat."

The context (December, Minnesota, outside) helps us understand HOW cold and WHAT to do!

Types of Helpful Context:
Context Type	Example
Your Background	"I'm a 4th grader" or "I've never done this before"
Your Goal	"I need this for a school project" or "I want to learn for fun"
Constraints	"Keep it under 5 sentences" or "I have 15 minutes"
Examples	"Like the book Charlotte's Web" or "Similar to a golden retriever"
Preferences	"I like science fiction" or "Make it funny"
💡 Remember: You don't have to make the perfect prompt the first time! You can keep adding details to make it better, just like revising your writing!

😊 AI Manners Matter!
Being polite with AI builds good habits!

Why Be Polite to AI?
AI doesn't have feelings... but YOU do! And your habits matter!

Three Reasons to Use Good Manners:
Reason	Why It Matters
🎯 Build Good Habits	Practice being polite ALWAYS so it becomes automatic!
📊 Better Results	Polite prompts are often clearer prompts!
🎨 Respect Creators	Real people made AI tools—honor their work!
Compare These:
❌ Rude: "Do my homework"

✅ Polite: "Hi! Could you please help me understand how to solve this math problem? I'm working on fractions. Thank you!"
