Project Concept — "RESTART Through Technology"

Project Title (Proposed)

"ThriveUp RESTART: AI-Powered Reentry Pathways to Skilled Trades & Technology Careers for Justice-Involved Youth and Young Adults"

Core Program Model

TCAF will leverage its ThriveUp Academy AI ecosystem to deliver a technology-integrated reentry workforce development program targeting justice-involved youth (15-17) and young adults (18-24) across 3+ non-contiguous metropolitan areas. The program focuses on FOA priority industries: AI infrastructure build-out, information technology including AI, and advanced manufacturing.

Three-Phase Service Delivery Model

Phase 1: Assessment & Readiness (Months 1-3)

  • Robust assessment of prior learning and skills using ThriveUp AI diagnostic tools
  • Individual Development Plan (IDP) creation with AI-assisted career pathway mapping
  • WIOA eligibility screening and co-enrollment
  • Digital literacy baseline assessment and foundational AI literacy training
  • Financial literacy training and needs assessment (repeated every 6 months for youth/young adults)
  • Criminal record review — verify participants eligible for licensure in target occupations

Phase 2: Training & Credentialing (Months 3-18)

  • Pre-apprenticeship programs in AI infrastructure, IT, and advanced manufacturing
  • Registered Apprenticeship placements with employer partners (RA sponsors)
  • On-the-Job Training (OJT) with up to 90% employer reimbursement
  • AI and digital literacy coursework integrated into all training tracks
  • Industry-recognized credential attainment (CompTIA, AWS, manufacturing certs)
  • Learning and Employment Records (LER) development where feasible
  • Paid work experiences and subsidized wages/stipends
  • Soft skills development: workplace etiquette, professionalism, job search strategies
  • Mentorship program pairing participants with industry professionals

Phase 3: Placement & Follow-Up (Months 12-42)

  • Unsubsidized employment placement in target industries
  • Registered Apprenticeship continuation and completion tracking
  • 12-month follow-up services (FOA requirement)
  • Recidivism tracking — 12-month window post-enrollment (per Amendment 1 definition)
  • Supportive services referrals and continuation
  • Credential portability through LER/CLR integration
