On the shape question (every story vs tag-gated):

Strongly recommend tag-gated. Three reasons:

The Repository's editorial frame is RPLICE — Risk → Pathway → Lever → Intervention → Cost-benefit → Evidence. Stories that don't speak to that frame would dilute the inline panel and the AI Analyst's grounding pool.
Cleaner queryability. Both the inline RPLICE Explorer panel and the planned AI Analyst citation flow filter on tags, topics, and geographies[].state. A curated tag means our reads stay sharp.
Easier to widen than narrow later. If we start with everything, we end up doing janitorial work to dedupe and demote.
Suggested tag vocabulary (you pick the canonical set, but here's a starting menu that maps cleanly to what we already publish):

gun-violence — direct firearm-injury or fatality content
social-determinants — poverty, housing, employment, healthcare access
mental-health — adult depression, ACEs, school counselor capacity
policy — state law, federal grants, local ordinance
youth-violence — under-18 victims/perpetrators, school context
community-impact — first-person or local-org narratives tied to one of the above
evidence — pieces grounded in a specific federal dataset (FBI CDE, CDC WONDER, Census ACS, BLS LAUS, BRFSS)
rplice-aligned — meta-tag for anything you've explicitly mapped to a Risk/Pathway/Lever/Intervention chain
Send any story carrying ≥1 of those tags. Always include geographies[] (at minimum a state) when the story has a location — that's how we surface it on the right map and Explorer view.
