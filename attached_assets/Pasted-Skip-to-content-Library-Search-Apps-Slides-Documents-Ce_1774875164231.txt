Appendix: Technical Specifications
1. Executive Summary
We are pleased to submit this capability packet in response to Solicitation #2603-002 for the Central Health Compensation Management System. Our solution is a fully operational, enterprise-grade compensation intelligence platform purpose-built for Travis County Healthcare District's unique mission of serving 300,000+ residents through equitable, competitive, and transparent workforce compensation.

Key differentiators:

Fully operational on Day 1 — not a prototype, not a roadmap. The live system is deployed and accessible now with pre-seeded accounts, sample data, and a guided onboarding experience.
15 integrated modules covering every aspect of the compensation lifecycle — from job architecture and market pricing through AI-powered equity analysis, workflow automation, policy management, and executive reporting.
AI-native intelligence — six guided AI wizards, RAG-powered decision support, daily briefings, proactive alerts, and community impact analysis connecting compensation decisions to patient care outcomes.
Public healthcare focus — every feature is calibrated for the realities of public-sector healthcare compensation, including FLSA compliance, board-level governance, taxpayer accountability, and community health impact.
7-stage workflow engine with RAG accountability — automated batch processing with configurable Red/Amber/Green thresholds, three approval models, auto-escalation, and ripple-effect analysis.
Bidirectional data integration — native connectors for Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, PeopleSoft, and market survey providers (Mercer, Radford, Sullivan Cotter) with CSV/JSON import-export, webhook receiver, and outbound push capabilities.

7. Workflow Automation & RAG Accountability
Pipeline Stages
Stage 1: INTAKE
  Individual or batch entry → data capture → initial classification
Stage 2: VALIDATION
  Automated checks: equity compliance, market alignment, budget limits,
  FLSA requirements, governance thresholds (Four Amigos triggers)
Stage 3: ROUTING
  Clean records → standard approval path
  Exception records → review queue with reason codes
Stage 4: APPROVAL
  Single Approver: One authorized reviewer
  Parallel: Multiple stakeholders must all approve
  Cascading: Sequential chain (e.g., Manager → Finance → Executive)
Stage 5: EXECUTION
  Automated actions: payroll data push, statement generation,
  audit logging, notification dispatch
Stage 6: VERIFICATION
  Downstream system confirmation, data integrity checks
Stage 7: MONITORING
  Completion tracking, retry management, final reporting
RAG Status System
Status	Threshold	Action
GREEN	< 24 hours elapsed	On track — no action needed
AMBER	24-48 hours elapsed	Monitoring — review for delays
RED	> 72 hours elapsed	Critical — auto-escalation triggered
Thresholds are city-configurable per pipeline to match Central Health's operational tempo.

Governance Controls
Four Amigos Triggers: 50+ employees affected, $100K+ budget impact, equity gap change ≥2%, salary band adjustment ≥10%, new job family creation, compliance risk detection
Auto-escalation: Configurable hours before automatic escalation notification
Exception routing: Items failing validation are automatically routed to exception queue with detailed reason codes
Ripple effect analysis: Every batch action includes analysis of downstream impacts across modules
