I. IGN as a Branded Framework

IGN™: Intentional Guardrails & Navigation

Core Definition

IGN™ is a structured autonomy framework that allows individuals to make meaningful choices within clearly defined developmental boundaries, supported by guided reflection and trusted mentorship.

Short version students can understand:

“You choose the path. We protect the process.”

⸻

The IGN Triad

Autonomy
Guardrails
Navigation

All three must coexist.

If one disappears, growth becomes distorted.

⸻

IGN Operating Principles
	1.	Freedom must include accountability.
	2.	Mistakes must generate learning, not punishment.
	3.	Revisions must require reasoning.
	4.	Growth must be documented.
	5.	Guidance must empower, not control.

This becomes part of your academy’s language, branding, and training.

IGN is visible on:
	•	Student handbook
	•	Mentor training
	•	Parent onboarding
	•	Teacher development
	•	Investor materials
