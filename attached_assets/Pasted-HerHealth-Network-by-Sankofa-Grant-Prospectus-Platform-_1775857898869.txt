Executive Summary
HerHealth Network by Sankofa is a clinically rigorous, culturally grounded digital health platform built specifically for Black and minority women. It delivers comprehensive health information, an AI navigator, and 2,100+ verified local resources across 70 women's health conditions organized into 7 condition platforms. It is the anchor platform of the Sankofa Health Network — a three-platform ecosystem that also covers Black men's health (TheHealthyBlkMan.com) and Black maternal health (YourHealthBirthright.net), making it the only digital health network in the United States designed to serve the entire Black family across the lifespan.

The platform is live, production-ready, and currently seeking grant funding for dissemination, community outreach, and ongoing platform maintenance to reach the populations it was designed to serve.

The Problem
Black and minority women face a compounding crisis at the intersection of health, information, and access:

Black women are 40% more likely to die from breast cancer than white women, despite lower incidence rates
Black women are 3–4 times more likely to die from pregnancy-related causes than white women
Lupus is 2–3 times more prevalent in Black women than in white women, with later diagnoses and worse outcomes
Black women have higher rates of hypertension, Type 2 diabetes, fibroids, and autoimmune conditions — all underrepresented in mainstream health media
Existing digital health platforms are built on white, middle-class health data and do not reflect the lived experience, SDOH barriers, or clinical realities of minority women
Social determinants of health — housing instability, food insecurity, lack of transportation, insurance gaps, medical mistrust — are rarely addressed alongside clinical information in consumer platforms
The result: minority women are navigating a health system that was not designed for them, using tools that do not see them, with no culturally grounded AI navigator to guide their care decisions.

The Solution: HerHealth Network by Sankofa
HerHealth Network is a free-to-use, culturally grounded digital health platform that meets minority women where they are — with clinical depth, SDOH awareness, and genuine care.

Core Platform Architecture
Component	Description
70 Conditions	Full clinical coverage across 7 domains — Cancer, Cardiovascular, Autoimmune, Mental Health, Reproductive Health, Metabolic, and Maternal Health
Nia AI Navigator	AI-powered health navigator trained on minority women's health data, SDOH barriers, and clinical disparities. Powered by Perplexity AI (live web-grounded responses)
2,100+ Verified Resources	State-by-state database covering all 50 states, DC, and U.S. territories — including financial assistance, transportation, childcare, and clinical support programs
5 Guided Decision Wizards	Interactive tools guiding users through mammography scheduling, genetic testing decisions, clinical trial eligibility, treatment navigation, and support group matching
Appointment Preparation Center	Generates condition-specific question lists, symptom trackers, and supplement disclosure tools for medical visits
Find a Provider	ZIP-code-based culturally competent provider matching
MAP-GAP Methodology	Every page maps WHERE users are (the gap in care) and provides tools to bridge them to where they need to be
The 7 Condition Platforms (10 conditions each, 70 total)
Cancer Platform — Breast cancer (IBC, triple-negative, BRCA), cervical, ovarian, endometrial, colorectal, lung, thyroid, skin, and others
Cardiovascular Platform — Hypertension, heart disease, stroke, arrhythmia, heart failure, peripheral artery disease
Autoimmune Platform — Lupus (SLE), rheumatoid arthritis, multiple sclerosis, sarcoidosis, fibromyalgia, Sjögren's
Mental Health Platform — Depression, anxiety, PTSD, bipolar disorder, postpartum depression, grief, racial trauma
Reproductive Health Platform — Fibroids, endometriosis, PCOS, fertility, menopause, sexual health
Metabolic Platform — Type 2 diabetes, obesity, thyroid disorders, vitamin deficiencies
Maternal Health Platform — Preeclampsia, gestational diabetes, placenta previa, postpartum care
The Sankofa Health Network — Three-Platform Ecosystem
HerHealth Network is the anchor of a three-platform direct P2P network that communicates in real time across platforms:

Platform	Population	Focus
HerHealth Network	Black and minority women	70 conditions, 7 domains, AI navigator, resources
TheHealthyBlkMan.com	Black men	Prostate, cardiovascular, mental health, diabetes, HIV
YourHealthBirthright.net	Black maternal health	Pregnancy, postpartum, birth equity, reproductive justice
The networks share: Nia AI navigator, resource database, condition connections, and a direct API — enabling cross-platform referrals (e.g., a man's partner is referred to HerHealth for hypertension resources; a pregnant woman is referred for lupus-in-pregnancy clinical data).

No other digital health platform in the United States addresses the Black family — women, men, and maternal — with this depth of clinical specificity and cultural grounding.

Technology Infrastructure
AI Navigator (Nia): Perplexity AI (live, web-grounded responses) with culturally adapted prompting and 70-condition clinical context
Resource Database: 2,128 verified resources across all 50 states, updated quarterly
Cross-Platform P2P Mesh: PostgreSQL-backed direct messaging and shared knowledge base between all three Sankofa platforms — no intermediary
PWA (Progressive Web App): Mobile-installable, works offline, accessible on any device without an app store
SDOH Integration: Every condition page addresses social determinants — financial barriers, transportation, insurance navigation, food access, and housing
Deployment: Cloud-hosted, production-ready, HTTPS, accessible at herhealthmatters2.com
Population Served
Primary: Black and minority women, ages 18–65+, across all U.S. states and territories

Secondary (through network):

Black men (TheHealthyBlkMan.com)
Black pregnant and postpartum women (YourHealthBirthright.net)
Healthcare providers, community health workers, and patient navigators serving minority communities
Geographic Reach: National, with particular resource depth in Texas (Austin, Manor, Pflugerville), California, New York, Georgia, Illinois, and all major metropolitan areas

SDOH Populations Addressed: Medicaid-eligible, uninsured, underinsured, food insecure, housing unstable, transportation-limited, and medically underserved communities

Equity-Centered Design Principles
Built by and for the community — platform decisions are grounded in the lived experience of Black women
SDOH-first framing — every condition page names systemic failures explicitly and connects users to resources
Zero cost to users — the platform is completely free at the point of use
Cultural competence — language, imagery, and clinical framing center minority women's experience
Medical mistrust acknowledged — the platform explicitly addresses historical medical trauma and builds informed trust
Intersectionality — health conditions are presented in the context of race, class, gender, and geography
Business Model
B2B Revenue Only — Free at Point of Use

HerHealth Network does not monetize individual users. Revenue comes entirely from institutional partnerships:
