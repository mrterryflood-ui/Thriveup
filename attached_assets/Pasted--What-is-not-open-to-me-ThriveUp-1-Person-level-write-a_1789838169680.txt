## What is not open to me

### ThriveUp

1. **Person-level write access**
   - Authentication now succeeds.
   - The key exposes only `health:read`.
   - It does not expose `inbound:write`, required by `POST /api/partner/v1/push`.

2. **A complete `/push` contract**
   - Public documentation identifies the endpoint but does not publish a sufficient request/response specification for governed referrals.
   - Missing or insufficiently defined semantics include:
     - idempotency and duplicate handling;
     - durable acceptance versus temporary receipt;
     - correction and revocation;
     - rejection codes and retry classification;
     - linkage between a pushed record and subsequent outcomes.

3. **Verified webhook intake**
   - ThriveUp documents `referral.created` and `referral.outcome` webhooks with `X-TCAF-Signature` HMAC-SHA256 signing.
   - Our receiver remains closed because no one-time webhook secret has been securely registered and provisioned, and raw-body verification has not been proven.
   - It intentionally returns an unavailable response before mutating data.

4. **Programmatic referral outcome authority**
   - ThriveUp documents `PATCH /api/referrals/:id/outcome`.
   - It requires `inbound:write`, and the partner name on the key must exactly match the referral’s target organization.
   - The present key cannot use it.

5. **FHIR/clinical transport**
   - No canonical FHIR base URL, SMART authorization contract, tenant identity, or delivery acknowledgement contract is available.
   - Clinical packets therefore remain local and patient-reviewed.

Provider intelligence **is open and authenticated** through `health:read`.

## Audit result

### Before

The ecosystem had several defects that could have allowed:

- cross-organization reads or reconciliation;
- consent and delegation revocation races;
- separate audit chains for one subject;
- incomplete audit-envelope hashing;
- browser-entered observations being mislabeled as trusted device data;
- invalid operator-role alternatives;
- generic Toolkit forms sending incorrect request structures.

### Changed

- Added explicit source-organization ownership to consents, observations, outbox records, capabilities, and lifecycle records.
- Added tenant-aware composite foreign keys for community and research relationships.
- Bound caregiver actions to one exact locked delegation.
- Locked governing consent, delegation, participation, and study rows inside their mutation transactions.
- Made domain writes, audit records, events, and outbox records atomic.
- Unified locked audit-chain stream semantics and hashed every persisted integrity field, including nullable metadata.
- Scoped all patient, operator, reconciliation, referral, capability, and outbox operations to the correct organization.
- Blocked device-origin claims until cryptographic device attestation exists.
- Kept ThriveUp referral capability `unavailable` and `unverified`.
- Replaced the generic Toolkit lifecycle panel with real clinical, community, research, and implementation workflows.
