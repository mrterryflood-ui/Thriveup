**`platform-fixes-complete.ts`** — six server-side fixes:

**Fix 1 — TANF bug closed.** `computeEligibility()` now includes TANF (income ≤ 17% FPL with children), CCDF childcare assistance (income ≤ $53K with children), LIHEAP utility assistance (≤ 150% FPL), Section 8/HCV (≤ 50% AMI), and Veterans Benefits (any honorable discharge). Every eligible program now has a full navigation guide: exact application URL, documents required, enrollment window, processing time, estimated annual value. The screener no longer stops at identification — it hands the person a path.

**Fix 2 — Reentry intake wired to 4 side effects.** `createParticipantEnhanced()` runs household creation, benefits screening, fair-chance employer match, and risk scoring in parallel. A failure in any one never crashes the others. The risk screener is an 8-domain simplified RNR instrument — not a validated clinical tool, but enough for intake triage with a flag for full LSI-R/ORAS when score ≥ 6.

**Fix 3 — Employer match endpoint.** `getEmployerMatches(userId)` queries earned credentials from certificates, matches against ban-the-box employer job postings by title and requirements text, and returns match reasons per employer. Two new routes: `/api/workforce/match/:userId` and `/api/workforce/jobs`.

**Fix 4 — Mastery gating at 80%.** `processQuizSubmission()` raises the gate from 70% to 80%, tracks attempt count, identifies missed question topics, and returns `triggerSpark: true` + `sparkContext` on every non-mastery result. `canAdvance` is an explicit boolean the frontend uses to disable the next button.

**Fix 5 — Spark auto-surfaces on quiz fail.** `SparkAuto` component opens automatically when `triggerSpark` prop fires, injects a focused opening message about missed concepts, passes 800 characters of lesson content (not 150), uses grade level from learner profile instead of a user-selected dropdown, and shows an orange visual state when auto-triggered so the learner knows why it opened.

**Fix 6 — Schema additions.** 8 new columns across 4 tables: `isVeteran`, `isSingleParent`, `navigationGuides` on benefits screenings; `credentialTags`, `wageMin`, `wageMax` on job postings; `reentryPlanId` on placements; `riskScore`, `riskLevel`, `riskDomains`, `householdId` on participant profiles.

**`JobBoard-ReentryIntake.tsx`** — two new pages:

`JobBoard` at `/jobs`: credential-matched view first, all fair-chance jobs second. Expandable job cards with employer ban-the-box badges, match reasons tied to earned credentials, direct employer website links.

`ReentryIntakeEnhanced` at `/reentry/intake`: three-step flow — profile → 8-question risk screen → results. Results surface risk level with domain flags, benefits gaps with apply links, and fair-chance employer matches in a single integrated view.
