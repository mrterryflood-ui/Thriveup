Narrative Submission
Submit a 1–2 page narrative responding to the following questions:  
Organizational Background and Capacity: Briefly describe your organization's mission, history, and programs. What is your organization's experience at the intersection of mental health and workforce development? Please describe relevant staff expertise and programs that demonstrate your capacity to deliver this work. 

Nonclinical Mental Health Provider Experience: What is your organization's experience supporting nonclinical mental health providers? 

Program Description: Describe your proposed program. What will you do? Who will you serve? Which pipeline stage(s) will you address? (Attraction/Recruitment, Development/Training, Placement/Entry to Practice, Upskilling/Career Progression, Retention/Sustainability) 

Workforce Pipeline Need: What specific workforce pipeline gap does your program address? How do you know this need exists? 

Youth Focus: How does your program focus on youth mental health? Describe what makes working with youth-serving providers or youth providers unique. 

Pipeline Connectivity: Describe how participants will connect to your program and what happens after. How will participants enter your program? What opportunities or support will participants have after? 

Equity Strategy: What specific strategies will you use to ensure your program serves historically underserved populations?  

Youth Voice and Lived Experience Integration: How will you integrate youth perspective and/or mental health provider lived experience into program design and implementation?  

 Funding Request: What is your funding request amount? A high-level funding request is sufficient at the LOI stage. Please note, each applicant may request between $250,000 and $500,000 in total funding. Detailed budgets will be requested from invited applicants in Stage 2.

Partnerships (Optional): Describe any key partnerships that will support your pipeline work (e.g., upstream partners who help attract and recruit talent and/or downstream partners who provide training, placement, or ongoing support). 

Please upload your narrative here. You will need to log into a Google account to submit.
