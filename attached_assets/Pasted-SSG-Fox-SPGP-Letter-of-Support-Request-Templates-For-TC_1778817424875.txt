SSG Fox SPGP — Letter of Support Request Templates
For TCAF's FY2027 SSG Fox Suicide Prevention Grant Program application · Deadline June 12, 2026
How to use this page
Each card below is a separate Letter of Support request that can be emailed to the named partner. Print this page (or save as PDF) to send the full set in one packet, or copy individual sections. Replace bracketed [PLACEHOLDERS] with the partner's name, your contact details, and a personal note.
A. Outreach Email Template (use this for first contact)
Subject: Request for Letter of Support — SSG Fox Veteran Suicide Prevention Grant (FY2027)

Dear [PARTNER NAME / TITLE],

The Collaborative Advocate Foundation (TCAF) is preparing a Priority 2 new-applicant submission to the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs Staff Sergeant Parker Gordon Fox Suicide Prevention Grant Program (SSG Fox SPGP) for Fiscal Year 2027. The application deadline is June 12, 2026.

Our program — M2C Mission Transition (vetmissiontransition.com) — is a non-clinical, community-based suicide prevention program serving veterans, transitioning service members, and military families in the Pflugerville–Manor–East Austin corridor of Travis and Williamson counties. We combine a free public digital platform with trained peer navigators, Stanley-Brown safety planning, lethal-means counseling, C-SSRS screening, and warm hand-offs to VA and community providers.

We are writing to respectfully request a Letter of Support from [ORGANIZATION] confirming [SPECIFIC ASK — see below]. A short letter (one page) is sufficient. We would be grateful to receive it by [DATE — at least 14 days before June 12, 2026].

If helpful, we have drafted suggested language below that you are welcome to edit, abbreviate, or replace entirely with your own wording.

For questions or to schedule a brief call:
Terry Flood — TCAF / M2C Mission Transition
(254) 319-8460 · vetmissiontransition.com

With gratitude,
Terry Flood
Founder, The Collaborative Advocate Foundation

Submission notes
Letters should be on partner organization letterhead, signed and dated within 60 days of June 12, 2026.
PDF format preferred for VA eGMS upload.
Track receipt and follow up at the 14-day and 7-day marks before the deadline.
If a partner cannot commit by deadline, request a shorter "letter of acknowledgment" instead — still useful for scoring on the partnerships criterion.
