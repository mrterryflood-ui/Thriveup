1. Teacher Implementation Manual

TxEA Empowerment Academy

6–12 Longitudinal Delivery Guide

⸻

A. Instructional Philosophy

TxEA is built on five instructional pillars:
	1.	Identity before occupation
	2.	Exposure before commitment
	3.	Agency with accountability
	4.	Mentorship with structure
	5.	Graduation with continuity

Teachers are not “career instructors.”
They are pathway architects and longitudinal advisors.

⸻

B. Faculty Roles

Pathway Advisor
	•	Assigned to 10–15 students
	•	Reviews and unlocks pathway revisions
	•	Conducts quarterly 1:1 advisory sessions
	•	Monitors Panther Power growth
	•	Approves mentor pairing requests

SIM Facilitator
	•	Integrates Panther Village activities into weekly lessons
	•	Reviews flagged content
	•	Awards milestone approvals

Cohort Lead
	•	Tracks grade-level milestone completion
	•	Monitors house competitions
	•	Reports to admin dashboard

⸻

C. Annual Instructional Structure

Each year follows a predictable four-phase structure:

Quarter	Focus	Teacher Role
Q1	Identity & Planning	Advisory sessions + baseline assessments
Q2	Exploration & Simulation	Career Explorer + economic modules
Q3	Application & Mentorship	Mentor engagement + portfolio building
Q4	Demonstration & Reflection	Milestone validation + pathway revision


⸻

D. Weekly Structure (Middle School Example)
	•	Monday: Identity / Career Exploration Lesson
	•	Tuesday: SIM Engagement (Stock Exchange, Marketplace)
	•	Wednesday: Reflection + Portfolio Entry
	•	Thursday: Mentor or Pathway Work
	•	Friday: Quest Completion + House Points

High school shifts toward capstone and work-based learning integration.

⸻

E. Pathway Revision Governance (Faculty Process)
	1.	Student submits revision request
	2.	Parent notified automatically
	3.	Advisor reviews justification
	4.	Collaborative session required before unlock
	5.	Plan edited and saved with full revision history
	6.	Plan re-locked

No silent changes.
Every revision becomes a teachable moment in decision-making.

3. Funding-Ready Proposal Narrative

Executive Summary

TxEA Empowerment Academy is a seven-year longitudinal career architecture program serving cohorts of 60 students beginning in Grade 6 and continuing through post-graduation alumni engagement.

Unlike traditional career awareness programs, TxEA integrates:
	•	Simulation-based experiential learning
	•	Structured pathway governance
	•	Community-based mentorship
	•	Financial literacy through virtual economy systems
	•	Alumni reintegration as mentors

The program produces graduates who leave with:
	•	Documented multi-year pathway plans
	•	Professional mentor relationships
	•	Demonstrated credentials or portfolio artifacts
	•	Postsecondary acceptance or placement
	•	Community engagement experience

⸻

Problem Statement

Students often graduate without:
	•	Clear career direction
	•	Financial literacy
	•	Professional networks
	•	Portfolio evidence of readiness

Career exploration is typically episodic rather than longitudinal.

TxEA replaces exposure-based programming with structured developmental architecture.

⸻

Goals
	1.	100% of students maintain documented pathway plans.
	2.	100% mentor pairing by end of 8th grade.
	3.	80% graduates demonstrate postsecondary alignment.
	4.	60% alumni remain engaged within two years.

⸻

Sustainability Plan
	•	Virtual economy fundraising component (Print Shop)
	•	Community mentor sponsorship model
	•	Alumni reinvestment network
	•	Longitudinal data reporting to attract district support
