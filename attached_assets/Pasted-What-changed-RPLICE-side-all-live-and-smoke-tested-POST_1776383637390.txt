What changed (RPLICE side, all live and smoke-tested):

POST /api/v1/benefits/publish — RPLICE → peers (admin-triggered, signs each request with the peer's own shared secret, not a central one)
POST /api/v1/benefits/inbound — peers → RPLICE (verifies signature against that specific peer's secret)
GET /api/v1/benefits/events — shared event log, both directions, both peers
New peer_ecosystem_events table records every event, who sent it, signature validity, response status, latency
OpenAPI bumped to v1.2 with the trust model spelled out
What I deliberately did not do:

No central RPLICE secret. The earlier draft had RPLICE_PUSH_SECRET (one key, RPLICE-owned). That was hierarchical. Replaced with LIFE_TRANSITIONS_SHARED_SECRET and THRIVEUP_SHARED_SECRET — one secret per peer pair, neither side holds the other's. RPLICE is just one of three nodes; if a peer rotates its secret, only that pair is affected.
No queueing or retries. If you want at-least-once delivery, peers replay from their own log. Easy to add later.
