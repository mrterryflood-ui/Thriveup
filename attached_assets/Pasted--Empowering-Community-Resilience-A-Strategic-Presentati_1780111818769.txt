# Empowering Community Resilience: A Strategic Presentation for CTX United Way

## Presentation Strategy Overview

This presentation will transform how CTX United Way understands community transformation — moving from fragmented service delivery to a holistic, accountable ecosystem of support.

The fundamental innovation is not connecting services, but creating TRUE accountability between them. Traditional platforms aggregate data; ThriveUp orchestrates responsibility.
