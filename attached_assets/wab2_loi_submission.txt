FIELD 18 — REQUEST SUMMARY  (PASTE EXACTLY — 478 words)
================================================================================

Tens of thousands of 5-county residents are eligible for SNAP, WIC, Medicaid, CHIP, ACA, EITC, CTC, SSI, and SSDI and don't receive them. SNAP and Medicaid take-up trail eligibility by double digits; 35-40% of EITC-eligible filers never claim. Applications are long, fragmented across 9+ portals, English-default, and scheduled around bank hours families do not have. Maria, a Caldwell mother of two, was eligible for Medicaid, WIC, and EITC for three years — in 15 minutes on her phone, in Spanish, she enrolled in all three, capturing roughly $8,400 a year her family had been leaving on the table.

The experience is one 15-minute intake, in English, Spanish, Vietnamese, Mandarin, and Arabic, that screens all 9 benefits at once, routes to the right portal with deep-links, captures receipts, and runs denial-to-appeal. Any community health worker, promotora, school liaison, pastor, FQHC staffer, or resident on a personal phone runs the same flow. Partners embed the same intake on their own sites — no partner pays, no partner builds their own stack. Reviewers can click lifetransitionsaid.org/st-davids and run the intake themselves.

The infrastructure is already running in production. ChainWeb is a citation-traceable evidence engine; every county-, ZIP-, and tract-level number chains back to a primary federal source — Census ACS, CDC PLACES, ATSDR SVI, HUD CHAS, FBI CDE — across 955 live federal data connections at 67+ agencies. Two front doors share one network: residents can enter at lifetransitionsaid.org/st-davids or at TCAF's navigator hub at /st-davids; the intake, counties, and program catalog are identical. RPLICE, an authenticated peer-mirror event bus, keeps both sites in sync in real time. Records are deduplicated on peer + resident reference + program + status, so a resident enrolled once is counted once — verified end-to-end. A public Live Network View endpoint publishes localOwned, peerMirrored, and networkTotal broken out by peer, benefit, and county; St. David's can audit the count at any moment.

Twenty-four-month targets: 18,000+ residents enrolled in at least one new benefit, with at least 40% from rural Bastrop and Caldwell; $24M+ in captured annual benefit value to households; 85%+ six-month retention; 70%+ appeal-win rate on initial denials; a single deduplicated network total auditable live; and a renewal matrix delivered to St. David's every 90 days, broken out by County × Benefit Area × Language.

Confirmed partners: CommUnityCare, Lone Star Circle of Care, Central Health, Foundation Communities, El Buen Samaritano. MOUs in development with Austin ISD, Hays CISD, Bastrop ISD, faith networks, and county human-services agencies. Active HHSC Community Partner Program Level 1. A 30-day MAP-GAP fidelity cycle uses CFIR + RE-AIM checks. External evaluation by Dell Medical School / UT School of Social Work runs under an IRB-reviewed protocol with quarterly public dashboards. Sustainability: HHSC CPP reimbursements, Medicaid 1115-waiver navigator billing, and ongoing TCAF infrastructure investment carry the work past Year 2. Match: $120K in-kind partner clinical staff time + $80K TCAF platform infrastructure.
