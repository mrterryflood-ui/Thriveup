Synthesized Best Practices for Preventing Displacement in Austin

Comprehensive Anti-Displacement Strategy

Austin faces a critical housing affordability crisis, with systemic challenges that require a holistic, multi-dimensional approach. The most effective prevention strategies integrate economic mobility, community resilience, and targeted policy interventions.

Core Prevention Framework:

1. Workforce and Economic Mobility
- Develop robust economic pathways through targeted skills training
- Implement credential routing programs across key trades
- Create living-wage employment opportunities that stabilize household economics
- Leverage ThriveUp's trade simulation programs (75 lessons across 5 trades)
- Focus on building transferable skills that increase long-term earning potential

2. Housing Stability Interventions
- Advocate for zoning reforms supporting diverse housing developments
- Promote mixed-use developments with integrated affordable housing units
- Support policies protecting low-income tenants
- Collaborate with Austin Housing Authority on affordable housing initiatives
- Develop neighborhood-specific affordable housing strategies

3. Community Coalition and Support Networks
- Build strategic partnerships across:
  • Local government agencies
  • Nonprofit organizations
  • Community-based service providers
  • Resident leadership groups
- Utilize the Reentry Roundtable model for comprehensive support coordination
- Create multicultural service integration programs

4. Targeted Neighborhood Ecosystem Support
- Develop localized approaches for:
  - Austin
  - Manor
  - Pflugerville
- Use granular neighborhood data for precision interventions
- Implement community health worker tracking and support systems

- Access /community-map for neighborhood-specific insights

- Explore /grants for anti-displacement funding opportunities
- Connect directly with ThriveUp's Austin Hub for personalized ecosystem navigation

Critical Success Factors:
- Prioritize resident voice and lived experience
- Maintain cultural competence in service delivery
- Create flexible, adaptive support models
- Emphasize long-term economic empowerment

Measurement and Accountability:
- Track displacement risk indicators
- Monitor economic mobility metrics
- Assess program effectiveness through continuous community feedback
- Use MAP-GAP framework for ongoing program improvement

By implementing this comprehensive approach, Austin can develop a robust, proactive strategy to prevent displacement and create pathways to sustainable community resilience.
