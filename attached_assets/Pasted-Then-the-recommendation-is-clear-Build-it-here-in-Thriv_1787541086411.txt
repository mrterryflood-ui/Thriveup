Then the recommendation is clear:

## Build it here in ThriveUp, with GrantPathPro as the funding-operations layer.

### ThriveUp should own

- Understanding the organization’s mission, capabilities, and goals
- Needs assessments and community context
- Translating plain-language descriptions into opportunity categories
- Discovering adjacent paths the organization may not know about
- Mapping grants, contracts, sponsorships, research, technology transfer, equipment, and workforce opportunities
- Identifying potential partner categories
- Building scale-up and scale-out strategies
- Creating the evidence and community-data package
- Letting the organization choose which paths to pursue

### GrantPathPro should own

- Opportunity and pursuit management
- Funding workflow
- Proposal development and submission operations
- Funder and grant tracking
- Collaborative pursuit execution
- Mirror updates and grant-status events

### The relationship should be

```text
Organization speaks naturally
        ↓
ThriveUp interprets mission, needs, capabilities, and geography
        ↓
ThriveUp widens the opportunity lens
        ↓
Verified opportunity ecosystem is assembled
        ↓
Organization chooses a path
        ↓
Qualified package moves to GrantPathPro
        ↓
GrantPathPro manages the pursuit
        ↓
Status, milestones, and lessons return to ThriveUp
```

Do **not** build the same intelligence twice inside both platforms. ThriveUp should become the **discovery, strategy, community-context, and readiness layer**. GrantPathPro should become the **funding execution and pursuit layer**.

For the drone example, ThriveUp should recognize the organization’s description and suggest exploration across:

- youth STEM grants,
- aviation and drone workforce programs,
- school and workforce contracts,
- university research partnerships,
- technology-transfer or applied-research pathways,
- equipment sponsorships,
- environmental or public-safety applications,
- corporate partnerships,
- and regional or national expansion.

Then, once the organization selects a funding or partnership path, ThriveUp should send the verified opportunity and organization package to GrantPathPro.

That division is logical, avoids duplication, and makes the current GrantPathPro integration strategically useful rather than merely decorative.
