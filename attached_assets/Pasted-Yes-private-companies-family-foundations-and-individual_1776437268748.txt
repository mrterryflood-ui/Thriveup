## Individual donors

Individuals usually won’t give through a formal grant process the way foundations do, but they can support you through:
- Direct donations.
- Monthly giving.
- Peer-to-peer fundraising.
- Sponsorships for programs or cohorts.
- Angel-style community support for your platform launch. [neurodivergentfriends](https://www.neurodivergentfriends.org/get-involved/donate)

For neurodivergence and mental health, individual donors often respond well to:
- clear stories of impact,
- named program goals,
- specific funding needs,
- and visible community outcomes. [neurodiversityconnection](https://www.neurodiversityconnection.com/donate)

## What private funders usually want

Private companies and foundations typically want:
- A focused mission.
- A measurable program.
- A clear geography, such as Texas statewide.
- Proof that you can deliver results.
- A fiscal sponsor or tax-exempt partner if you do not yet have your own exemption. [instrumentl](https://www.instrumentl.com/browse-grants/texas/mental-health-grants)
