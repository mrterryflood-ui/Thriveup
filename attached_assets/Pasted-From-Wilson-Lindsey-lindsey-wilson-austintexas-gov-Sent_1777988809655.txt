About the Program
The FY26 Equity Mini Grant Program provides $25,000 one-time grants to support community-based efforts serving immigrant and refugee communities in Austin, Travis County, and the City’s Extraterritorial Jurisdiction (ETJ).
This year’s program focuses on projects that:
Foster belonging and trust
Expand access to critical services
Reduce barriers to participation
Support long-term stability and economic mobility
Key Details
Grant Amount: $25,000 (8 awards available)
Application Opens: April 17, 2026
Application Deadline: May 8, 2026
Award Notifications: Expected June 2026
Project Implementation: July 2026 – October 2027 (12–15 months)
Projects may include (but are not limited to):
Community education and outreach
Language access or ESL programs
Workforce readiness or leadership development
Cultural and community-building initiatives
Navigation support for housing, health, education, or employment services
Creative or storytelling projects that strengthen community connection
