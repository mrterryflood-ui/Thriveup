Describe two goals you aim to achieve within the 90 days of the program. 
*
Be concise, concrete, and as specific as possible (e.g., sign $1,5M contracts, secure $5M investment, or establish strategic partnerships with a Fortune 500 (exact name) company).
Your goals should be ambitious but realistic, meaning that with your current team, traction, and operational capacity you could reasonably achieve at least 30% of the goal even without the program’s support.

This helps us understand that your objectives are grounded in real execution rather than hypothetical outcomes.

Pitchdeck/Executive Summary (maximum 5 clear slides)
*
Please submit a concise pitch deck of no more than five clear slides. Include your key team members responsible for technology, revenue, investments and human capital. Do not list advisors who have not invested capital in the company. Show real traction, explain why your solution works, and be transparent about how many times you have failed while building this exact company or solution—if the answer is zero, you are likely not a fit for this program. We value authentic, experience-based stories and measurable outcomes, not generic or AI-generated narratives.

Note: We view AI today much like the internet 30 years ago—a foundational capability, not a differentiator on its own. Decks that simply describe the company as “an AI company” without clear substance will be placed at the end of the review list. Focus on the problem, the solution, and verifiable results.
