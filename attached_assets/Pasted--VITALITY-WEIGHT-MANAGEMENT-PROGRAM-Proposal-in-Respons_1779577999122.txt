# Section 2 — Executive Summary

## 2.1 The three-sentence thesis

Sedgwick County is already spending approximately $5.15M per year on unmanaged GLP-1 medications for 373 employees and dependents, with a ~47% twelve-month non-continuation rate — consistent with published unmanaged-GLP-1 patterns of 30–50% — that demonstrates prior authorization alone is not working. Vitality's integrated clinical-plus-coaching managed-care protocol represents a **modeled Year-1 capture opportunity of approximately $2.09M** against that current spend, addressable through migration from unmanaged to managed-protocol therapy and nearly funding the entire program; we also serve the broader 1,525 at-risk adult population with evidence-based lifestyle and clinical pathways. We build with, not against, UnitedHealthcare, OptumRx, and Rally; we displace no existing vendor; and we are the only respondent purpose-built for the four-partner program design model the County asked for.

## 2.2 What Vitality is

Vitality is a comprehensive, virtual-only weight management program designed for self-insured public-sector employers. The program combines:

- **Three evidence-based clinical pathways** (Lifestyle, GLP-1-Managed, and Surgical-Coordinated), each anchored in published evidence (NIH/DPP, STEP Trials, LOOK AHEAD, RAND).
- **A 5-phase member journey** (onboarding, baseline, intensive intervention, maintenance, graduation) overseen by Dr. Chela Love, DNP and her clinical team at Love Clinic MedSpa.
- **A behavioral coaching engine** led by Dr. J. Michelle Vann, with weekly check-ins, lifestyle accountability, and family/youth program extensions.
- **A digital Progressive Web App platform** (no app-store install required) built and operated by Dr. Terry D. Flood's team at The COLLABORATIVE Advocate Foundation, with FHIR/HL7 integration, persistent-memory AI coaching, and HIPAA-grade security.
- **Prime contractor administration** by Hargrave Innovative Solutions, providing government contract management, compliance oversight, reporting coordination, and single-point accountability.

## 2.3 Why Vitality wins on the County's three named KPIs

The County named three KPIs in Addendum #2, in this order:

1. **Member engagement** — Vitality's VITA AI persistent-memory advisor remembers every prior conversation with each member and resumes context across sessions. Combined with Dr. Vann's weekly behavioral cadence, the program is designed to sustain engagement over 12–24 months, not just at signup.
2. **Proper use of GLP-1s** — Dr. Love's structured titration protocol, OptumRx fill-history reconciliation, and side-effect management directly address the non-continuation pattern visible in the County's 705 → 373 cohort data.
3. **Cost savings** — modeled Year-1 economic opportunity of approximately $3.05M (the $2.09M GLP-1 capture plus $675K medical and $282K productivity savings) against a $1.71M Year-1 program cost.

## 4.2 The GLP-1 utilization picture

The single most operationally significant disclosure in Addendum #2 is the GLP-1 utilization data. We interpret it as follows:

**(a) Current GLP-1 spend is substantial and unmanaged.** At the conservative RAND 2023 figure of $13,800 per unmanaged GLP-1 patient per year, the 373 current GLP-1 members represent approximately $5.15 million in annual pharmacy cost to the County's medical plan. This spend is occurring with no clinical oversight beyond standard PBM prior authorization.

**(b) The ~47% twelve-month non-continuation rate is the diagnostic signal.** 705 members used a GLP-1 in the past twelve months, but only 373 are on therapy today — a 332-member differential, or approximately 47% non-continuation. We use "non-continuation" rather than pure "discontinuation" because the County figure may include planned course completions, approved-but-never-filled prior authorizations, within-class therapy switches, and members recently started who are still in titration. Even with those qualifications, the figure sits squarely within the published unmanaged-GLP-1 range of 30–50% twelve-month non-continuation (Gleason et al., JAMA Network Open 2024; AHIP real-world adherence analysis 2023), and the underlying drivers are well-established: side effects (nausea, fatigue) without titration support, absence of behavioral coaching, and no clinical follow-up.
