You are **not** a UN agency vendor today. Below is an honest map of **tools, signals, mechanisms**, and **where you can actually help inflect change** versus where you should only **catalog, partner, or stay out**.

## 3. **Mechanisms** you bring that match how the UN wants change to happen

These are your strongest “UN-adjacent” contribution — often **more distinctive** than another dashboard.

| Mechanism | What it does | UN-style problem it addresses |
|-----------|--------------|-------------------------------|
| **DIS (Diagnostic Implementation Science)** | Diagnose before prescribe; 5W+H; local adaptation | “Solutions exported without context” |
| **Discovery protocol** (SIGNAL → … → LEARN) | Every signal gets a **disposition**, not a orphan chart | Monitoring without action (SDG criticism) |
| **Implementation incongruence (4 axes)** | Aggregate vs lived, capability vs access, guidance half-life, reported vs verified | Headline indicators vs ground truth (`docs/SYMMETRY.md`; **admin-1 internationally**) |

| **Iron Rule + warrants + export gates** | Fail-closed claims; graded evidence | Misinformation / AI hallucination in policy |
| **Federation ingest + `discovery.learn.recorded`** | Learn events flow back | Country-led adaptation and M&E loops |
| **Thrive international backbone** (PR #79) | Evidence contract + 249-country **honest depth** + framework-to-action | One platform for **all regions** without faking national statistics |
| **Population / equity lenses** | Refugees, children, LGBTQ+, disability, etc. | LNOB / human rights–sensitive programming (`population-dashboard`, equity rubrics) |
| **Team science HIC/LMIC comparator** | WHO/World Bank–grounded context for scaling/adaptation | Escoffery / Abimbola-style **adaptation**, not copy-paste |

## 4. How you map to **UN “areas”** (honest fit, not “we do everything”)

Think in **UN pillars + SDG clusters**, not one product per goal.

### A. Health (WHO, UHC, outbreaks) — **Strong fit**

- **Signals:** GHO, outbreak/news, global disease tracker, condition evidence templates  
- **Change:** Pre-registered claims, stratified gaps, implementation packages (studios, disposition)  
- **Limit:** No replacement for WHO country office authority, IRIN, or in-country surveillance systems; **Tier 2** for WHO production hosting  

### B. Children, education, social protection (UNICEF, UNESCO, ILO) — **Moderate fit (framework + catalog + Thrive)**

- **Signals:** Registry metadata; comparators when WB/WHO indicators overlap  
- **Change:** Thrive programs (workforce, youth, family) + **ERIC/CFIR** action maps; UNICEF-style indicators when you wire adapters or partner data  
- **Limit:** No automatic UNICEF/UIS API spine for every country yet  

### C. Development & SDGs (UNDP, World Bank, DESA) — **Moderate–strong on method, thin on official SDG reporting**

- **Signals:** World Bank adapter; UNDP/HDI in catalog; operationalization examples (e.g. WWF SDG Hub / UN DESA case studies on `/operationalization-guide`)  
- **Change:** **Implementation workspace** = practical SDG delivery (multi-sector barriers, partners, indicators) without claiming certified SDG indicator reporting  
- **Limit:** You are **not** a UNSTATS SDG official reporting tool unless you deliberately build to their indicator API and governance  

### D. Food, agriculture, climate (FAO, UNEP-adjacent) — **Thin–moderate**

- **Signals:** FAOSTAT catalog; climate/resilience bundles; environmental loaders (often US-heavy)  
- **Change:** Multisector correlation and community domains (food, climate) in analysis frameworks  
- **Limit:** FAO/UNEP live operational integration is mostly **catalog + narrative**, not full adapter grid  

### E. Human rights, governance, inclusion — **Moderate (governance of evidence, not courts)**

- **Signals:** Governance indicator *domains* in international UI; equity rubrics; symmetric incongruence discipline  
- **Change:** Transparent provenance, stratification, contestation registers (outbreak/policy patterns)  
- **Limit:** Not a human rights monitoring body; no UN Human Rights Council workflow  

### F. Humanitarian, refugees, peace (UNHCR, OCHA, DPO) — **Narrow fit**

- **Signals:** UNHCR/UNICEF displacement **referenced** (`population-dashboard`); MDMP/military **humanitarian assistance** UX and terminology  
- **Change:** Coordination vocabulary, partner hubs, referral *patterns* via Thrive  
- **Limit:** **Not** a humanitarian cluster tool, CASH pipeline, or peacekeeping C2 system — and you should not market as such

## 5. How you are able to **inflect change** (theory of change, not hype)

Change inflection here means: **better decisions, implemented with fidelity, measured honestly, adapted locally** — not “RPLICE runs the SDGs.”

```text
UN / multilateral signal  →  RPLICE (validate, stratify, disposition, warrant)
                                    ↓
              Framework + evidence package (per country, honest depth)
                                    ↓
              Thrive / local partners (referrals, consent, action, outcomes)
                                    ↓
              Federation learn events  →  update evidence & next cycle
```

**Where you actually move the needle:**

1. **Stop false certainty** — tiered provenance before ministers, donors, or community boards see a number.  
2. **Name the implementation gap** — incongruence + MAP-GAP instead of “awareness campaigns.”  
3. **Turn guidelines into owned actions** — framework-to-action rows Thrive assigns to real orgs.  
4. **Support LMIC adaptation** — HIC/LMIC comparator + localization hints; no silent US export.  
5. **Close the loop** — discovery learn + DIS “learning changes the system.”  

**Where you do not inflect change alone:** funding flows, treaty obligations, peace negotiations, sovereign data systems, or replacing UN country teams.
