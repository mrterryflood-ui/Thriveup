Spencer Foundation Small Research Grant — this is a really strong fit for ISSS. Here's why:

What makes this better than the NSF grant:

No university-as-lead requirement. Spencer awards to any nonprofit, public institution, school district, or 501(c)(3). If your organization has or can obtain 501(c)(3) status (or if you partner with a school district or nonprofit that can administer the funds), you're eligible. This sidesteps the IHE partnership barrier you'd face with NSF.

Field-initiated — you pick the topic. There's no requirement to frame it around STEM. You can propose exactly what ISSS does: implementation science infrastructure for whole-child support. No contorting the proposal to fit a narrow category.

Budget is manageable. Up to $50,000 (plus optional $10,000 course release supplement), no indirect costs allowed. This is a focused grant — enough to fund a rigorous pilot study without the overhead of a massive multi-site trial.

1-5 year timeline gives flexibility to design a meaningful study.

Regarding your SALP Science platform as research support:

That's a smart play. Having your own research infrastructure at salp-science--mrterryflood.replit.app strengthens the proposal because you can point to it as part of your data collection and analysis capability. Spencer wants to see that you have the tools to conduct rigorous research — and having a purpose-built platform alongside ISSS demonstrates that.

What a strong Spencer proposal could look like:

Research question: "How does adaptive implementation infrastructure affect fidelity of evidence-based student support practices across diverse school contexts?"
Method: Mixed-methods study using ISSS's built-in data (fidelity scores, readiness assessments, Proctor's 8 outcomes, practice-policy reports) plus qualitative interviews
Sites: 3-5 schools using ISSS, with your SALP Science platform supporting the research analysis
Budget: Personnel time for the PI, participant compensation, travel for site visits, and dissemination
Key things to know:

Applications open twice a year — check their deadlines page for the next window
They have a generative AI policy you'll need to acknowledge (given ISSS uses GPT-4o and Claude, be transparent about how AI supports practitioners vs. conducts the research itself)
The PI can only hold one active Spencer grant at a time
Your VOSB status isn't a barrier here as long as the administering organization is a nonprofit or public entity
