James J. Henderson 
James J. Henderson III is a community advocate and civic leader serving as the current President of the Port Chester–Rye Branch of the NAACP. Elected in January 2025, Henderson assumed leadership during a pivotal moment for civil rights and democracy, bringing a deep respect for the branch's historic legacy and a clear commitment to keeping it a visible and effective force across Port Chester, Rye and greater Westchester County.

Under his leadership, the branch has continued its work in voter engagement, public education, health equity and youth scholarships, while strengthening partnerships with local civic and community institutions. Prior to his election, Henderson served on the Executive Committee of the NAACP White Plains–Greenburgh Branch and held leadership roles with the Lois Bronz Children's Center, the NYS Division for Youth and the Westchester County Youth Board.

A development executive, writer and proud member of Kappa Alpha Psi Fraternity, Inc., Henderson has called Port Chester home for more than two decades. His work as Branch President reflects an unwavering belief that justice, opportunity and dignity must be accessible to all.
