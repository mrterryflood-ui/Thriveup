1. Executive Overview

TxEA Empowerment Academy is a 7-year longitudinal pathway system beginning in Grade 6 and continuing through post-graduation alumni engagement.

It integrates:
	•	Simulation-based experiential learning (Panther Village)
	•	Structured career pathway governance
	•	Mentor pairing with approval workflow
	•	Financial literacy via virtual economy
	•	Portfolio and credential alignment
	•	Alumni ambassador cycle

This model shifts career programming from exposure-based events to structured developmental architecture.

Across districts:

	•	Financial literacy is disconnected from lived practice.
	•	Mentor access is uneven.
	•	Alumni disengage after graduation.

Students graduate with transcripts but without trajectory clarity.
