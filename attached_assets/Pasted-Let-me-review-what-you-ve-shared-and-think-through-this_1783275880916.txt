## ThriveUp Academy — Strategic Enhancement Roadmap

### The Core Problem to Solve First

With 64+ surfaces, the platform's biggest risk is **breadth without depth**. Users (especially WIOA populations — veterans, returning citizens, rural workers) can get lost. Every improvement should answer: *does this help a learner go from zero to employed credential?*

---

### Tier 1 — Highest Impact Improvements

**1. Unified Learner Intelligence Layer**
Right now your tools appear to operate as separate surfaces. The biggest upgrade is a single AI backbone that tracks the learner *across all 64 surfaces* and synthesizes a living profile: skills demonstrated, gaps remaining, employer-readiness score. This powers everything else.

**2. Employer Demand Integration (Live)**
The trade sims, certifications, and career pathways need to be wired to real labor market signals — BLS, Texas Workforce Commission, Indeed/LinkedIn APIs — so a learner in Pflugerville sees *which credentials are actually hiring in their zip code right now*, not generic pathway maps.

**3. Skills Translation Engine (MOS → Civilian Credentials)**
Your military veteran population is being underserved without this. An Army 131A (Fires/Targeting) or 91B (Wheeled Vehicle Mechanic) should walk in and see their MOS translated immediately into: civilian equivalents, transferable credit toward certifications, and the delta they need to close.

**4. WIOA Outcome Dashboard (Funder-Facing)**
Your teacher dashboard tracks learner progress. Your funders need to see *employment outcomes* — placement rates, 90-day retention, wage at placement. This is what turns a WIOA grant renewal from uncertain to automatic.

---

### Tier 2 — Platform Depth Improvements

**5. Trade Sim Depth Upgrade**
The five trade sims (Electrical, Plumbing, HVAC, Welding, Automotive) are your strongest differentiator. Each needs:
- A harder "job-site scenario" mode beyond the current physics sim
- AWS/NCCER/ASE alignment explicitly labeled on each module so learners know exactly which industry cert they're building toward
- Time-pressure scenarios that simulate real exam conditions

**6. Financial Literacy → Financial Navigation**
15 modules of financial literacy is good. What's missing is *decision consequence simulation* — show a learner what happens to their budget over 24 months if they choose payday loan vs. credit union loan vs. savings plan. The Stock Market Sim is close; it needs to connect to the real financial decisions WIOA populations face.

**7. Mentor Finder → Mentorship Operating System**
The Mentor Finder surface exists but matching alone isn't enough. Add: structured 4-week mentorship protocols, mentor accountability check-ins, and completion milestones tied to XP. This matters especially for your promotoras/CHW facilitator model.

---

### Tier 3 — Engagement & Retention

**8. Daily Anchor Mechanic**
Academy Village/Avatar/XP exists, but there needs to be one *daily reason to return* — a 10-minute "Daily Mission" that combines a trade concept, a financial literacy micro-lesson, and one career action step. This is what keeps WIOA participants engaged between cohort sessions.

**9. Houses → Team-Based Accountability**
The Houses (cohort teams) need a weekly group challenge tied to a real-world outcome — not just points, but something like "your house completes 3 mock job applications this week." Social accountability is a proven retention driver for your population.

**10. Offline/Low-Bandwidth Mode**
Rural workforce is one of your named verticals. If the platform requires strong connectivity, you're excluding the people you're trying to serve. Core trade sims and curriculum modules need a progressive web app (PWA) offline cache.
