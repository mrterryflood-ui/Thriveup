Hi Everyone!

Our next meeting is on June 11 at 1:00PM. We were planning to try to move back to in-person meetings. Toward that goal, @rob@georgetownproject.org or @Marco Cruz, can either of you host the meeting? If either of you can, please let us know as soon as possible so that we can update the invitation.

At our last meeting, we decided that updating the North Wilco Childcare Landscape Analysis would be our first action step in moving the model forward. I have attached the current Landscape Analysis as a reminder of our current baseline (remember the stats are dated).  ​Landscape%20Analysis_%20Child%20Care%20in%20North%20Williamson%20County_EXPANDED_ServiceArea_withMap.docx​ UWATX previously completed a Childcare Landscape Analysis for ATX that is more robust than our current document. Please take a look at the ATX Landscape Analysis and compare it to ours. You can access the ATX Landscape Analysis here. The City of San Antonio conducted a robust early childhood education Landscape Analysis that you can access here. The San Antonio analysis included early education (ISD Pre-K) and discussed families with targeted needs and sites that accept children with special care needs. 

Please review our current Landscape Analysis and compare it to the ATX and San Antonio documents. As you review the documents, consider these questions:
What information is included in the ATX and San Antonio Landscape Analysis that would be helpful to include in our North Wilco Landscape Analysis?
Do we have access to additional data that we would like to include in our analysis?
Presently, we do not include information from childcare providers. If we include provider information, what information would we like to have?
Do we have resources that could help us with a childcare provider survey?
If we are only able to ask 3 questions of childcare providers, what 3 questions would we want to ask to include in our analysis?
Is there other information missing from our analysis that would require data collection and, if so, how might we access this information?

You can send your comments via email or be prepared to have this discussion at our next meeting. Thank you and I look forward to seeing you all in-person on the 11th.
