I. The 5 Panther Power Domains (Spiral Framework)

Every grade builds across these five domains:
	1.	Education – Academic mastery and intellectual curiosity
	2.	Character – Ethics, resilience, self-regulation
	3.	Leadership – Influence, collaboration, responsibility
	4.	Entrepreneurship – Financial literacy, innovation, ownership
	5.	Community – Service, civic responsibility, mentorship

Each grade deepens complexity within all five.

The SIM platform becomes the experiential lab where these domains are practiced, not just taught.

⸻

II. Instructional Architecture

Each grade follows the same structural rhythm:
	1.	Identity Work
	2.	Career Exposure or Specialization
	3.	Financial & Life Literacy
	4.	Mentor Engagement
	5.	Portfolio & Evidence Creation
	6.	Milestone Demonstration
	7.	Reflection & Revision

That repetition builds cognitive stability while increasing complexity.
