
MEASURE Austin — founded 2015 by Jameila "Meme" Styles

What they do: Black-led nonprofit that uses data and research to fight structural racism and eliminate social disparities in Central Texas. Their tagline is essentially "show me the numbers" — Meme founded it after challenging the Austin Police Department to prove the data behind their performance claims.

Mission: Use data and education to mobilize communities to fight structural racism and eliminate social disparities.

Vision: A world free of health, education, criminal justice, and economic inequities.

How they work — three pillars:

Research — measurement, data collection, community-based surveys
Advocacy — action based on what the data reveals
Education — training communities and organizations to use data for equity
4 proprietary tools:

Community-Based Survey Tool
Equity Focus Group Tool
Community-Impact Metric Tool
CARE Model — their flagship evaluation framework, built because traditional evaluation fails the communities most impacted by inequity
Key programs:

The Innocence Initiative — addresses adultification bias against Black girls. Won Impact Austin's first social innovation grant. Over 1,040 attorneys trained. Led to Austin ISD adopting legislative agenda addressing inequitable discipline outcomes.
CROWN Act advocacy — helped pass hair discrimination protections in Austin, Pflugerville, and statewide
Community-Engaged Research Course at Huston-Tillotson University (HBCU in Austin)
Over 3,000 hours of free data and anti-racist evaluation support provided
Geographic focus: Austin, Pflugerville, Travis County — same territory as your regional hubs.
