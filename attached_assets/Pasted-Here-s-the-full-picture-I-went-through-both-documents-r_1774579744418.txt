The gaps that matter most:

No attack surface inventory — They want to see every entry point enumerated and classified. Your platform has them (API endpoints, event bus routes, 28 data feeds, service worker), but they're not formally documented or assessable from within the app.
No mission criticality mapping — Which of your 65 modules are mission-essential? What happens when one fails? What cascades through the event bus? You know this intuitively, but it's not built into the platform as a demonstrable tool.
No recovery objectives — How fast does each module restore after attack? What's acceptable data loss? The offline-first architecture IS your resilience, but without defined RTOs and RPOs, you can't prove it meets requirements.
