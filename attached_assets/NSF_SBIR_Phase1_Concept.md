# NSF SBIR Phase I Concept — M&T Consulting

## Project Title
**Community Intelligence Consensus Engine: A Multi-Model AI Architecture for Real-Time Social Determinants Analysis and Community Agency**

## Entity
M&T Consulting (For-Profit Small Business)
Dr. Terry Flood, Founder & CEO

## Technology Innovation

### The Problem
Community organizations, workforce development boards, local health departments, and frontline service providers lack access to integrated, real-time community data. Federal datasets (Census ACS, CDC PLACES, CDC/ATSDR SVI, FBI Crime Data, USDA Food Access, HUD, SAMHSA, BLS) exist in silos, require technical expertise to query, and produce raw statistics without actionable interpretation. The result: communities most in need of data-driven advocacy have the least access to the data that describes them.

### The Innovation: 4-Engine Consensus AI Architecture
ThriveUp Academy introduces a novel multi-model AI consensus architecture that eliminates single-model bias in community data interpretation:

| Engine | Model | Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Engine 1 | Google Gemini 2.0 Flash | Rapid data synthesis and pattern recognition |
| Engine 2 | Anthropic Claude Haiku 4.5 | Nuanced ethical reasoning and compassionate framing |
| Engine 3 | OpenAI GPT-4o-mini | Structured output and resource matching |
| Engine 4 | DeepSeek R1 | Deep analytical reasoning and cross-domain inference |

Every AI output is synthesized across all four engines — no single model's training bias, hallucination tendency, or cultural blind spot dominates the community data story. This is processed through a RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) knowledge base of 67+ curated chunks and validated against implementation science frameworks (RPLICE, CFIR 2.0, RE-AIM).

### Key Technical Components

**1. Neighborhood Intelligence Engine**
- Accepts any location format (ZIP, neighborhood name, street, city)
- Multi-layer geocoding: Census geocoder → Nominatim reverse geocode → address variation attempts
- Real-time Census ACS ZCTA-level queries across 15 social vulnerability indicators
- SVI computation using CDC/ATSDR methodology (4 themes, 16 variables)
- Automated strength/challenge identification with solution matching
- Live SAM.gov API integration for federal grant matching (up to 20 grants)
- "What If" scenario sandbox with real-time SVI recomputation

**2. RPLICE Implementation Science Instrument**
- Research-Practice-Linkage for Implementation, Community, and Equity
- Bridges academic implementation science (CFIR 2.0, RE-AIM) with community practice
- Embedded as validation layer in all AI outputs
- Provides fidelity measurement for community interventions

**3. 24-Platform Autonomous Collaborative Operating System**
- Platforms function as autonomous agents with inter-platform communication
- Shared data layer, authentication, and AI intelligence engine
- Spans 6 domains: Criminal Justice, Health Equity, Behavioral Health, Workforce & Business, Education & Learning, Community & Advocacy

## Phase I Objectives ($305,000 / 6-9 months)
1. Validate consensus AI accuracy against expert community assessments in 10 Austin-area ZIP codes
2. Conduct user testing with 50+ community health workers, faith leaders, and workforce counselors
3. Measure community agency outcomes (pre/post) using validated self-efficacy instruments
4. Demonstrate technical feasibility of scaling to all 41,000+ U.S. ZIP codes
5. Develop commercialization plan with 3+ LOIs from target customers

## Phase II Potential ($1.2M)

- Advanced health outcome integration (CDC PLACES at tract level)
- Predictive analytics for community vulnerability trends
- API marketplace for third-party platform integration
