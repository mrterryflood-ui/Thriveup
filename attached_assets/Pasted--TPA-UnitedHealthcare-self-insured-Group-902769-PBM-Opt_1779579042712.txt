## 4.2 The GLP-1 utilization picture

The single most operationally significant disclosure in Addendum #2 is the GLP-1 utilization data. We interpret it as follows:

**(a) Current GLP-1 spend is substantial and unmanaged.** At the conservative RAND 2023 figure of $13,800 per unmanaged GLP-1 patient per year, the 373 current GLP-1 members represent approximately $5.15 million in annual pharmacy cost to the County's medical plan. This spend is occurring with no clinical oversight beyond standard PBM prior authorization.

**(b) The ~47% twelve-month non-continuation rate is the diagnostic signal.** 705 members used a GLP-1 in the past twelve months, but only 373 are on therapy today — a 332-member differential, or approximately 47% non-continuation. We use "non-continuation" rather than pure "discontinuation" because the County figure may include planned course completions, approved-but-never-filled prior authorizations, within-class therapy switches, and members recently started who are still in titration. Even with those qualifications, the figure sits squarely within the published unmanaged-GLP-1 range of 30–50% twelve-month non-continuation (Gleason et al., JAMA Network Open 2024; AHIP real-world adherence analysis 2023), and the underlying drivers are well-established: side effects (nausea, fatigue) without titration support, absence of behavioral coaching, and no clinical follow-up.

**(c) The capture opportunity is concrete and addressable.** RAND's 2023 conservative figure for the cost differential between unmanaged and managed GLP-1 therapy is $5,600 per patient per year ($13,800 − $8,200). Applied to the 373 current GLP-1 members, the modeled Year-1 capture opportunity is approximately **$2.09 million** (373 × $5,600). Migrating the cohort to a managed protocol does not require a single new prescription or any change to OptumRx's formulary — it requires only that the existing 373 members receive structured clinical oversight, titration support, side-effect management, and behavioral coaching. We label this a "capture opportunity" rather than a "guaranteed saving" because realization depends on member uptake; we convert it into a contractual commitment through the at-risk pricing structure described in Section 8.5.

## 4.3 Current gap analysis

The Addendum disclosed: *"Current cost containment is tied to prior authorization program with PBM."* The County has identified this as insufficient and has stated its preference for *"a broader support-focused program centered around outcomes, health coaching participation, and sustainable lifestyle changes."*

We share the County's diagnosis. Prior authorization gates approval at the moment of prescription but does not address:
- Side-effect management during titration (the primary driver of unmanaged-GLP-1 non-continuation)
- Behavioral coaching to support sustained dietary and activity changes
- Lifestyle accountability and engagement over 12–24 months
- Coordination across members' broader care team (PCP, OB-GYN, mental health, etc.)
- Outcome measurement and reporting to the County

Vitality is designed to fill exactly this gap, while preserving OptumRx's PBM role and UnitedHealthcare's TPA role.

# Section 5 — Program Design: Vitality Comprehensive Weight Management

## 5.1 Program philosophy

Vitality is built on five principles:

1. **Member-centric, not vendor-centric.** Every design decision starts from the member's job-to-be-done.
2. **Evidence-based.** Every clinical recommendation cites the underlying study (DPP, STEP, LOOK AHEAD, RAND, NIH).
3. **Integration-first.** We coexist with the County's existing UHC, OptumRx, and Rally relationships. We do not replace them.
4. **Support-focused.** We meet the County's stated preference: coaching and clinical wraparound, not compliance enforcement.
5. **Outcomes over activity.** Every dashboard leads with the three KPIs the County named.

## 5.2 Member journey — the 5-phase clinical protocol

Every Vitality member moves through five phases. Each phase has defined entry and exit criteria, a clinical owner, and a measurable outcome.

| Phase | Duration | Owner | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Onboarding | 1–2 weeks | Vitality coaching | Identity verified, employer eligibility confirmed, consent collected, baseline survey complete |
| 2. Clinical baseline | 2–4 weeks | Dr. Love (Clinical) | Medical baseline, BMI, A1C, blood pressure, comorbidity inventory, pathway assignment |
| 3. Intensive intervention | 12–26 weeks | Dr. Love + Dr. Vann | Pathway-specific intervention (lifestyle, GLP-1, surgical-coordinated); weekly coaching cadence; biweekly clinical check |
| 4. Maintenance | 26–52 weeks | Dr. Vann (Behavioral) | Reduced cadence (biweekly to monthly); habit consolidation; outcome stabilization |
| 5. Graduation | Ongoing | Self-directed with quarterly check-in | Member graduates to alumni program with quarterly touchpoints; clinical and engagement data continue to feed reporting for the savings carryover model |

## 5.3 Three clinical pathways

Members are assigned to one of three pathways during Phase 2 (Clinical baseline), based on Dr. Love's clinical assessment, member preference, and clinical guideline match.

**Pathway A — Lifestyle Intervention** (estimated 72% of enrolled members)
- Evidence base: NIH/DPP Trial (58% reduction in type 2 diabetes progression at 5–10% weight loss)
- Components: structured nutrition coaching, activity tracking, behavioral group sessions led by Dr. Vann, weekly check-ins
- Clinical cadence: quarterly Dr. Love review
- Cost component: lifestyle baseline ($3,200/member/year all-in)

**Pathway B — GLP-1 Managed Care** (estimated 20% of enrolled members)
- Evidence base: STEP-1, STEP-2, STEP-3 trials (15–17% body weight reduction at 68 weeks)
- Components: GLP-1 prescription, structured titration, side-effect management, monthly clinical visits, weekly coaching, OptumRx fill-history reconciliation
- Clinical cadence: monthly Dr. Love visit during titration; quarterly during maintenance
- Member can elect: in-program prescribing (Dr. Love) OR coaching-only support with member's existing prescriber (per Addendum #2 confirmation that either model is acceptable)

**Pathway C — Surgical-Coordinated** (estimated 8% of enrolled members)
- Evidence base: LOOK AHEAD Trial (sustained 7–8% loss at 4 years with intensive lifestyle); bariatric surgery outcomes literature
- Components: pre-surgical clinical evaluation, post-surgical lifestyle support, coordination with member's chosen surgical center, long-term behavioral coaching
- Clinical cadence: aligned with surgical center protocol; Vitality provides wrap-around support

## 5.4 Behavioral engagement model

Dr. Vann's behavioral engagement model is the engine that sustains member engagement — the County's #1 KPI.

- **Weekly check-in cadence** during Phase 3 (intensive intervention), via the Vitality PWA, with structured prompts and a coaching response within 24 hours.
- **Biweekly group sessions** during Phase 3 and Phase 4, virtual cohort format, 8–12 members per cohort, facilitated by Dr. Vann or a licensed coach under her supervision.
- **Family and youth programming extensions** for members with dependents — addressing the household environment that drives sustainable weight management.
- **Automated escalation** for any member who does not check in for 14 days, routed to a human coach for outreach.
- **Engagement scoring** for the dashboard, surfaced to the County in quarterly reports.

## 5.5 VITA AI persistent-memory advisor

VITA is Vitality's AI coaching assistant, embedded in the member portal and the employer dashboard. VITA is differentiated from generic AI chatbots by three properties:

1. **Persistent memory.** Every member conversation is stored. VITA resumes context on every new session — there is no "starting over." A returning member sees a "Resuming — X prior exchanges" badge on their portal.
2. **Live clinical context.** VITA receives the member's current weight, BMI, GLP-1 status, active care plan phase, recent weight trend, and achieved milestones with every message. Responses are anchored to the member's real situation, not generic content.
3. **Evidence-cited responses.** VITA's clinical answers cite specific studies (DPP, STEP, LOOK AHEAD, RAND). This is a deliberate design choice for an RFP-evaluator audience as well as for clinical defensibility.

VITA operates in two modes: a warm, Dr. Love–voiced member mode, and an analytical, ROI-focused employer mode for the County dashboard. Both modes share the same persistent-memory architecture.

## 5.6 The GLP-1 managed-care protocol — the headline differentiator

This is the operational answer to the $2.09M Year-1 modeled capture opportunity. The protocol has six components:

1. **Initial clinical evaluation by Dr. Love** for every member entering Pathway B. Establishes baseline metrics, comorbidities, contraindications, and titration plan.
2. **Structured titration over 12–16 weeks** with proactive side-effect management. Members are contacted on day 3, day 7, day 14, and weekly thereafter during titration to surface and address side effects before they cause discontinuation. This is the single most important intervention against the GLP-1 non-continuation pattern visible in the County's 705-to-373 cohort transition.
3. **OptumRx fill-history reconciliation.** Vitality receives weekly fill-history feeds from OptumRx and reconciles them against expected fills. Members who miss a fill receive coaching outreach within 48 hours.
4. **Behavioral coaching from Dr. Vann's team** throughout titration and maintenance. Members on GLP-1 receive structured nutrition support, activity coaching, and sleep/stress modules — the lifestyle scaffolding that makes the medication work.
5. **Coordination with member's existing prescriber** (if the member chooses to remain with their PCP rather than have Dr. Love prescribe). Vitality acts as the supporting coaching and reporting layer; the member's PCP retains prescribing authority. Per Addendum #2, the County has confirmed this model is acceptable.
6. **Monthly clinical visits during titration; quarterly thereafter.** Dr. Love (or a credentialed clinician on her team) maintains direct medical oversight throughout the member's GLP-1 course.

The protocol directly targets the non-continuation pattern visible in the County's 705-to-373 cohort transition. Vitality commits to a measurable performance threshold tied to this protocol: **≥30% of the County's current 373-member GLP-1 cohort retained in active managed-protocol participation at month 12** (a conservative target relative to published managed-GLP-1 retention rates of 55–70%). At-risk pricing tied to this threshold is described in Section 8.5, converting the modeled capture opportunity into a contractual commitment.

The Vitality member experience is delivered as a Progressive Web Application (PWA). This means:

- **Offline capability.** Members can log weight, complete daily check-ins, and review coaching materials offline; data syncs when connectivity returns.

- **Accessibility:** Design conformance to WCAG 2.1 AA and Section 508; screen-reader support, keyboard navigation, and high-contrast mode in the current member portal. **Optional at-risk performance overlay (offered above base price, negotiable at contract):**
