What the oracle outputs:

At each developmental checkpoint (18m, 24m, 30m, 36m, 48m, 60m, kindergarten entry):

Trajectory classification: On track / Needs monitoring / Elevated concern / High priority intervention

Never generates a label for a child that follows them beyond the platform's use

Never frames output as prediction of criminality or failure — only as current need for support and the specific support indicated
Always surfaces the protective factor pathway alongside the risk flag
