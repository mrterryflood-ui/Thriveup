# ThriveUp Workforce Readiness Academy
## Curriculum Summary for Pflugerville ISD

**Program:** ThriveUp Workforce Readiness Academy
**Provider:** The Collaborative Advocate (501(c)(3))
**Contact:** Dr. Terry Flood, Founder & CEO
**Alignment:** 100% aligned to TEKS §127.15 — Career and Technical Education Employability Skills (adopted 2025)
**Duration:** 15 weeks per cohort
**Grades:** 9–12 CTE students
**Credential:** ThriveUp Workforce Readiness Certificate

---

## Program Overview

The ThriveUp Workforce Readiness Academy is a 15-week employability skills training program that covers all 20 TEKS §127.15 standards across 5 core modules. The program is AI-powered, allowing personalized pacing for each student, and results in a verifiable digital credential.

---

## Curriculum Modules

### Module 1: Professional Presence (3 weeks)
**TEKS Standard: (A) Professional Conduct**

Students learn to:
- Dress appropriately for different industries
- Communicate professionally in written and verbal formats
- Present themselves effectively in interviews
- Conduct themselves according to workplace behavioral expectations

Activities: Industry dress code research, professional email workshops, AI-powered mock interviews, workplace scenario role-plays, personal brand development

---

### Module 2: Workplace Rights & Responsibilities (3 weeks)
**TEKS Standard: (H) Discrimination & Harassment**

Students learn to:
- Define and identify workplace discrimination and harassment
- Understand employee rights under Title VII, ADA, and ADEA
- Follow reporting procedures
- Exercise bystander responsibilities
- Understand consequences for violations

Activities: EEOC case study analysis, scenario identification exercises, reporting procedure practice, bystander intervention training

---

### Module 3: Workplace Safety Essentials (3 weeks)
**TEKS Standard: (I) Workplace Safety**

Students learn to:
- Understand OSHA's role and workers' rights
- Identify common workplace hazards across industries
- Understand PPE requirements
- Outline emergency procedures and safety plan components
- Understand the right to refuse unsafe work

Activities: Multi-industry hazard hunts, PPE matching, emergency procedure walkthroughs, safety plan development, incident report documentation

---

### Module 4: Time & Priority Management (3 weeks)
**TEKS Standard: (D) Time Management**

Students learn to:
- Apply the Eisenhower Matrix for task prioritization
- Create and maintain digital calendars
- Set SMART goals with actionable milestones
- Identify and eliminate time-wasting behaviors
- Coordinate group schedules

Activities: Prioritization workshops, weekly planning challenges, time audits, group deadline simulations

---

### Module 5: Work Ethic & Career Leadership (3 weeks)
**TEKS Standards: (E) Work Ethic, (G) Meritocracy & Equal Opportunity, (J) Roles of Managers / Managers vs. Leaders**

Students learn to:
- Define work ethic and its core characteristics (punctuality, dependability, reliability, responsibility)
- Explain meritocracy and equal opportunity principles
- Describe organizational structures
- Compare managers and leaders
- Connect work ethic to career advancement

Activities: Self-assessments, dependability tracking, organizational chart building, manager vs. leader case studies, career character pledges

---

## Delivery Model

- **AI-Powered:** Students work through interactive lessons on the ThriveUp platform with personalized pacing — students who need more time get it without holding back others
- **Instructor-Led:** Trained facilitators guide each module with hands-on activities, group exercises, and real-world scenario practice
- **Bilingual:** Platform supports English and Spanish
- **Accessible:** WCAG 2.1 AA compliant, dyslexia-friendly fonts, high contrast options
- **24/7 Access:** Students can review material, practice scenarios, and complete assessments outside scheduled class time
