## Program Overview

The ThriveUp Workforce Readiness Academy is a 15-week, AI-powered employability skills program covering all 20 TEKS §127.15 standards across 5 modules. The curriculum is **culturally responsive** — designed for the students we actually serve: Black and Latino CTE students, first-generation career seekers, English Language Learners, students from low-income households, and students with disabilities in CTE pathways.

Every lesson features **relatable student characters** navigating real workplace scenarios in retail, food service, warehouse, and construction settings. A **progressive resume builder** threads across all 5 modules so students graduate with a complete professional resume built from their real experiences. The program integrates **neighborhood champions** (local professionals and mentors), **weekly live virtual check-ins**, **motivational video content**, and connections to ThriveUp's full ecosystem of career and community tools.

**This is not a worksheet program. It's built FOR students, not AT students.**

---

## Design Principles

- **Phone-First:** Delivered as a Progressive Web App (PWA) — students install it on Day 1 for 24/7 access from any device
- **Culturally Responsive:** Relatable scenarios, code-switching honored as a skill, "Real Talk" sections addressing actual barriers (no printer, no interview clothes, no transportation, family responsibilities)
- **Student-Centered:** Characters students see themselves in — Jaylen at H-E-B, Aaliyah at a clothing store, Marcus at a warehouse, Sofia at Chick-fil-A, DeAndre at an auto parts store
- **AI-Coached:** Private AI mock interview practice, email writing feedback, resume review — available anytime, judgment-free
- **Community-Connected:** Neighborhood champions, weekly live check-ins, motivational video posts, cohort accountability
- **Inclusive:** WCAG 2.1 AA compliant, dyslexia-friendly fonts, bilingual (English/Spanish), accommodations for students with disabilities

## Curriculum Modules

### Module 1: Professional Presence (3 weeks, 4 lessons)
**TEKS Standard: (A) Professional Conduct, (C) Communication**
**Character: Jaylen** — 16, just hired at H-E-B

Students learn to:
- Make strong first impressions using the 7-Second Rule
- Communicate professionally via email, phone, and in person
- Practice job interviews privately with the AI Mock Interview Lab
- Identify and professionally name existing skills (babysitting = childcare experience, church volunteering = event coordination)
- Download and set up ThriveUp PWA on Day 1

**Resume Builder Part 1:** Header, Objective Statement, Education

Activities: Professional Identity Inventory, AI Email Coach (write 3 professional emails with real-time feedback), AI Mock Interview Lab (Starter, Standard, and Tough levels), industry dress code research

**Real Talk:** What if you can't afford interview clothes? Resources for free professional clothing, thrift store tips, "clean and pressed matters more than brand names."

---

### Module 2: Workplace Rights & Responsibilities (3 weeks, 4 lessons)
**TEKS Standard: (H) Discrimination & Harassment**
**Character: Aaliyah** — 17, works at a clothing store

Students learn to:
- Identify workplace discrimination under Title VII, ADA, and ADEA
- Recognize harassment vs. uncomfortable-but-legal situations
- Practice bystander intervention using the 5 D's (Distract, Delegate, Document, Delay, Direct)
- Understand digital rights — employer monitoring, social media, NLRA wage discussion protections
- Conduct a personal Digital Presence Audit

**Resume Builder Part 2:** Skills Section (hard skills + soft skills with evidence)

Activities: "Is This Discrimination?" interactive scenarios (8 real-world cases), Bystander Intervention role-play with AI, Digital Presence Audit (Google yourself, review social media, set up LinkedIn)

**Real Talk:** "But I need this job" — documenting violations, anonymous EEOC reporting, free legal aid resources (Texas RioGrande Legal Aid, Lone Star Legal Aid).

---

### Module 3: Workplace Safety Essentials (3 weeks, 4 lessons)
**TEKS Standard: (I) Workplace Safety**
**Character: Marcus** — 16, works at a warehouse

Students learn to:
- Understand OSHA rights including the right to refuse dangerous work
- Identify hazards in retail, food service, warehouse, and construction environments
- Match PPE to specific workplace scenarios
- Execute emergency procedures (fire, medical, active threat)
- Write a formal incident report

**Resume Builder Part 3:** Certifications & Training Section

Activities: Workplace Hazard Hunt (5 industry photos), PPE Matching Challenge, Emergency Scenario Walkthroughs (interactive decision trees with AI), free certification guide (OSHA 10-Hour, Texas Food Handler, Google Digital Garage)

**Real Talk:** "But I'm new — I can't say no to my boss" — yes you can, and here's why it protects both of you.

---

### Module 4: Time & Priority Management (3 weeks, 4 lessons)
**TEKS Standard: (D) Time Management**
**Character: Sofia** — 17, AP classes + Chick-fil-A + caring for little brother

Students learn to:
- Apply the Eisenhower Matrix to real priorities (school, work, family, social)
- Build and maintain a color-coded digital calendar
- Set SMART goals with weekly milestones and accountability partners
- Identify and eliminate time-wasting patterns
- Write accomplishments using Google's XYZ resume formula

**Resume Builder Part 4:** Projects & Accomplishments Section

Activities: Time Audit (track 3 days in 30-minute blocks), Eisenhower Matrix with YOUR real tasks, Digital Calendar Setup, SMART Goal Workshop with AI coaching

**Real Talk:** "I don't have time" — acknowledging that not everyone has the same 24 hours, finding 30-60 recoverable minutes per day.

---

### Module 5: Work Ethic & Career Leadership (3 weeks, 4 lessons)
**TEKS Standards: (E) Work Ethic, (G) Meritocracy & Equal Opportunity, (J) Roles of Managers / Managers vs. Leaders**
**Character: DeAndre** — 18, about to graduate, asked to train new employees

Students learn to:
- Define work ethic through 4 pillars: punctuality, dependability, reliability, responsibility
- Understand organizational structures and how promotions actually work
- Compare managers vs. leaders and identify personal leadership style (Servant, Transformational, Democratic, Coaching)
- Connect work ethic to career advancement and earning power
- Complete and polish a professional resume with a tailored cover letter

**Resume Builder Part 5:** Final polish, Work/Volunteer Experience, References, Cover Letter tailored to a real job posting

Activities: Work Ethic Self-Assessment, Org Chart Builder, Leadership Style Quiz (AI-powered, 15 questions), Career Character Pledge, resume tailored to a real job posting with AI review

## Engagement & Support Model

### Neighborhood Champions
Local professionals, business owners, faith leaders, and mentors from the students' own communities are integrated throughout the program. Champions serve as:
- Guest speakers during weekly live check-ins
- Real-world examples referenced in lesson content
- Accessible mentors students can connect with after the program
- Proof that people who look like them have walked this path successfully

### Weekly Live Virtual Check-Ins
Every week, the cohort gathers for a live virtual session to:
- Ask questions they don't want to ask in class
- Hear from a neighborhood champion guest
- Share wins and challenges with peers
- Receive real-time motivation and accountability

### Motivational Video Content
Regular video posts from neighborhood champions and program alumni sharing:
- Communication stories and mistakes they made early on
- How they balanced work, school, and family
- Leadership moments that changed their career trajectory
- Honest advice they wish someone had given them

### ThriveUp Ecosystem Integration
The Workforce Readiness Academy connects students to ThriveUp's full platform:
- **AI Mock Interview Lab** — unlimited private practice with AI coaching
- **Career Pathways** — explore industries, salaries, and career ladders
- **Financial Literacy Hub** — understand what a paycheck actually means
- **Community Resource Directory** — free professional development resources nearby
- **Neighborhood Intelligence** — local job market data and opportunities
- **Apprenticeship Tracker** — paid training and earn-while-you-learn programs
- **AI Creation Studio** — build a professional portfolio website

## Delivery Model

- **AI-Powered:** Students work through interactive lessons on the ThriveUp platform with personalized pacing
- **Phone-First (PWA):** Students download ThriveUp to their phone on Day 1 — full access from any device, anytime
- **Instructor-Facilitated:** Trained facilitators guide each module with hands-on activities and group exercises
- **Neighborhood Champion-Supported:** Community mentors participate in weekly check-ins and share real-world content
- **Bilingual:** Platform supports English and Spanish
- **Accessible:** WCAG 2.1 AA compliant, dyslexia-friendly fonts, high contrast options, screen reader optimized
- **24/7 Access:** Students can review material, practice mock interviews, build their resume, and complete assessments outside scheduled class time
