## Important compensation issue

The proposed **7% plus 3% fee arrangement needs legal and procurement review before acceptance**.

Some federal grants and public procurement rules restrict or prohibit:

- Contingency fees based on an award
- Charging grant-writing costs directly to the award
- Percentage-based compensation
- Costs incurred before the authorized grant period
- Unapproved consultant procurement

A safer structure may be:

- Fixed-price opportunity assessment
- Fixed or milestone-based application fee
- Hourly technical assistance under a capped budget
- Separately authorized post-award administration fee
- Any success-based compensation used only when the specific funder and City counsel confirm it is permissible

This is not a legal determination. Manor’s attorney, procurement officer, and finance department should approve the compensation and procurement structure.
