**`justice-employer-backend.ts`** — infrastructure for data and employers:

**Schema** — 4 new tables: `justice_indicators` (normalized feed from BJS + housing courts, FIPS-coded, demographic-segmented), `foia_requests` (full lifecycle: draft → submitted → acknowledged → response received → closed), `foia_responses` (parsed documents linked back to requests), `employer_registrations` (self-registration pipeline with facilitator review queue).

**BJS ingestion pipeline** — `runBjsIngestion()` fetches two BJS NCRP public datasets, filters to Texas, normalizes into `justice_indicators`, skips rows gracefully without crashing the batch. Manual trigger at `POST /api/foia/ingest/bjs`. Annual scheduled call wires into server startup. No auth required from BJS — public CSVs.

**FOIA tracker** — `generateFoiaLetter()` produces a complete Texas Public Information Act letter with correct statutory citations (Tex. Gov't Code § 552.001, § 552.228), TCAF letterhead, 10-business-day response deadline, and TCAF-FOIA reference number. Four county clerk contacts pre-loaded with emails, addresses, and fax numbers. Three housing court request templates ready to customize. `ingestFoiaResponse()` takes parsed records from a response and loads them directly into `justice_indicators`.

**CareerOneStop feed** — `syncCareerOneStopJobs()` pulls DOL job listings filtered to trades, infers credential tags from job titles and SOC codes, syncs into `job_postings` with `barrierFriendly: true`. Runs daily. Needs two env vars: `CAREERONESTOP_USER_ID` and `CAREERONESTOP_API_KEY` (free registration at careeronestop.org/Developers).

**Employer registration** — `POST /api/employers/register` (public), `GET /api/employers/registrations` (facilitator queue), `PATCH /api/employers/registrations/:id/review` (approve → automatically creates `employer_partners` record and sets `approvedEmployerId`).

**`clinical-foia-employer.ts`** — the three instruments and routing engine:

**25-item RNR criminogenic needs screener** — built on Andrews & Bonta's Central Eight. Eight domains: criminal history (10pts), education/employment (8pts), family/social (6pts), substance use (8pts), housing (4pts), mental health (4pts), antisocial cognition (6pts), leisure/recreation (4pts). Total 0–50. Risk levels: low/moderate/high/very high. Flags domains at 50% of max, intervention-level at 75%. Recommends full LSI-R at score ≥ 20. No proprietary license required.

**PHQ-9** — Kroenke & Spitzer, public domain. 9 items, 0–3 each, total 0–27. Five severity levels. Item 9 (suicidal ideation) triggers immediate crisis referral — 988 surfaces immediately, facilitator notified, response never continues without surfacing crisis resources first.

**PCL-5** — Weathers et al., VA public domain. 20 items, 0–4 each, total 0–80. Four DSM-5 clusters (B/C/D/E). Cutoff ≥ 33 = probable PTSD. Dominant cluster identified to guide treatment focus.

**Referral routing engine** — `generateReferrals()` maps domain scores to your existing partner network: CommUnity Care FQHCs, Integral Care, Austin Recovery, LifeBridge, Foundation Communities, HACA Section 8, ACC Continuing Education, Workforce Solutions Capital Area, Texas RioGrande Legal Aid (expungement), Lone Star Justice Alliance, Thinking for a Change (CBT), ThriveUp Academy trade sims. Urgency levels: immediate/within_week/within_month/routine. Crisis override always fires first. Referrals deduplicated by resource name.

**`frontend-pages-complete.tsx`** — three pages:

**ClinicalScreening** at `/clinical-screening/:participantId` — five-step flow: intro (explains instruments, surfaces 988) → RNR (25 yes/no questions by domain) → PHQ-9 (9 frequency questions, item 9 flagged in red with crisis language) → PCL-5 (20 intensity questions) → Results (score cards, crisis alert if triggered, referral list with urgency badges, clinical interpretation notes). All three instruments co-administered in one session.

**FoiaTracker** at `/foia-tracker` — list view with status pipeline (draft → submitted → acknowledged → processing → response received → closed), overdue flags when deadline passes, one-click status updates. Create view generates the full TPIA letter ready to copy and email.

**EmployerRegistration** at `/employer/register` — public-facing. Credential tag selector (10 trade categories as toggle chips). At least one fair-chance commitment required to submit. Confirmation screen on success.

**What the platform can now do that it couldn't before:**

A facilitator opens `/clinical-screening/participant-123`. In 20 minutes they have a criminogenic needs score, a depression severity rating, a trauma indicator, and a referral list mapped to real Austin-area organizations — automatically generated, no manual lookup. If the PHQ-9 item 9 fires, 988 is on screen before the results page even loads.

A staff member opens `/foia-tracker`, selects Travis County Clerk, describes the housing court records they need, and gets a complete TPIA-compliant letter ready to email. When the response comes back, they log it in the same interface and the data flows into `justice_indicators` — which feeds the equity dashboard and policy signal engine.

An employer lands on `/employer/register`, checks "Ban the Box," selects "electrical" and "hvac" credential tags, submits. A facilitator reviews and approves. The employer's open positions immediately appear in the job board matched to learners with those credentials.

The CareerOneStop feed runs daily and seeds the job board with DOL listings so it's never empty while employer partnerships are being built.
