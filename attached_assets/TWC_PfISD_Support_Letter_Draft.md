RFA 32026-00162 — Workforce Readiness Training for CTE Students II

Dear TWC Grant Review Committee,

I am writing in support of The Collaborative Advocate's application for the Workforce Readiness Training for CTE Students II grant (RFA 32026-00162). Pflugerville Independent School District is aware of and supports this proposed program.

The Collaborative Advocate's founder, Dr. Terry Flood, currently serves on the Pflugerville ISD School Health Advisory Council (SHAC), and we have an established working relationship with his organization. Dr. Flood has demonstrated a genuine commitment to the success and well-being of our students.

The proposed ThriveUp Workforce Readiness Academy is a 15-week employability skills training program aligned to TEKS §127.15 (Career and Technical Education Employability Skills). The program covers all required standards across professional conduct, workplace rights, workplace safety, time management, and work ethic — areas that directly support the goals of our CTE pathways.

Pflugerville ISD supports this partnership and is willing to:

- Coordinate with The Collaborative Advocate on access to CTE students in grades 9–12
- Work with our CTE department to integrate the program into existing career pathways
- Provide classroom or lab space for instructor-led sessions as available and appropriate
- Share FERPA-compliant student outcome data for program evaluation purposes

We understand that this program is fully funded through the TWC grant and requires no financial commitment from the district. The Collaborative Advocate will provide all curriculum materials, trained facilitators, technology platform access, and student credentialing at no cost to PfISD.

We believe this program will strengthen our students' readiness for the workforce and complement our existing CTE offerings. We look forward to collaborating with The Collaborative Advocate on this initiative.
