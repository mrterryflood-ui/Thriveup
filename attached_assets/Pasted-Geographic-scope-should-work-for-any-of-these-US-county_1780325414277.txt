US county (e.g., county=Burnet, state=TX)

Zip code
Census tract
Congressional district
Tribal nation/territory
Multi-county region
Endpoint 1 — Community Profile:
GET /api/community/profile?state=TX&county=Burnet
(or city=, zip=, tract=, district=, tribe= as alternatives)

Return everything you have on:

Who they are

Population size, density, geographic type (urban/suburban/rural/frontier)
Age, race/ethnicity, language, nativity breakdowns
Median income, poverty rate, child poverty, senior poverty
Gini coefficient / inequality index
Health picture

Uninsured and underinsured rate
Chronic disease burden (diabetes, hypertension, heart disease, obesity, asthma, cancer)
Behavioral health: mental illness prevalence, substance use, suicide rate
Maternal/infant: maternal mortality, infant mortality, low-birthweight rate
Life expectancy, premature death rate
HPSA (Health Professional Shortage Area) status
Nearest emergency room distance / hospital access
Oral health, vision care gaps
Economic reality

Unemployment, underemployment, labor force participation
Top 5 industries and employers
Wage levels by sector
SNAP/WIC/Medicaid/CHIP enrollment rates
Eviction rate, housing cost burden (>30% / >50% income on housing)
Homeownership rate, vacancy rate
Business formation rate, minority-owned business count
Opportunity Zone designation (yes/no)
Education

HS graduation rate, dropout rate
3rd-grade reading proficiency, 8th-grade math proficiency
College-going rate, college completion rate
Adult literacy rate
CTE program availability
Title I school count / percentage
Digital access: broadband penetration, device access in homes
Workforce & skills

Skills gap by industry sector
Credentialing/certification needs
Returning citizens / justice-involved population data
Veterans population, veteran unemployment
Youth NEET rate (Not in Education, Employment, or Training)
Social determinants

Food desert / food swamp designation
Transportation: car-free households, transit access
Childcare desert status, childcare cost burden
Domestic violence / adverse childhood experiences (ACEs) data
Disability rate
Broadband / digital divide
Special federal designations (these unlock specific grant eligibility)

Promise Zone
Opportunity Zone
HUD Choice Neighborhoods
USDA Rural designation
Medically Underserved Area (MUA/MUP)
Indian Health Service catchment area
Empowerment Zone
Promise Neighborhood
CDFI designated distressed community
Political & civic context

Local government structure (county commission, city council, mayor-council, etc.)
Recent elections / political composition
Existing community-based organizations, nonprofits active in the area
Active federal/state programs already operating here
Recent disasters or declared emergencies (opens FEMA/CDBG-DR lanes)
Community narrative

A vivid, data-backed story of this community's strengths, challenges, and aspirations — written at a level that can go directly into a grant proposal
Endpoint 2 — Matched Funding Opportunities:
GET /api/community/opportunities?state=TX&county=Burnet

For this community, return every funding program that fits their profile:

Program name and funder (federal agency, foundation, state agency)
Funding type (grant, contract, loan, tax credit)
Amount range (floor / ceiling)
Deadline or cycle
Why it matches this community specifically (plain-language eligibility reasoning)
Application URL
Whether a nonprofit, for-profit, or government entity applies
Endpoint 3 — Comparative Context:
GET /api/community/compare?state=TX&county=Burnet

How does this community rank vs.:

State average
National average
Peer counties (similar size/geography/demographics)
Useful for making the "need" case in proposals.

Auth format: API key in header — Authorization: Bearer <key> or X-API-Key: <key> — whichever your system uses.
