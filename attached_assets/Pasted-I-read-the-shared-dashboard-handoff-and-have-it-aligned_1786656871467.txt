### Alignment decisions now captured
- **Private partner dashboard:** organization-bound, authenticated, scope-enforced access.
- **Public shared dashboard:** separate, narrowly scoped visibility—not the private dashboard exposed publicly.
- **Share tokens:** must be opaque, high-entropy, hashed server-side, organization-bound, expiry/revocation-capable, and read-only.
- **No partner key exposure:** a `tcaf_*` key must never appear in a share URL, browser storage, browser request header, or public page.
- **No public writes:** referrals, outcomes, key management, and any client/household data remain unavailable from shared links.
- **Evidence integrity:** partner narratives and calculated indicators remain source material; they do not become ECS-reviewed evidence or reports automatically.
