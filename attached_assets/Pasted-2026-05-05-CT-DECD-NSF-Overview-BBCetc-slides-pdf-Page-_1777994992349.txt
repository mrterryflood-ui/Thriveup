Small Business Innovation and Economic Security Act
Ø
Senate Bill S.3971 became PL 119
-
83 on April 13, 2026
Ø
Key elements and changes:
Ø
AUTHORIZES both SBIR & STTR (and existing pilots) until Sept 30, 2031
Ø
EXPANDS Direct to Phase II to STTR and adds DOE and NASA (keeps NIH, DOD & Education)
Ø
NEW Strategic Breakthrough Awards
Ø
Up to $30 M as Phase II follow on
Ø
Requires 1:1 outside matching funds
Ø
DOD has additional matching requirements
Ø
UPDATED Foreign Influence disclosures
Ø
List of Prohibited Entities
Ø
Agencies MUST provide reason for denials, AND resubmission is allowed
Ø
NEW Each Agency must define annual proposal limits (by company, solicitation or Topic)
Ø
NEW Phase III improvements (Agency staff training and simplified & standard procedures)
Ø
UPDATES Technical and Business Assistance (TABA )
Ø
All Agencies must participate
Ø
Amounts remain the same ($6,500 Phase I, $50,000 Phase II)
