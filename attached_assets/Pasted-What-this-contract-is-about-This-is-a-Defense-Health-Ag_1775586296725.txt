What this contract is about:

This is a Defense Health Agency (DHA) contract called "Deployment Solutions" under PEO DHMS (Defense Healthcare Management Systems). It's a multiple-award IDIQ (meaning multiple companies can win task orders under it).

What they need:

The government needs contractors to deploy their healthcare IT products (like MHS GENESIS / military electronic health records) to military treatment facilities. Specifically, the work includes:

Site Preparation — getting facilities ready before the software goes live
Deployment/Installation — actually rolling out DHMS products at each site
Training — teaching staff how to use the new systems
User Adoption — making sure people actually use the systems properly
Change Management — helping organizations transition from old systems to new ones
Post-Installation Support — staying on after go-live to fix issues and ensure smooth operations
The goal is seamless integration with minimal disruption to existing healthcare systems.

Key details:

NAICS: 541512 (Computer Systems Design Services)
Due: April 15, 2026 (8 days)
No small business set-aside — open competition
18 attachments including the PWS (Performance Work Statement), QASP, and 11 CDRLs (deliverable requirements) covering things like Quality Control Plan, Training Curriculum, Deployment Approach, Transition Plans, Implementation Plan, and Integrated Master Schedule
Bottom line: They want companies that can show up at military hospitals, deploy healthcare IT systems, train the staff, manage the change, and stick around until everything runs smoothly.
