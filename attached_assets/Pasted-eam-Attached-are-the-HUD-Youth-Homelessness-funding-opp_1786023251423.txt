RFP summary

HUD expects to award approximately 49 YHSI grants of about $1 million each for a 30-month, nonrenewable project. No matching funds are required. The application is due August 10, 2026, at 11:59:59 p.m. Eastern through HUD’s e-snaps system.

YHSI supports system-level work rather than housing or direct services. Eligible work includes youth leadership, community planning, cross-agency coordination, referral systems, education and employment connections, HMIS/data capacity, training, evaluation, and organizational capacity building.

Proposed project

The proposed Turning Point Youth Homelessness Response Collaborative would focus on Wichita and Sedgwick County. Its goal would be to establish a coordinated, youth-informed system that identifies children and young adults experiencing or at risk of homelessness and connects them with existing housing, education, employment, behavioral-health, and family-stability resources.

Suggested Prime Fit work share

Serve as the legal applicant and fiscal recipient.

Oversee partner agreements and project expenditures.
Lead or co-lead youth engagement, mentoring, positive youth development, and the Youth Action Board.
Assign fiscal and program-management staff for the 30-month project.
Participate in evaluation, performance reporting, and sustainability planning.
