# ThriveUp Academy
## Executive Summary — Evaluation & Feedback Draft

---

## What It Is

ThriveUp Academy is an AI-powered workforce development and community enablement platform that serves as a complete grant readiness infrastructure for nonprofit organizations. It combines digital literacy training, career development, substance use prevention, behavioral health integration, case management, and community coalition building into a single, interconnected system. The platform is not just a learning management system — it is the operational backbone that helps organizations win, execute, and report on federal contracts in the nonprofit space.

The platform currently hosts 90+ functional pages spanning 11 operational domains: Community Intelligence, Workforce Solutions, Coalition Management, Grant Engine, AI Tools, Student Portal, Campus Life, Teaching & Staff, Prevention & Wellness, Case Management, and Build & Create.

---

## Who It Serves

ThriveUp Academy serves **all ages and all circumstances**, with specific programming tailored for:

- **Justice-Involved & Returning Citizens** — Reentry case management with phase-based plans (Pre-Release through Independence), milestone tracking, court-ready reporting, and fair-chance employer pipelines
- **Opportunity Youth (Ages 16-24)** — Career exploration, AI mastery curriculum, digital literacy, and workforce readiness aligned with all 14 WIOA youth program elements
- **K-12 Students (Ages 10-18)** — Substance prevention curriculum (24 modules across 8 substances and 3 age tiers), gamified learning campus (Panther Village Academy), STAAR test prep, and social-emotional development
- **Single Parents** — Parent prevention education (13 modules), family strengthening assessments, AI-powered conversation starters, and workforce development pathways
- **Veterans & Military Families** — Military-to-civilian career translation, workforce assessment, transition support, and veteran-specific resource navigation
- **Seniors** — Digital literacy training, community resource access, and intergenerational engagement
- **Neurodivergent Individuals** — Served through the Perfectly Different ecosystem partner, with neurodiversity-affirming tools and accommodations

**Stakeholder Ecosystem (10 populations surveyed and engaged):**
Youth, Parents, Educators, Law Enforcement, Healthcare Providers, Faith-Based Organizations, Business/Employers, Veterans, Returning Citizens, and Seniors

## Where It Operates

- **Community-level deployment** with interactive GIS mapping pulling real-time data from CDC PLACES, CDC/ATSDR Social Vulnerability Index, FBI Crime Data, Census Bureau ACS, USDA Food Access Atlas, HUD, SAMHSA, and BLS
- **School district integration** through K-12 classroom management tools and curriculum document alignment

## Why It Matters

### The Problem
Under-resourced communities face fragmented services. A returning citizen might need job training, substance use support, housing assistance, and digital literacy — but those services exist in separate organizations with separate intake processes, separate data systems, and no coordination. Federal grants require evidence of measurable outcomes, cross-sector collaboration, and implementation fidelity — requirements that overwhelm organizations using spreadsheets and disconnected tools.

### The Solution
ThriveUp Academy eliminates silos by connecting every service domain into a single data ecosystem where:
- A risk assessment taken by a youth automatically informs DFC Core Measures reporting
- An evidence-based program implementation creates a dosage tracking entry
- A coalition meeting action item links to an environmental strategy
- A media campaign's reach updates the RE-AIM implementation science dashboard
- Every tool shows "Related Tools" cards with live metrics from connected features
- A unified Command Center dashboard pulls real-time data from all subsystems simultaneously

### The Differentiator
This is not a single-purpose tool. It is a **grant readiness infrastructure** that:
1. **Discovers** opportunities (SAM.gov API integration with AI-powered fit scoring)
2. **Prepares** applications (Logic Model builder, Grant Narrative generator pulling live platform data, readiness checklists)
3. **Executes** programming (24-module prevention curriculum, 50+ career pathways, case management, coalition management)
4. **Measures** outcomes (RE-AIM, CFIR, SPF fidelity, WIOA performance indicators, DOJ PMT alignment)
5. **Reports** results (SAMHSA Core Measures format, CSV/PDF export, court-ready reporting, WIOA-compatible service hours)

## How It Identifies, Wins, and Executes Contracts

### Phase 1: Identify Opportunities
- **Grant Discovery Engine** searches SAM.gov in real-time with semantic analysis
- **AI Fit Scoring** evaluates each opportunity against platform capabilities (youth reentry, digital literacy, prevention, workforce development)
- **Grant Alignment Matrix** maps all 8 ecosystem platforms against 5 federal funding streams to show exactly which capabilities align with which grants
- **Community Needs Assessment** uses real-time federal data (CDC, FBI, Census, USDA, HUD, SAMHSA, BLS) to document community need with authoritative data

### Phase 2: Win the Contract
- **Logic Model Builder** creates interactive theory-of-change visualizations using the Relief-Stabilize-Contribute framework, auto-populated with platform activities and outcomes
- **Grant Narrative Generator** drafts application sections using AI, pulling live metrics (participants served, service hours, outcome rates) directly from the platform
- **DFC Application Wizard** walks through all 10 steps of a DFC application from SAM.gov verification to final submission readiness
- **Coalition Setup Wizard** builds a 12-sector coalition from scratch with sector mapping, member recruitment, and capacity assessment
- **Community Readiness Assessment** implements the Tri-Ethnic Center model with 6-dimension scoring and 9 readiness stages
- **Stakeholder Commitment Tracker** documents letters of support and MOUs across all 12 required DFC sectors
- **Cost Match Documentation** tracks in-kind and cash contributions against the 100% federal match requirement
- **DFC Readiness Checklist** verifies SAM.gov registration, UEI, Grants.gov account, coalition eligibility, and all application components

### Phase 3: Execute the Program
- **Prevention Curriculum** delivers 24 evidence-based modules across 8 substance topics and 3 age tiers (10-14, 15-18, 19-24)
- **10 SAMHSA/NIDA Evidence-Based Programs** tracked with implementation fidelity scoring (LifeSkills Training, Communities That Care, Strengthening Families, Project SUCCESS, and more)
- **7-Category Environmental Strategies** tracker covers access reduction, social norms change, policy/enforcement, community design, economic incentives, media advocacy, and coalition capacity building
- **Parent Education** with 13 modules, family assessments, and AI conversation starters
- **Career Pipelines** with 50+ pathways from exploration to job placement and 30/90/365-day retention tracking
- **Case Management** with smart intake wizard, service delivery records, and phase-based reentry plans
- **AI Creation Studio** with 10 professional-grade AI tools for content creation, workforce preparation, and digital literacy
- **Dosage Tracking** records every service hour across every tool for grant-reportable participation data

### Phase 4: Measure & Report
- **DFC Command Center** — Single dashboard pulling live metrics from all subsystems: coalition health, prevention impact, community engagement, and grant readiness
- **SAMHSA Core Measures** — 30-day substance use rates, perception of risk/harm, parental disapproval, peer disapproval, age of first use, and substance availability by age group
- **RE-AIM Framework** — Reach, Effectiveness, Adoption, Implementation, Maintenance scores computed from live data
- **CFIR Assessment** — 5-domain implementation research scoring (Intervention Characteristics, Outer Setting, Inner Setting, Individuals, Implementation Process)
- **Stakeholder Surveys** — 10 population-specific survey templates with coverage tracking
- **Outcome Reporting** — DOJ-aligned metrics with CSV export
- **WIOA Performance Indicators** — Employment rates, credential attainment, measurable skill gains
- **Period Comparison** — Baseline vs. follow-up data with trend arrows and directional indicators

### Phase 5: Sustain & Improve
- **MAP-GAP CQI Engine** — Continuous quality improvement with 5-phase cycles: Monitor, Analyze, Plan, Gap-Close, Assess Progress
- **Community Readiness Tracking** — Stage progression over time with recommendations to advance to the next level
- **Coalition Capacity Assessment** — Ongoing organizational health monitoring
- **Media Campaign Planner** — Sustained community awareness with population-specific messaging, campaign calendars, and reach/engagement metrics
- **First 30 Days Onboarding** — Population-specific guided onboarding for new participants with milestone tracking

## Implementation Science Foundation

Every feature is grounded in recognized frameworks:
- **Strategic Prevention Framework (SPF)** — SAMHSA's 5-step process for substance use prevention
- **RE-AIM** — Reach, Effectiveness, Adoption, Implementation, Maintenance
- **CFIR** — Consolidated Framework for Implementation Research
- **Tri-Ethnic Center Community Readiness Model** — 9-stage community readiness assessment
- **RPLICE** — Implementation science engine for continuous quality improvement
- **WIOA 14 Program Elements** — Full alignment with federal youth workforce requirements

## Technology Foundation

- **Frontend:** React + Vite + Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui components
- **Backend:** Express.js + Node.js + PostgreSQL (Neon) + Drizzle ORM
- **AI Integration:** Google Gemini Flash, OpenAI, provider-agnostic architecture
- **Authentication:** OIDC-based authentication with role-based access
- **Data Sources:** CDC PLACES, CDC/ATSDR SVI, FBI Crime Data, Census Bureau ACS, USDA Food Access Atlas, HUD, SAMHSA, BLS, SAM.gov
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.1 AA compliant with dyslexia-friendly fonts, high contrast mode, reduced motion, screen reader optimization
- **Languages:** English and Spanish (i18n support)
- **Security:** Authentication required on all sensitive endpoints, Zod validation on all inputs
