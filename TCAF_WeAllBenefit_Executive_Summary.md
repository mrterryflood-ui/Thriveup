Across the five-county region:
- **1,700,156 people are eligible** for at least one public benefit
- **1,029,573 are enrolled** (60.5%)
- **670,583 are NOT enrolled** (39.5% gap rate)
- This represents **$3.2 billion in unclaimed annual benefits**

The crisis is not equal across programs.

## OUR APPROACH: TCAF AS THE TECH CONDUIT

The Collaborative Advocate Foundation is **not** another enrollment organization trying to compete with existing nonprofits. We are the **coalition backbone**—the data platform, the technology, the coordination engine that makes 18 existing organizations work together as one system instead of 18 isolated efforts.

### What We Bring

**1. Benefits Intelligence System**
- Census-tract-level data across all 501 high-need tracts in five counties
- Real-time eligibility estimates by benefit program, language, transportation access, and digital capacity
- AI-powered barrier profiling: we identify where language is the bottleneck versus where transportation is the problem
- Outcome tracking: every person screened, enrolled, and renewed is tracked by program and by barrier type
- No organization loses data; all data flows into a shared portal visible to authorized partners

**2. Three-Minute Screening Tool**
- Single intake form checking eligibility for 9 programs simultaneously: SNAP, Medicaid, CHIP, EITC, WIC, SSI, SSDI, Marketplace, CTC
- Available online AND offline (progressive web app for field staff with no internet)
- Generates personalized enrollment pathway for each person
- API-connected to partner organizations' systems (if they choose integration)

**3. Outreach Modality Engine**
- Algorithm that matches outreach strategy to neighborhood barrier profile
- High language barriers + low broadband → door-to-door + paper applications + bilingual CHWs
- High transportation barriers → co-located services and mobile enrollment
- High digital capacity → online enrollment + SMS reminders
- Not one-size-fits-all; precision targeting by census tract

**4. Community Health Worker (CHW) Deployment & Tracking**
- Recruiting, training, and deploying CHWs into high-need neighborhoods
- Real-time GPS tracking + completion logging + barrier documentation
- Payment coordination across multiple partners (CHWs can serve multiple organizations but report to one system)
- Career pathway: from volunteer to hired to credentialed to supervisory roles
- Criminal justice reform partnership: we place people with records into paid CHW roles (proven impact: criminal justice involvement drops when people have employment + community connection + stable benefits)

**5. Partner Coordination Portal**
- Real-time dashboard showing: screenings by census tract, enrollments by program, barrier types, partner capacity, renewal dates, and outcomes
- Calendar coordination: which organizations are doing events where and when (prevents duplication, enables co-location)
- Capacity planning: when a partner is overwhelmed, the system routes clients to nearby partners
- HHSC Community Partner Program (CPP) pathway: we guide partners through levels 1-3 of state certification

**6. Sustainability & Compliance**
- TCAF houses three entities: TCAF (nonprofit/grants), CIP LLC (tech/AI development), M&T Consulting (staffing/payroll)
- This structure allows us to absorb costs that individual nonprofits cannot, reducing dependence on fragile grants
- We pursue CPP contracts with HHSC directly, creating revenue streams beyond foundation funding
- Partners stay funded through their own grants + shared data infrastructure reduces overhead

### **Line of Effort 5: Retention & Renewals**
**Goal:** Ensure people don't lose benefits after enrollment (retention crisis that St. David's prioritizes equally with initial enrollment).

**The Renewal Problem:**
Medicaid, SNAP, CHIP, and CTC all require annual or biennial renewals. A person can enroll in March, then lose coverage in August because they missed a renewal deadline, missed a notice (language barrier), or couldn't navigate the renewal system.

**Our Renewal Infrastructure:**
- Automated calendar alerts 90 days before renewal deadline (SMS + email + mail, based on language preference)
- Simplified renewal assistance: CHWs help people through recertification process face-to-face or by phone
- Barrier-matched renewal: if transportation was a barrier at enrollment, we provide assistance at the same co-location site
- Real-time renewal tracking: dashboard shows renewal due dates and completion status for every person enrolled
- Appeal support: if renewal is denied, we provide free appeal assistance

**Year 1 Cohort:** ~35,000 people enrolled → Year 2 focus on keeping them enrolled (target: 95% renewal rate)  
**Year 2 Cohort:** ~100,000 people enrolled Year 1 + Year 2 → Year 3 focus on sustaining (target: 95%+ across all cohorts)

**Responsible:** TCAF + Partner organizations (CHWs + case managers)  
**Success Metric:** 95% renewal completion rate across all programs by Year 3
