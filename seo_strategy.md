# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing and landing pages
- Public program, initiative, resource, and informational pages
- Public community storytelling and intake surfaces intended to be shareable or discoverable
- Public static files that act as crawlable landing pages

## Out of scope
- Authenticated dashboards and admin workspaces
- Internal grant, proposal, and staff-only tools behind `RequireAuth`
- API endpoints except where they directly affect crawlability (`robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt`)

## Target audience
- Under-resourced communities of all ages seeking workforce development, education, benefits, and support
- Community partners, funders, and public-sector collaborators evaluating ThriveUp

## Primary keywords
- ThriveUp
- workforce development
- community enablement
- AI education
- career pathways
- community resource platform
