### 3. Texas HHS — Rural Border Intervention (RBI) Program
**THIS IS THE BIG ONE — Up to $330K/year for 5 years**

**What it is:**
- HHSC grant for Substance Use Intervention services to Youth and Adults in remote rural border areas
- Covers HHS Regions 8, 10, and 11 (South Texas border)
- Total program: $990K/year, max $330K per awardee per fiscal year
- 5-year contract period = up to $1.65M total per awardee

**Why TCAF fits:**
- Community Health Worker delivery model
- Substance use intervention services
- Youth and adult programming
- CHW defined in RFA as "liaison providing cultural mediation between health care, social services, and the community"

**Full RFA document:**
https://resources.hhs.texas.gov/sites/default/files/documents/hhs0012454_rural_border_intervention_rbi_rfa_final.pdf

**Do you have to be in the border region?** You need to SERVE Regions 8, 10, or 11 — you don't have to be headquartered there. TCAF could partner with border-region organizations or establish service delivery sites.

**Action:** Download and review the RFA PDF above. Check if this is still accepting applications (it may be an open enrollment).

## RESIDENCY REQUIREMENTS — GENERAL RULES

| RFP Type | Residency Required? |
|----------|-------------------|
| Texas state agency (DSHS/HHSC) | Must serve Texas populations. HQ can be anywhere in TX. TCAF qualifies. |
| City of Austin | No strict requirement but strong local preference. TCAF (Pflugerville) qualifies as local. |
| Federal pass-through (like SMVF) | No state restriction for the federal component, but TX HHS administers it for Texas only. |
| County/ISD RFPs | Often require local presence. Varies by solicitation. |

**Bottom line: TCAF is positioned perfectly for all Texas-based opportunities. Being in Pflugerville gives you local status for Austin and Travis County.**
