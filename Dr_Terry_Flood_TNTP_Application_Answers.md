# TNTP Application — Partner, Education to Career
## Dr. Terry Flood

---

### Why TNTP? What draws you to this work?

I built a 24-platform technology ecosystem from my living room in Pflugerville, Texas — not because someone asked me to, but because I watched communities get served by systems that were never designed for them. Workforce development in one building. Health services across town. Education somewhere else entirely. Justice-involved young people falling through every crack between them.

TNTP's Education to Career work is the organizational version of what I've been building independently — the recognition that career pathways don't live inside any single system. They live in the space between PK-12, postsecondary, workforce boards, employers, and communities. That space is where I work.

What draws me specifically is TNTP's commitment to evidence over aspiration. I built an implementation science system (RPLICE) that uses CFIR 2.0 and RE-AIM frameworks to measure whether programs actually work in the communities they serve — not just whether they exist on paper. That's the same rigor I see in TNTP's research-to-practice approach, and I want to bring that discipline to ecosystem-level career pathway work at national scale.

I'm also drawn by the honesty in TNTP's mission — the acknowledgment that even excelling in school doesn't outweigh the effects of poverty. I've spent years proving that with Census tract data: when 2-parent households fall below 60% in a tract, poverty exceeds 17% — every single case I've studied. County averages hide this. Tract-level data reveals it. That kind of truth-telling is what TNTP does, and it's what I do.

### What experience qualifies you for this role?

I've spent the last seven years building exactly what this role describes — cross-sector career pathway systems that connect education, workforce, employers, and communities. Here's what that looks like in practice:

**Career Pathway Design at Scale:** I designed and launched 50+ stackable career pathways through ThriveUp Academy, each aligned to labor market demand data for the Austin MSA. These aren't theoretical — they include CNA, CompTIA, CDL, Community Health Worker certification, welding, and advanced manufacturing credentials, each mapped to specific employer partners and wage outcomes.

**Cross-Sector Coalition Building:** I lead partnerships spanning Workforce Solutions Capital Area (our local workforce development board), employer partners including Ascension Seton, H-E-B, Dell Technologies, Samsung Austin Semiconductor, and Tesla Gigafactory, community health centers (CommUnityCare), and PK-12 systems. These aren't MOU-only relationships — they're active referral pipelines with signed commitment letters.

**Business Development & Fundraising:** I manage a $3.5M+ active grant pipeline that includes federal sources (DOJ Second Chance Act — $1M, DOL/WIOA Title I Youth — $500K), state funding (Texas Veterans Commission, St. David's Health Equity — $1M), and foundation grants (Rare Impact Fund — $500K, Austin FC Dream Starter — $100K, Texas Capital Foundation — $100K). I draft proposals, lead funder briefings, and manage progress reporting across all engagements simultaneously.

**Data-Driven Decision Making:** I developed Census tract-level analytics that replace misleading county averages with block-by-block evidence of where poverty, health disparities, and crime concentrate. This data directly informs program design, resource allocation, and funder narratives. Our Justice Command Center maps how crime migrates with gentrification — original research backed by Census data from Buffalo NY, Wilmington NC, and Austin TX.

**Workforce Policy Expertise:** I have working familiarity with WIOA Title I (including all 14 required youth program elements, 6 performance indicators, and LWDB procurement processes), Perkins V career and technical education requirements, and federal grant compliance through Grants.gov and JustGrants. This isn't textbook knowledge — it's from writing actual applications and building systems to track the required performance metrics.

**Military Service:** As a U.S. Army veteran, I bring the disciplined execution, team leadership, and mission-focus that this role demands. I also built the Mission Transition (M2C) platform specifically because I understand the military-to-civilian transition challenge firsthand.

### What impact do you hope to make in this role?

Three things:

**1. Bring technology infrastructure to ecosystem-level work.** Most career pathway initiatives operate on spreadsheets and quarterly reports. I've proven that an integrated technology platform — one where workforce, health, education, and employer systems talk to each other — transforms both the speed and quality of outcomes. I want to bring that capability to TNTP's partners nationwide.

**2. Ground every recommendation in tract-level data.** I've seen too many workforce strategies built on county-level averages that hide the communities with the greatest need. I'll bring the data discipline that reveals where career pathways need to be built, which credentials matter in which labor markets, and which populations are being missed by current systems.

**3. Scale what works through implementation science.** The gap between "evidence-based" and "actually working in communities" is enormous. My RPLICE system and implementation science approach ensure that career pathway programs don't just get designed — they get implemented with fidelity, measured for real outcomes, and adapted based on what the data shows. I want to bring that rigor to every TNTP Education to Career engagement.

The communities I serve in Central Texas — the same communities TNTP serves nationally — deserve systems that were designed for them, with them, and accountable to them. That's what I build. That's what I want to build at scale with TNTP.
