30-Second Testimony — Dr. Flood
"We built a platform that tracks every foster youth from risk identification at age 12 through housing, workforce, and health stability in adulthood — in real time, across agencies. We have already reduced risk for 350 young people on this platform. The math is simple: a $3,000 six-month investment prevents a $40,000 annual incarceration cost. 30-Second Testimony — Cortney Jones
"I sit on Texas CASA's Public Policy Committee. I've seen what happens when a young person ages out of the foster care system without a housing bridge. They don't disappear — they show up in our emergency rooms, our jails, and our homeless shelters. That is reactive spending. What TCAF has built is predictive spending — with data that shows it's working. The platform is live. The navigation system is live. What's missing is the policy authorization to fund the 6-month window that makes everything else possible."
Leave-Behind One-Pager — Key Numbers
Foster Youth Housing Vouchers + Navigation: The Case
  • The problem: 25–30% of foster youth face homelessness by 21. System silos mean vouchers go unused and instability persists.
  • The cost of inaction: $40K/year per incarcerated individual. 2,000 youth diverted = $8M annual savings. $250M over 5 years.
  • The intervention: $3,000/youth for 6-month stabilization — housing voucher + navigation + benefits + workforce pathway + real-time case tracking.
  • Platform proof (live): 350 youth with measurable risk reduction. 0% eviction in tracked voucher programs. 100% workforce/education engagement.
