Who We Are — The Partnership
The Collaborative Advocate Foundation (TCAF) and Hargrave Innovative Solutions (HIS) are a veteran-founded, Black-led community infrastructure partnership operating at the intersection of fatherhood support, behavioral health, benefits navigation, and workforce development. Together, we bring a rare combination: TCAF's AI-powered data platform covering 15 service domains across Central Texas and HIS's proven federal contracting experience and coalition-building depth in fatherhood and family stabilization programming. Our combined reach spans Travis, Williamson, Hays, Bastrop, and Caldwell counties — with a model designed to replicate nationally. We don't just identify gaps. We build the infrastructure that closes them, measure the results, and show funders exactly what worked.

Combined Differentiators
The Tool Already Exists
The CTX Benefits Initiative was built before the grant, not as a promise. The 5-county enrollment engine, CHW workflow, efficacy dashboard, and outcome tracking are live — not aspirational. Funders are investing in a proven tool, not a concept.
The Coalition Already Exists
TCAF + HIS bring an active United Way partnership, Williamson County fatherhood coalition, board seat on Williamson County Childcare Infrastructure Partnership, and confirmed relationships with Episcopal Health Foundation funders. Not a cold application.
Two Entities, Full Spectrum
TCAF (501c3) pursues foundation, federal, and state grants. ISS LLC (for-profit) pursues SBIR/STTR and commercial vehicles. HIS (501c3) anchors federal contracting and program management. Together: the full funding spectrum — foundation through commercial.
