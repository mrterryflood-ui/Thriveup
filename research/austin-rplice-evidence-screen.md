## Question

Does the live RPLICE public research library contain direct matches,
setting-limited related sources, or implementation/evaluation process support
for the five components of the candidate bundle?

## Method and boundary

- **Source screened:** live public `GET
  https://www.bettersciencelab.com/api/research`, retrieved August 26, 2026.
- **Library size at retrieval:** 49 catalog records.
- **Review rule:** a source is a *direct component match* only if the title or
  abstract supplied by the library describes the component and a comparable
  setting/population. A merely adjacent setting is recorded as a **related,
  setting-limited source**. A framework is recorded as **process support**, not
  intervention-effect evidence.
- **Important limitation:** This is a screen of one curated library, not a
  reproducible systematic search of all databases. “No direct match” means no
  direct match was identified in this 49-record library; it does **not** mean
  no relevant research exists.
- **Claim rule:** None of these sources establishes effectiveness, feasibility,
  fidelity, cultural legitimacy, youth acceptability, local capacity, or
  applicability in Austin. Those are separate evidence and authorization
  questions.

## Required next evidence work

Before the candidate may be described as an EBI, the evidence packet must add:

1. A documented, reproducible targeted search for youth/resident co-design,
   culturally grounded continuity/place-based action, nonclinical navigation
   and warm handoffs, and community-accountable learning.
2. Study-level applicability reviews that name population, setting, design,
   outcome, limitation, and fit/non-fit with the partner-confirmed Austin
   geography and participation model.
3. A distinction between evidence for a component, evidence for a delivery
   model, and evidence for the candidate bundle as a whole.
4. A CFIR readiness worksheet, RE-AIM measure plan, core-component definition,
   adaptation/fidelity log, and predeclared stop/adjust/scale rules.
5. Community/cultural/youth review and explicit authorization before any
   cultural or youth claim, data collection, recruitment, referral, public
   release, or implementation decision.

Operating a pilot also requires the separate authorization, governance,
privacy, safeguarding, capacity, and public-claims gates in the blueprint and
organization-fit matrix; evidence work alone cannot open those gates.

## Recommendation

**Hold the EBI label.** The current RPLICE library supports implementation
planning and exposes the correct evidence gaps. It does not yet support a
claim that this multicomponent Austin bundle is evidence-based, effective, or
ready to operate.
