# Austin Community Bridge — Organization-Fit & Authority Matrix

**Status:** Private planning record. It does **not** establish a partnership,
data-sharing agreement, funding relationship, delivery obligation, public
endorsement, authority to represent any organization, or authority to conduct
outreach on another organization’s behalf.

**Prepared:** August 26, 2026  
**Related planning artifact:** [Austin Community Bridge Resource & Pilot

## Purpose and decision rule

This record answers a narrower question than “who should be involved?”:

> Based only on organizational records and each organization’s own public
> description, what contribution appears mission-aligned, and what still needs
> explicit authorization before it can become a pilot responsibility?

It does not assign a role from public association, a logo, an event, a
directory listing, or a platform capability. A role becomes operational only
when the relevant organization accepts it in a documented governance record.

### Evidence labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Verified purpose/context** | Direct organizational record or current first-party public statement reviewed on August 26, 2026. |
| **Potential fit** | A bounded contribution that is compatible with the verified purpose/context. It is a recommendation, not an assignment. |
| **Authorization required** | A responsibility that cannot begin without the responsible organization’s explicit approval. |
| **Unknown / not inferred** | No adequate source was reviewed; this record intentionally does not guess. |

## Immediate use in the baseline and EBI evidence packet

The next private packet may use this matrix in three limited ways:

1. **Role-bound evidence preparation.** Prepare cultural, youth, data, and
   claims questions for prospective authorities rather than assigning a
   reviewer or treating every organization as a general-purpose partner.
2. **Method hold.** Do not classify a response gap, select a priority area, or
   expose a map until the geography, source register, review process, and
   decision rights are approved.
3. **Invitation, not extraction.** Any youth or resident participation must
   begin only after the authorization route exists and the relevant authorized
   parties approve the consent, safeguarding, compensation, accessibility, and
   data-sharing model described in the blueprint.

## Recommendation

**Hold operational pilot design; proceed only to private evidence-packet
preparation and role-confirmation preparation.**

The source record supports **considering** a culturally anchored and
youth-inclusive design direction. It does not establish community consent,
cultural legitimacy, youth participation, or operational assignments. The
matrix can prepare questions for any future, separately authorized contact with
prospective participants. No organization should be described as a partner
until it has documented its decision to participate and the relevant
authorization route.
