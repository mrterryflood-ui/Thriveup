## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Austin-Pflugerville corridor faces a severe affordable housing crisis affecting veterans, working families, and underserved communities. With median home prices at $429K–$435K in Austin (only 2 of 75 zip codes affordable to median-income families) and $390K in Pflugerville, the region has a documented gap of **48,000+ units** for households earning below 30% Area Median Income. ThriveUp Academy's 19-platform ecosystem is uniquely positioned to address housing stability through workforce development, health integration, and wraparound services — directly aligned with 5 active grant opportunities totaling $2.1M–$3.4M in potential funding.

## PART 1: AUSTIN HOUSING CRISIS — BY THE NUMBERS

### Market Snapshot (2025–2026)
| Metric | Austin | Pflugerville |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Median Home Price | $429,000–$435,000 | $390,000 |
| Affordable Zip Codes | 2 of 75 | Limited |
| Unit Gap (< 30% AMI) | 48,000+ | Part of regional gap |
| Homelessness (ECHO 2024) | 3,785 PIT count | Regional contributor |
| Cost-Burdened Renters | 46% of renters | ~40% of renters |
| Market Condition | Cooling but unaffordable | Buyer's market (6.7 months supply) |

### Who's Most Affected
- **Veterans**: Austin has 45,000+ veterans; veteran homelessness increased 12% regionally. HUD-VASH vouchers have 18-month waitlists.
- **Black & Hispanic Households**: Homeownership rate 30% (Black) vs. 70% (white) in Austin. Hispanic families face 2.3x cost burden.
- **Working Families**: A family needs $95,000/year to afford median Austin home. Median household income: $80,954.
- **Youth Aging Out of Foster Care**: 250+ annually in Travis County with no housing plan.
- **Individuals with Disabilities**: 14% of Austin's population; accessible affordable units virtually nonexistent.
