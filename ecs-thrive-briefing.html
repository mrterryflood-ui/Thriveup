Foster Youth Referrals — Warm Handoffs That Close the Loop
Submit foster youth referrals through ThriveUp and track every touchpoint to outcome — HMIS-compatible, funder-reportable.

When ECS serves a youth transitioning out of foster care, you can submit a structured referral to ThriveUp's Youth Homelessness System Initiative (YHSI) — and get back a secure intake record, tracked engagement milestones, and outcome data you can export for funder reporting.

  • 1
    Submit the referral — POST the youth's immediate needs, referring caseworker, and state code. Returns an intakeId and a secure intakeUrl — no youth PII exposed in any API response.
  • 2
    Touchpoints tracked automatically — first contact, warm handoff, meeting, and outcome are timestamped without manual logging on your end.
  • 3
    HMIS-compatible export — de-identified, consent-gated CSV export compatible with your HMIS reporting requirements. Floor-5 suppression protects small-cell counts per HUD guidance.
  • 4
    Outcome notifications — when the receiving org confirms housing, employment, or benefit enrollment, you receive a notification. The loop closes automatically without a follow-up phone call.

Chainweb — Prove the Dollar Value of What You Do
Causal modeling that translates ECS's interventions into prevented costs and net savings — in language county commissioners and foundations understand.

Every intervention ECS makes has a downstream causal chain that prevents more expensive outcomes. Chainweb models those chains with peer-reviewed coefficients and returns a counterfactual: what would have happened if ECS hadn't intervened?

ECS InterventionDownstream Cost PreventedChainweb Domain
Emergency rent assistance Prevented eviction → averted emergency shelter stays, child welfare involvement, school instability Housing → Stability
Utility assistance Prevented disconnection → averted hypothermia ER visits, food spoilage, health crisis Health / Poverty
Food assistance Prevented food insecurity → averted pediatric ER visits, cognitive impacts, school absences Food → Health / Education
Foster youth housing support Prevented youth homelessness → averted justice involvement, long-term dependency costs Safety / Housing → Employment
