APPLY FOR THE CHALLENGE
ABOUT THE CHALLENGE
The T.D. Jakes Foundation (TDJF), in partnership with Wells Fargo, is proud to launch the Social
Impact Grant Challenge, a transformative funding opportunity designed to identify, support,
and amplify nonprofit organizations creating meaningful change in communities across the
                                                                                          Register →
United States.

Designed around the T.D. Jakes Foundation's pillars of service (Educational Access & Workforce
Readiness, financial inclusion, and community transformation and well-being), the Challenge
seeks innovative nonprofit organizations that are addressing critical social issues through
impactful, scalable, and sustainable solutions.

Finalists selected through this process will have the opportunity to showcase their organization
through a live presentation at the inaugural Thriving Seven (T7) Summit, taking place November
12th–14th in Atlanta, Georgia.

In addition to competing for grant funding, finalists will gain national visibility for their work and
connect with nonprofit leaders, funders, and changemakers from across the country.

TDJF serves as the sole administrator of the Challenge and is responsible for the evaluation, selection, and final
determination of award recipients. Wells Fargo serves as a strategic funding partner and does not participate in
selection of the award recipients or grant-making decisions.

ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS
Only one application may be submitted per organization. To be eligible, organizations must:

     Provide documentation supporting registered nonprofit organization status in good
     standing under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.

     Be headquartered and operating within the United States, the District of Columbia, or
     Puerto Rico.
     Demonstrate measurable community impact and organizational capacity.
                                                                                   Register →
     Be willing and able to participate in the Social Impact Grant Challenge process.

     Comply with all applicable laws, rules, and regulations, including anti-discrimination laws, as
     an organization.

     Ensure all programs or activities are open to participants regardless of race, gender,
     ethnicity, heritage, or similar characteristics.

     Agree to all Challenge rules, eligibility requirements, and reporting obligations.

There is no purchase, donation, or fee required to enter or participate in the Challenge.
