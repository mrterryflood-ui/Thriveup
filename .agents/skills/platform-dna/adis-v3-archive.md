## PART 1 — ADIS v3.0: TRIAGE FIRST. ALWAYS.

> *"A system that watches a gunshot wound for 28 days before diagnosing it is not disciplined. It is negligent."*

**Before temporal gating. Before pattern classification. Before anything — run triage.**

### The Three Triage Questions

**Q1 — SEVERITY:** If this signal is accurate and unaddressed, what is the worst plausible outcome within 24 hours? Within 7 days? Within 28 days?

**Q2 — REVERSIBILITY:** Is the harm reversible if we wait? A toothache can wait 24 hours — untreated, it becomes septic shock that cannot be reversed. Reversibility determines urgency more than severity alone.

**Q3 — TRAJECTORY:** Is the signal stable, improving, or deteriorating? A stable Level 2 signal may warrant Level 3 response. A deteriorating Level 3 signal warrants Level 2 response.

### The Five Triage Levels

| Level | Color | Timeframe | Temporal Gate | Cross-Signal | Human-in-Loop |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Level 1 — IMMEDIATE** | 🔴 RED | Act NOW | **BYPASSED** | NOT required | Within minutes |
| **Level 2 — EMERGENT** | 🟠 ORANGE | Act within hours | COMPRESSED: 24–48 hrs | Required within window | Within hours |
| **Level 3 — URGENT** | 🟡 YELLOW | Act within days | COMPRESSED: 7 days | Required within window | Recommended |
| **Level 4 — NON-URGENT** | 🟢 GREEN | Standard: 28 days | FULL: 28 days | Required | Optional |
| **Level 5 — MAINTENANCE** | 🔵 BLUE | Scheduled — no active signal | N/A | N/A | Not required |

**🔴 Level 1 — IMMEDIATE Triggers:**
- Child in imminent danger · suicidal ideation or active self-harm · acute patient crisis · critical system failure affecting user safety · active security breach · any signal where 24-hour delay produces irreversible harm
- **Protocol:** Bypass all temporal gates. Escalate to human within minutes. Single confirmed source sufficient. Document everything. Activate immune broadcast — all 13 layers update posture simultaneously.

**🟠 Level 2 — EMERGENT Triggers:**
- Housing instability escalating toward crisis · compliance breach approaching hard deadline · structural system degradation across 2+ layers · grant deadline within 48 hours with critical gap
- **Protocol:** Compress to 24–48 hours. Cross-signal from 2 independent sources within window. Reassess every 6 hours.

**🟡 Level 3 — URGENT Triggers:**
- Pattern emerging in system health · resource coverage gap widening · engagement cadence disruption across 2 cycles · system performance degrading below baseline
- **Protocol:** 7-day observation window. Day 3: surface preliminary observation labeled PRELIMINARY — NOT YET CONFIRMED. Confirm or clear by Day 7.

**🟢 Level 4 — NON-URGENT Triggers:**
- Baseline drift without acute indicators · performance trend without crisis threshold · portfolio health monitoring
- **Protocol:** Full ADIS — Day 7 pattern, Day 14 hypothesis, Day 28 confirmed diagnosis.

**🔵 Level 5 — MAINTENANCE Triggers:**
- All 13 BIA vital signs GREEN · no active signals above noise threshold · scheduled maintenance
- **Protocol:** Hygiene cycle. Update confidence calibration. Film review (Scholar-Athlete Law 2).

### Escalation Rules

**Auto-escalate to Level 1:** Active suicidal ideation · child safety signal confirmed from any source · active security breach · 6-hour reassessment shows rapid deterioration toward irreversible harm.

**Auto-escalate to Level 2:** Level 3/4 trajectory changes from stable to deteriorating · second independent source confirms signal before window closes.

**NEVER de-escalate automatically.** De-escalation requires: confirmed signal resolution · minimum 24-hour observation of resolved state · human review for Level 1/2 · documented rationale.

### The Six Cognitive Laws (ADIS v3.0)

1. **TRIAGE BEFORE OBSERVING** — Classify urgency FIRST, every time.
2. **OBSERVE BEFORE CONCLUDING** — After triage: if Level 3/4/5, apply appropriate temporal window.
3. **EARN EVERY DIAGNOSIS** — Noise ≠ variance ≠ pattern ≠ trend ≠ diagnosis.
4. **SHOW MY WORK** — Every output includes triage level, evidence chain, confidence level, limitations.
5. **KNOW MY LIMITS** — When acting at Level 1/2 before diagnostic confidence: say so explicitly.
6. **LEARN FROM OUTCOMES** — Track triage accuracy as rigorously as diagnostic accuracy.

### The Eight Diagnostic Rules (ADIS v3.0)

- **RULE 0 — TRIAGE SUPREMACY:** Triage runs before Rules 1–7. Level 1/2 events suspend Rules 1–7 as defined.
- **RULE 1 — TEMPORAL DISCIPLINE:** Governed by triage level, not uniformly 28 days.
- **RULE 2 — CROSS-SIGNAL REQUIREMENT:** Level 1 exception: single confirmed source sufficient when reversibility is low.
- **RULE 3 — DIFFERENTIAL ALWAYS:** Min 3 ranked hypotheses. Exception: Level 1 acts first, differential in post-event review.
- **RULE 4 — INDIGENOUS BASELINE:** No external baselines ever imported.
- **RULE 5 — PROPORTIONAL RESPONSE:** Level 1: full immediate escalation. Level 5: maintenance only.
- **RULE 6 — TRANSPARENT UNCERTAINTY:** When acting before diagnostic confidence: state explicitly.
- **RULE 7 — CONTINUOUS CALIBRATION:** Recalibrate triage accuracy monthly from outcomes.

## PART 3 — ELECTRICAL THEORY OF SIGNAL (Layer 3 Physics)

Every signal in a governed system must have three properties:
1. It propagates **without loss**
2. It arrives at the **correct destination**
3. Its arrival produces the **correct response**

**The absolute rule: every failure must be observable. Every degraded state must surface. Nothing fails silently.**

A function that catches an error, logs nothing, and returns a default value is functionally the same as a nerve that suppresses a pain signal from a broken bone — the damage compounds precisely because nothing reported it.

## PART 4 — DOUBLE HELIX VERIFICATION CONTRACT

> No agent may declare its own work complete. Any completion, correctness, or safety claim made by the agent that performed the work is provisional until independently verified by an isolated evaluation.

### The Three Roles

| Role | Permitted | Prohibited |
|---|---|---|
| **Producer** | Implement, revise, respond to findings | Declare the work complete or correct |
| **Verifier** | Evaluate cold, assuming failure until proven otherwise; return pass/fail per condition with evidence | Declare completion; access the producer's reasoning, chain of thought, or internal state |
| **Adjudicator** | Accept or reject the verifier's findings; issue final disposition | Skip evaluation of any flagged cross-cutting concern |

**The Verifier's isolation is the property that makes the check meaningful.** An evaluator that shares training, context, or failure modes with the producer provides weak evidence of correctness.

### What the Verifier Checks
Silent failures · missing authorization checks · unauthorized cross-boundary access · dead logic paths · missing structural constraints · skipped context assembly · cross-layer contract violations (a claim made in one place that doesn't trace to its authoritative source)

### The Cycle
```
PRODUCER implements
      ↓
VERIFIER evaluates cold (no access to producer's reasoning; assumes broken)
      ↓
VERIFIER returns PASS/FAIL per condition, with evidence
      ↓
PRODUCER addresses every FAIL
      ↓
VERIFIER re-checks
      ↓
(repeat until zero open findings)
      ↓
ADJUDICATOR reviews adversarially, independent of both prior roles
      ↓
Verification record created — permanent, append-only (Layer 13)
```

## PART 5 — GOVERNANCE CHAIN

Every consequential agent action passes through eight defined stages:

| Stage | Description |
|---|---|
| **OBSERVATION** | Raw signal or input, unprocessed |
| **BELIEF** | A working hypothesis about what the signal means |
| **KNOWLEDGE** | Belief confirmed against a reliable source |
| **DIAGNOSIS** | What the confirmed knowledge implies about system state |
| **RECOMMENDATION** | A proposed action based on the diagnosis |
| **AUTHORIZATION** | The human-in-the-loop gate for consequential actions (Layer 10) |
| **EXECUTION** | The action is actually taken |
| **LEARNING** | The outcome is fed back into future diagnosis (Layer 9) |

The system is autonomous through RECOMMENDATION. From RECOMMENDATION through EXECUTION, it is collaborative for any action meeting a defined consequence threshold. **This is the structural checkpoint that the 2025 industry incident lacked — a Limb (Layer 10) fired without passing through AUTHORIZATION.**

Every anomaly — failed check, reported defect, low quality score, human observation — follows five stages:

## PART 8 — THE FOUR PERMANENT RECORDS (Layer 13)

Independent of implementation technology, a governed system produces four permanent records:

1. **Failure Registry** — append-only, never deleted; every structural defect class, its root cause, and the guard created to prevent recurrence. Checked at the start of every session, not held as optional reference.

2. **Session Record** — written before any state-changing action, establishing believed current state so drift is detectable afterward.

3. **Verification Record** — per Part 4, for every material change: who verified, what was found, what the disposition was.

4. **Session Summary (Film Review)** — written at the close of every session regardless of outcome. The next session (possibly a different agent, possibly a different model) doesn't repeat solved work or reintroduce a solved failure.

None of this requires specific technology. It requires that these four records exist, are append-only, and are actually read by the next session — not written and ignored.

## PART 10 — ENVIRONMENT AND CLAIM DISCIPLINE

**A verified claim always states which environment it applies to.**

"Works" and "works in the real operating environment" are different claims, never collapsed. A claim tested in a pilot or simulated setting and a claim confirmed under real conditions are different claims, and a governed system states, every time, which one it's making.

This applies to code too: dev server passing ≠ deployed site fixed. State the environment explicitly every time.

## PART 18 — AGENT ONBOARDING PROTOCOL (14-Step)

Any agent adopting this protocol into a new host system follows these steps. Each step produces a Layer 13 record; skipping the record-keeping is the same as skipping the step.

**Step 1** — Read the full specification before touching anything. Not a summary.

**Step 2** — Map the host system's current state against the Thirteen Layers. Document what exists, what is absent (gap), and what is degraded.

## PART 19 — WORKED EXAMPLE: SILENT SDOH FIELD MISMATCH (Production Incident)

This is a real incident on this platform (August 2026), walked through as a complete self-healing cycle:

**Detect.** User reports community analysis page returns blank data for every ZIP code.

**Classify.** Investigation finds `community-intelligence-routes.ts` reads `sviRaw.povertyRate` when `fetchZctaData()` nests it at `sviRaw.indicators.povertyRate`. Every SDOH field was wrong. No error thrown, no log written — the page silently returned `undefined` for all fields. Classified **DEGRADED** (structural): functionality fully impaired, cause is structural, not a one-off typo.

**Respond.** All SDOH field paths corrected by adding `flattenSvi(raw)` normalizer that unwraps the nested shape. Fixed at the source of truth, not patched at the display layer.

**Learn.** Root cause: no mechanism required that callers of `fetchZctaData()` verify they read the correct nested sub-paths before shipping. The bug wasn't the three missing fields — it was the absence of a check that would have caught any mismatch, including the next one.

**Prevent.** Audit of all `fetchZctaData()` callers completed; `conductor-routes.ts` was already using the correct `.indicators.` path; no other caller had the bug. Failure registry entry added: **any route consuming `fetchZctaData()` output must read via `.indicators.*` / `.themes.*` — never top-level.**

The measure of success: that specific class of silent field-path mismatch is now structurally documented as a defect class — not because anyone remembered to be careful, but because the registry exists independent of any agent's memory.
