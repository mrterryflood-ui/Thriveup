---
name: Platform focus and distinction
description: User-reported usability feedback and standing whole-platform direction.
---

The user reports the reaction: “Awesome site. Too overwhelming. Needs focus, separation and distinction before integration.”

The user reiterated: “people don't know where to start and they want to run.”

The user also describes the platform as “a box of knowledge that no one knows how to open.” The intended outcome is practical utilization, not more exposition.

Apply that direction going forward to “the whole system and platform,” not just one page.

The user approved task-first, workspace-first, and optional guided conversation as three alternative entry methods, while retaining the useful information and existing tools.

**Why:** The user stated this feedback and scope.

Technical and navigation tests can pass while a first-time phone visitor sees no task choices in the first viewport, or cannot identify the next action at the destination. Those tests establish implementation behavior, not achievement of the user's ease-of-use goal or sustained utilization.

**How to apply:** Evaluate new work against whether people can identify their purpose, distinguish the relevant workflow, and understand the next step before being exposed to cross-platform integration. Do not interpret this as authorization to begin a redesign during a discussion-only request.
