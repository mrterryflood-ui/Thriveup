---
name: International surface boundaries
description: Which current domestic surfaces must not be presented as international infrastructure.
---

Community Voice, Whole-Person Health, and LifeBridge remain domestic implementation surfaces unless a country-specific adapter has a local owner, service contract, safeguarding/legal review, and human escalation agreement.

**Why:** Domestic service routing, crisis handling, language support, and implementation methods do not automatically transfer across countries; international claims without local contracts create safety and truthfulness risk.

**How to apply:** Use RPLICE and live research providers for discovery, but require primary-source provenance and local adaptation before expanding evidence, voice, referrals, or crisis workflows internationally.
