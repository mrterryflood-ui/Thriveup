description: Why the numeric receipt remains secondary to binding grounding and why foreign benchmark scores are not ThriveUp calibration.

Keep the advisory receipt subordinate to binding grounding and describe only the delivered answer. A failed or unavailable receipt is a disclosure, not authorization to strip content or bypass existing redaction.

**Why:** The user supplied a portable literal numeric/forecast-label screen, not a general truth verifier. Its external HazardAware benchmark scores were real measurements in a different domain; transplanting them would misrepresent ThriveUp capability.

**How to apply:** Preserve provider string/stream compatibility, label caller-supplied facts separately from assembled evidence, and leave unknown scores/prices unknown. Calibrate against approved source-pinned domain evidence, attribute complete cases to the model that actually served them, and disclose the evaluation's limited domain and case count. Policy snapshots and illustrative projections must never be relabeled as observed participant outcomes.

Source coverage, missing values, provenance, and permission-filtered handoffs must be delivered as source-owned structured evidence, independently of generated interpretation.

**Why:** A real government-data journey passed lookup and draft checks but its generated interpretation changed coverage, omitted a missing nutrition measure, and provided no usable handoff. Testing the draft did not prove interpretation or delivery.

**How to apply:** Verify an actual delivered answer, preserve the validated source receipt across streaming and authorized history replay, bind closed-form counts before delivery, and keep exact estimates in source-owned UI. Supply genuinely assembled evidence to advisory receipts, without relabeling general prose or predicted benefits as verified. A public tool opening proves navigation, not the availability of every tool feature or successful downstream analysis.
