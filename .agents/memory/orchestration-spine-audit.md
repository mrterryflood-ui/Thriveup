---
name: Orchestration spine audit
description: The platform has real evidence, resource, action, and reporting organs, but lacks one durable context-preserving handoff across them.
---

The platform should coordinate existing specialist organs through a small,
reviewable handoff envelope rather than a new super-engine. The envelope must
carry the issue, place/time, evidence references and freshness, claim type,
resource verification state, human-selected action, accountable owner,
follow-up, outcome eligibility, consent boundary, and unresolved gaps.

**Why:** Current Community Impact, Time–Place–Need, Conductor/Chainweb/RAG,
Partner Portal, and Community Events surfaces are individually real but use
separate entry points and payloads. Without a shared handoff, breadth looks
like coordination and modeled or unverified information can lose its
disclosure during a transition.

**How to apply:** Treat `issue → place/time → evidence → options → human
action → follow-up → outcomes → report` as the product spine. Recommendations
must be transparent and user-selected; never turn this into automatic
matching, surveillance, identity collection, or autonomous consequential
action. Preserve the PII wall, consent defaults, small-cell suppression, and
observed-vs-modeled labels at every handoff.
