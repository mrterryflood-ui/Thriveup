## Overview

This document identifies open and upcoming federal health grant opportunities from CDC, NIH, SAMHSA, HRSA, and ARPA-H that align with TCAF's 24-platform AI-powered workforce development and community health ecosystem. Opportunities are organized by complexity and fit, with priority given to grants that do not require complex federal application packages.

---

## TIER 1: Strong Fit, Low-to-Medium Complexity

### 1. ARPA-H: Proactive Health ISO (Rolling — Always Open)
- **Solicitation:** ARPA-H-SOL-24-106
- **Funder:** Advanced Research Projects Agency for Health
- **Amount:** Varies (typically $500K–$5M+)
- **Deadline:** Rolling — no deadline, accepts submissions continuously
- **Entry Barrier:** White paper submission (not a full federal application)
- **TCAF Fit:** The Proactive Health mission office funds projects that keep people from becoming patients. TCAF's Community Health Worker (CHW) training pipeline, health equity data analytics, and preventive care platforms (WholeMind Learning, Autoimmune Thrive) directly address this mission.
- **Apply:** arpa-h.gov → Explore Funding → Mission Office ISOs
- **Key Advantage:** Uses Other Transactions (OTA), not traditional grants. No SF-424, no JustGrants. Nonprofits explicitly eligible.

### 2. ARPA-H: Scalable Solutions ISO (Rolling — Always Open)
- **Solicitation:** ARPA-H-SOL-24-105
- **Funder:** Advanced Research Projects Agency for Health
- **Amount:** Varies
- **Deadline:** Rolling
- **Entry Barrier:** White paper submission
- **TCAF Fit:** The Scalable Solutions office seeks to address technical barriers that prevent health interventions from reaching as many people as possible — including last-mile delivery, health equity across geography, and reaching underserved populations. TCAF's ecosystem model (24 platforms serving 5 counties, Census tract-level targeting) is exactly this.
- **Apply:** arpa-h.gov → Explore Funding → Mission Office ISOs

### 3. ARPA-H: Resilient Systems ISO (Rolling — Always Open)
- **Solicitation:** ARPA-H-SOL-24-103
- **Funder:** Advanced Research Projects Agency for Health
- **Amount:** Varies
- **Deadline:** Rolling
- **Entry Barrier:** White paper submission
- **TCAF Fit:** The Resilient Systems office funds digital health platforms, interoperability solutions, and approaches that mitigate disruptions to healthcare access. TCAF's integrated ecosystem — connecting workforce, health, education, and justice platforms — is a resilient system by design.
- **Apply:** arpa-h.gov → Explore Funding → Mission Office ISOs

### 4. Drug-Free Communities (DFC) Support Program
- **Funder:** SAMHSA / ONDCP
- **Amount:** Up to $125,000/year for 5 years ($625K total)
- **Deadline:** April 14, 2026
- **Complexity:** Medium — coalition-based application
- **TCAF Fit:** Community-based prevention, youth substance use prevention. TCAF's youth empowerment platforms (ThriveUp Academy career pathways, WholeMind Learning) and coalition partnerships qualify. Requires demonstrating an active community coalition with 12 required sectors represented.
- **Apply:** Grants.gov

### 5. CDC Community Health Workers (CHW) Program Grant
- **Funder:** Centers for Disease Control and Prevention
- **Amount:** Varies
- **Deadline:** Check Grants.gov for current NOFO
- **Complexity:** Medium
- **TCAF Fit:** TCAF built a CHW training pipeline addressing healthcare access gaps in underserved Central Texas neighborhoods. This is a direct hit. Eligible as a nonprofit in partnership with a public health agency (partner with Travis County Health or Austin Public Health).
- **Nonprofit Note:** Requires partnership with a public health agency — identify your local health department contact.
- **Apply:** Grants.gov

### 6. SAMHSA Prevention Programs of Regional/National Significance
- **Funder:** Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration
- **Amount:** Varies by program
- **Deadline:** Check Grants.gov / samhsa.gov/grants
- **Complexity:** Medium
- **TCAF Fit:** Congress directed SAMHSA to expand eligibility to private nonprofits AND faith-based organizations. Community prevention focus aligns with TCAF's health equity and youth development work.
- **Apply:** Grants.gov

## TIER 3: OTA / Non-Traditional Paths

### 10. CDC Unsolicited Proposals
- **Funder:** CDC
- **Amount:** Negotiated
- **Deadline:** None — accepts unsolicited proposals at any time
- **How:** Email OFRPolicy@cdc.gov with a pitch for TCAF's community health infrastructure ecosystem. CDC accepts unsolicited proposals per FAR Subpart 15.6 as a means to obtain innovative and unique methods for accomplishing the agency's mission.
- **TCAF Angle:** Pitch the 24-platform ecosystem as a novel public health infrastructure model — integrating workforce development, CHW training, health equity data, and community engagement in a single technology system.

### 11. NIH Other Transactions (OTA)
- **Funder:** National Institutes of Health
- **Amount:** Varies by Research Opportunity Announcement (ROA)
- **Deadline:** Varies — check active ROAs
- **Eligibility:** Nonprofits with an interest in program goals are explicitly eligible.
- **How to Find:** Search grants.nih.gov/funding/searchguide filtered by "Other Transactions" funding mechanism. Submit applications through eRA ASSIST.
- **TCAF Angle:** Health equity research, community health interventions, implementation science (RPLICE framework using CFIR 2.0 and RE-AIM).

- **TCAF Fit:** Supports workforce diversity pipeline. Would require partnership with an institution holding an active NIH grant.
