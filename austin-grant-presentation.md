## THE CRISIS: AUSTIN BY THE NUMBERS

### Housing
- **$435,000** median home price — only **2 of 75 zip codes** affordable to median-income families
- **48,000+ unit gap** for households below 30% Area Median Income
- **85%+** of extremely low-income renters pay more than half their income on rent
- Ranked **#3 nationally** for worst home value declines — market instability hurts everyone
- Austin added **120,000 housing units** (2015–2024) but still can't keep up with demand for affordable housing
- Municipal development fees increased **$15,000–$25,000** per home since 2020

### Homelessness
- **3,238 people** counted in 2025 Point-in-Time (up **36%** from 2023)
- Estimated **6,235–6,358** experiencing homelessness on any given day
- **68%** became homeless IN Austin — these are our neighbors, not transplants
- **44%** experiencing homelessness for the first time
- **13%** are military veterans
- Youth homelessness **nearly quadrupled**: 247 (2020) → **934** (2024)
- **911 homeless individuals** in Travis County Jail — 36% of jail population
- City budget: $30.3M — needs to **double to $60M+** to address the crisis
- 10-year plan requires **$350M investment**

### Workforce
- A family needs **$95,000/year** to afford Austin's median home. Median household income: **$80,954**
- Without credentials beyond high school, Central Texans have only a **12% chance** of earning a living wage
- Healthcare workforce shortage: thousands of unfilled positions across Central Texas
- **I-35 corridor**: $10B+ in construction creating tens of thousands of jobs — but workers need training AND housing

### Health Equity
- **Black homeownership**: 30% vs. 70% white — a 40-point gap
- Hispanic families face **2.3x cost burden** compared to white families
- **46% of renters** are cost-burdened across the metro
- Housing instability is the **#1 social determinant of health**

### Why ThriveUp Is Built for Austin
