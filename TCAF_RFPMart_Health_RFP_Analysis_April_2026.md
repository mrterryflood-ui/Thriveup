## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**200+ RFPs reviewed. 18 opportunities identified as actionable. 5 are high-priority pursuits.**

Most of this list consists of direct clinical services (physical therapy, psychiatric staffing, dental, pharmacy management, insurance brokering) that require licensed clinical providers, medical facilities, or insurance industry credentials. Those are **not** TCAF's lane and have been filtered out.

What remains are the opportunities that align with TCAF's actual strengths:
- Community Health Worker (CHW) programs & health promotion
- AI-powered workforce development & training
- Behavioral health support (non-clinical, community-based)
- Youth mental health education & prevention
- Reentry/diversion community-based services
- Veteran family support services
- Health equity & culturally responsive programming
- Substance abuse recovery navigation (peer support, not clinical treatment)

- **Why This Is #1:** Home city. TCAF operates in Austin/Pflugerville. Local government wellness contracts strongly favor local nonprofits with established community presence. TCAF's 24-platform ecosystem, CHW dashboard, health & wellness modules, and prevention programming are a direct match. Zero geographic barrier. Maximum competitive advantage.
- **Action:** Pull the full RFP from RFPMart immediately. This is a 9-day window.
- **TCAF Platforms That Apply:** Health & Wellness Hub, CHW Dashboard, Prevention Platform, AI Navigator
- **Budget Estimate:** Unknown — wellness program contracts typically $50K–$300K annually

### 2. HEALTH-9411 — Minnesota — Evidence-Based, Culturally Responsive and Holistic Health Services
- **Deadline:** April 10, 2026
- **Why This Fits:** The language "culturally responsive and holistic" is TCAF's exact positioning. Black-led, veteran-founded organization delivering AI-powered community health with cultural competency baked in. This is the kind of RFP where TCAF's identity IS the competitive advantage.
- **Action:** Pull full RFP. Fast turnaround needed (9 days).
- **TCAF Platforms That Apply:** All 24 — this is a holistic services contract
- **Risk:** Minnesota location requires either remote delivery model or local partnership

### 3. HEALTH-9714 — Tacoma, WA — Veteran and Veteran Families Outpatient Behavioural Health Services
- **Deadline:** May 1, 2026
- **Why This Fits:** Veteran-founded organization serving veteran families. Dr. Flood's military background is a direct differentiator. TCAF's ecosystem includes veteran-specific programming. "Outpatient behavioral health" could include peer support, navigation, and community-based services alongside clinical partners.
- **Action:** Pull full RFP. 30-day window allows thorough proposal. May need to partner with a licensed behavioral health provider for clinical components.
- **TCAF Platforms That Apply:** CHW Dashboard, Health & Wellness, Prevention, AI Navigator, Peer Review
- **Budget Estimate:** Federal/VA contracts typically $100K–$500K

### 4. MRB-53264 — New York — Health Equity Promoting Services
- **Deadline:** May 7, 2026
- **Why This Fits:** Health equity is foundational to TCAF's mission. Black-led organization with AI-powered health platforms, community health workers, and 24-platform ecosystem designed to address health disparities. The proposal practically writes itself from TCAF's existing mission statement.
- **Action:** Pull full RFP. 36-day runway is comfortable for a strong proposal.
- **TCAF Platforms That Apply:** Full ecosystem — health equity is the through-line of all 24 platforms

### 5. HEALTH-9593 — New York — Community Based Diversion Programs Services
