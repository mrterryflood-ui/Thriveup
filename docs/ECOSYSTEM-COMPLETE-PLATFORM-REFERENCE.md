## 4. HERHEALTH SCREENING TOOLS & ASSESSMENTS {#herhealth-screening-tools}

### Interactive Assessment Tools (Built Into Platform)
1. **Nia AI Navigator** — Health navigator trained on minority women's health data and SDOH barriers; powered by Perplexity AI; provides culturally grounded guidance conversations
2. **5 Guided Decision Wizards:**
   - Mammography Decision Wizard
   - Genetic Testing Decision Wizard
   - Clinical Trial Finder Wizard
   - Treatment Navigation Wizard
   - Support Group Finder Wizard
3. **Behavioral Health Screening** — Assesses emotional well-being, sleep quality, social connection (8 questions, scored rubric)
4. **Stress & Coping Evaluation** — Measures perceived control, coping effectiveness, support systems (7 questions, scored rubric)
5. **Holistic Wellness Check** — Covers physical, emotional, social, spiritual, financial, cultural dimensions (9 questions, scored rubric)
6. **Appointment Preparation Center** — Condition-specific question lists, symptom trackers, supplement/medication disclosure tools
7. **Risk Level Algorithms** — Automated scoring generates risk levels (low/moderate/elevated/high) with tailored recommendations

### What These Are NOT:
- These are **navigation and wellness tools**, not clinical diagnostic instruments
- Clinical instruments (PHQ-9, GAD-7, C-SSRS, PCL-5) live in **Whole-Person Health**, not HerHealth
- The separation is intentional — HerHealth navigates, Whole-Person Health screens clinically
