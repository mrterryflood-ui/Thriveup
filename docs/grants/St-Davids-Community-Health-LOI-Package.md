## 1. STRATEGIC POSITIONING

### Why TCAF for Community Health

St. David's Foundation funds community health improvement, behavioral health, maternal health, and health equity initiatives across the Austin/Central Texas region. TCAF brings something no other applicant can: a **health equity technology ecosystem** — 7 purpose-built health platforms operating under the Sankofa Health Network, connected to 17 other community platforms that address every social determinant of health simultaneously.

### The Sankofa Health Network Cluster

| Platform | Focus | Live URL |
|---|---|---|
| **Sankofa Health** | Black maternal health, culturally responsive care | yourhealthbirthright.net |
| **Holistic Black Feminine Health Hub** | Feminine health, reproductive wellness | holisticfemininehealth.net |
| **Black Men's Health Hub** | Men's health, chronic disease, mental health | blackmenshealthhub.net |
| **Autoimmune Thrive** | Autoimmune conditions, chronic disease management | autoimmunethrive.net |
| **PillScheduler** | Medication adherence, reminder systems | pillscheduler.net |
| **SafeCogniCare** | Cognitive health monitoring, elder safety | safecognicare.net |
| **LifeBridge** | Benefits navigation, SDOH data, community voice | lifebridge.community |

### What Makes This Different

1. **Connected, not siloed:** When SafeCogniCare identifies an elder with cognitive decline, PillScheduler ensures medication adherence, LifeBridge screens for benefits eligibility, and the Emergency Management platform flags caregiver burnout risk — all through one integrated system.

2. **Data-powered targeting:** CDC PLACES tract-level health data + Social Vulnerability Index + Census ACS data identifies exactly which neighborhoods face which health disparities. Not county averages — census tract precision.

3. **Culturally responsive by design:** Sankofa platforms were built by and for Black, Latino, and immigrant communities. The technology reflects how these communities actually seek and receive health information — through trusted voices, mobile-first access, and culturally relevant content.

4. **Implementation science rigor:** RPLICE (built on CFIR 2.0 and RE-AIM) monitors whether programs are being delivered as intended and whether outcomes are equitable across populations. MAP-GAP drives 30-day improvement cycles.

## 2. TARGET POPULATIONS & HEALTH EQUITY DATA

### Primary Target: Historically Marginalized Communities in Central Texas

**Black Maternal Health:**
- Black women in Texas are 2.3x more likely to die from pregnancy-related causes than white women
- Travis and Williamson counties have growing Black populations with inadequate culturally responsive maternal care
- TCAF's Sankofa Health and Black Maternal Health platforms directly address this disparity

**Black Men's Health:**
- Black men in Central Texas have disproportionately high rates of hypertension, diabetes, and cardiovascular disease
- Mental health stigma in Black male communities creates invisible crisis
- Black Men's Health Hub provides culturally affirming health education and navigation

**Chronic Disease in Communities of Color:**
- Autoimmune conditions are underdiagnosed in Black and Latino populations
- Medication non-adherence rates are higher in communities with language barriers, transportation barriers, and pharmacy deserts
- PillScheduler + Autoimmune Thrive address these gaps through technology

**Cognitive Health & Elder Safety:**
- Aging populations in Pflugerville/Round Rock/Manor face growing cognitive health needs
- SafeCogniCare provides monitoring that doesn't require expensive clinical infrastructure
- Connects to caregiver support through the broader TCAF ecosystem

### Geographic Focus

- **Williamson County:** Pflugerville (78660), Hutto (78634), Round Rock (78681) — fastest-growing county, health infrastructure hasn't kept pace
- **Travis County:** Eastern Travis County (Del Valle, Colony Park, Montopolis) — highest concentration of health disparities
- **Bastrop County:** Rural health access gaps, limited behavioral health infrastructure

## 3. DRAFT LOI (~500 Words)

**The Collaborative Advocate Foundation — Community Health Grants LOI**

In eastern Williamson County, a Black mother managing gestational diabetes navigates her pregnancy without a single culturally responsive maternal health resource within 15 miles. In Del Valle, a 72-year-old veteran's cognitive decline goes unmonitored because he doesn't qualify for expensive clinical programs. In Bastrop, a Latino father with hypertension skips medications because he can't afford both his prescriptions and his family's groceries — and no one has told him he's eligible for prescription assistance programs.

These aren't hypothetical families. They represent the health equity gap that persists across Central Texas — not because services don't exist, but because the systems connecting people to those services weren't designed for the people who need them most.

The Collaborative Advocate Foundation (TCAF), a 501(c)(3) headquartered in Pflugerville, proposes to close this gap through an integrated health equity technology infrastructure — the Sankofa Health Network — connecting 7 purpose-built health platforms to the communities St. David's Foundation serves.

**How it works:** TCAF's platform uses CDC PLACES tract-level health data and Social Vulnerability Index scoring to identify exactly which neighborhoods face which health disparities — not county averages, but census tract precision. When a community health worker encounters a family, the platform simultaneously screens for maternal health needs, chronic disease management, medication adherence support, cognitive health monitoring, benefits eligibility, and behavioral health — in a single interaction, through a single system.

**Culturally responsive:** The Sankofa Health Network was built by and for Black, Latino, and immigrant communities. Every platform delivers health information through culturally affirming content, multilingual access via Speech Bridge, and mobile-first design that meets families where they already are — on their phones, not in clinics.

**Connected, not fragmented:** When PillScheduler identifies medication non-adherence, LifeBridge screens for benefits that could reduce prescription costs. When SafeCogniCare flags cognitive decline, the caregiver receives resources through the same ecosystem. When Sankofa Health supports a pregnant mother, WholeMind Learning simultaneously supports her school-age children. Health doesn't happen in isolation — and neither does TCAF's model.

**Measurable:** Implementation fidelity is monitored through RPLICE (CFIR 2.0 + RE-AIM frameworks). Health outcomes are tracked at the individual and census-tract level. MAP-GAP drives 30-day improvement cycles — the model gets better every month, not every year. We measure what matters: screenings completed, conditions identified, referrals closed (not just made), medications adhered to, benefits enrolled, and disparities narrowed.

**Sustainable:** TCAF's technology infrastructure is permanent. The health data gets richer each year. Community health workers gain better tools with each cycle. Partner organizations gain capacity they keep after the grant period. This isn't a program that disappears — it's infrastructure that compounds.

TCAF is ready to participate in St. David's office hours, provide platform demonstrations, and begin building health equity infrastructure where Central Texas needs it most.

## 4. PARTNER STRATEGY

### Partnership Logic

| Partner Type | Target Organizations | Role | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| FQHC / Primary Care | CommUnityCare, Lone Star Circle of Care | Clinical referral pathway, patient screening | Already serving target populations; TCAF adds SDOH screening layer |
| Maternal Health | Mama Sana Vibrant Woman, Austin Area Birthing Center | Black maternal health navigation | Trusted voices in maternal health; TCAF provides data + technology |
| Behavioral Health | Integral Care, NAMI Central Texas | Mental health screening and referral | Behavioral health is a core St. David's priority |
| CHW Network | Williamson County CHW programs | In-community health navigation | Trusted community presence; TCAF provides the platform they work through |
| Schools / Family Health | Pflugerville ISD (SHAC connection), Manor ISD | Family health screening through school systems | Universal touchpoint; school health data maps to community health needs |
| Elder Services | AGE of Central Texas, Meals on Wheels | Cognitive health + medication adherence | Aging population needs technology-assisted monitoring |

### TCAF's Role in Every Partnership

TCAF is the **technology backbone** — not a direct service provider competing with existing organizations. TCAF provides:
- CDC PLACES + SVI data intelligence (tract-level targeting)
- Multi-platform health screening (one interaction, all needs)
- Implementation fidelity monitoring (RPLICE)
- Continuous quality improvement (MAP-GAP)
- Multilingual access (Speech Bridge)
- Benefits navigation (LifeBridge)

Partners provide:
- Trusted community presence
- Clinical expertise
- Cultural authority
- In-person service delivery
