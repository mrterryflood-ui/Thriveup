**12. Agency Description**

TCAF d/b/a ThriveUp Academy is a Texas-based 501(c)(3) (IRS determined 01/14/2026) operating a production platform of 15 service surfaces — built and run in-house — that close the gap between research-validated programming and community delivery. Our Travis County implementation is the live template: ThriveUp Academy delivers AI literacy, financial literacy, FAFSA, STEM and workforce learning, including 90 Trade Sims lessons across six trades (electrical, plumbing, HVAC, welding, automotive, software engineering); LifeBridge navigates SNAP, WIC, housing, transit, utility, and Medicaid-waiver benefits; Whole-Person Health and SafeReport deliver clinical-grade behavioral screening (PHQ-9, GAD-7, C-SSRS, PCL-5, ACEs) with HITL clinician review and zero PHI egress; Perfectly Different supports families navigating IEP/504 and neurodiversity-affirming pathways; Talk Your Talk provides 89 spoken plus 18 signed language coverage including dialect-aware delivery for AAVE, Spanglish, and Arabic RTL. The platform sits on 271 production data tables, 211 frontend pages, and 84 backend modules instrumented against the 39 CFIR implementation-science constructs. We do not sell software; we run service.
