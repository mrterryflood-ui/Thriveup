## Section 2 — Executive Summary

### 2.1 The three-sentence thesis

Sedgwick County is already spending approximately $5.15M per year on unmanaged GLP-1 medications for 373 employees and dependents, with a ~47% twelve-month non-continuation rate (consistent with published unmanaged-GLP-1 patterns of 30–50%) that indicates prior authorization alone is not yielding sustained outcomes. Vitality's integrated clinical-plus-coaching managed-care protocol is structured to capture a **modeled Year-1 opportunity of approximately $2.09M** of that spend — nearly funding the entire program — while delivering a sustainable lifestyle change pathway for the broader ~1,525 at-risk adult population. We build with, not against, UnitedHealthcare, OptumRx, and Rally; we displace no existing vendor; and we are the only respondent purpose-built for the four-partner program design model the County asked for in Addendum #2.

### 2.2 What Vitality is

Vitality is a comprehensive, virtual-only weight management program designed for self-insured public-sector employers. The program combines:

- Three evidence-based clinical pathways (Lifestyle, GLP-1-Managed, and Surgical-Coordinated), each anchored in published evidence (NIH/DPP, STEP Trials, LOOK AHEAD, RAND).
- A 5-phase member journey (onboarding, baseline, intensive intervention, maintenance, graduation) overseen by Dr. Chela Love, DNP and her clinical team at Love Clinic MedSpa.
- A behavioral coaching engine led by Dr. J. Michelle Vann, with weekly check-ins, lifestyle accountability, and family/youth program extensions.
- A digital Progressive Web App (no app-store install required) built and operated by Dr. Terry D. Flood's team at The COLLABORATIVE Advocate Foundation, with FHIR/HL7 integration, persistent-memory AI coaching, and HIPAA-grade security.
- Prime contractor administration by Hargrave Innovative Solutions, providing government contract management, compliance oversight, reporting coordination, and single-point accountability.

### 2.3 The three KPIs the County named (Addendum #2), in order

1. **Continued member engagement** — VITA AI persistent-memory advisor + Dr. Vann's weekly behavioral cadence.
2. **Proper use of GLP-1 medications** — Dr. Love's structured titration protocol, OptumRx fill-history reconciliation, side-effect management.
3. **Cost savings** — modeled Year-1 economic opportunity of approximately $3.05M (the $2.09M GLP-1 capture opportunity plus ~$675K medical and ~$282K productivity opportunity) against a $1.71M Year-1 program cost. Realization is contingent on member uptake; we hedge accordingly.

## Section 5 — Program Design: Vitality Comprehensive Weight Management

### 5.1 Program philosophy

Member-centric. Evidence-based (DPP, STEP, LOOK AHEAD, RAND, NIH). Integration-first. Support-focused. Outcomes over activity.

### 5.2 Member journey — the 5-phase clinical protocol

| Phase | Duration | Owner | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Onboarding | 1–2 weeks | Vitality coaching | Identity verified, eligibility confirmed, consent collected, baseline survey |
| 2. Clinical baseline | 2–4 weeks | Dr. Love | Medical baseline, BMI, A1C, BP, comorbidity inventory, pathway assignment |
| 3. Intensive intervention | 12–26 weeks | Dr. Love + Dr. Vann | Pathway-specific intervention; weekly coaching; biweekly clinical |
| 4. Maintenance | 26–52 weeks | Dr. Vann | Reduced cadence; habit consolidation; outcome stabilization |
| 5. Graduation | Ongoing | Self-directed | Quarterly touchpoints; data continues to feed reporting |

### 5.3 Three clinical pathways

- **Pathway A — Lifestyle Intervention** (estimated 72%): NIH/DPP evidence base; structured nutrition coaching, activity tracking, behavioral groups led by Dr. Vann; quarterly Dr. Love review.
- **Pathway B — GLP-1 Managed Care** (estimated 20%): STEP-1/2/3 evidence base (15–17% body-weight reduction at 68 weeks); structured titration, side-effect management, monthly clinical visits, weekly coaching, OptumRx fill reconciliation. Member can elect in-program prescribing (Dr. Love) OR coaching-only support with member's existing prescriber, per Addendum #2.
- **Pathway C — Surgical-Coordinated** (estimated 8%): pre/post-surgical clinical evaluation, coordination with member's chosen surgical center, long-term behavioral coaching.

### 5.4 Behavioral engagement model (Dr. Vann)

Weekly PWA check-ins during Phase 3 with 24-hour coaching response; biweekly virtual cohort groups (8–12 members); family/youth extensions; automated 14-day escalation to human coach; engagement scoring surfaced in County dashboard.

### 5.5 VITA AI persistent-memory advisor

VITA is differentiated by three properties: (1) **persistent memory** — every member conversation stored, context resumes on every session; (2) **live clinical context** — member's current weight, BMI, GLP-1 status, care plan phase, and recent trend on every message; (3) **evidence-cited responses** — clinical answers cite specific studies (DPP, STEP, LOOK AHEAD, RAND). Two modes: warm Dr. Love–voiced member mode and analytical employer mode for the County dashboard.

### 5.6 The GLP-1 managed-care protocol — the headline differentiator

Six components: (1) initial clinical evaluation by Dr. Love for every Pathway B member; (2) structured titration over 12–16 weeks with proactive day-3 / day-7 / day-14 / weekly contact for side-effect management — the single most important intervention against the non-continuation pattern; (3) OptumRx fill-history reconciliation with 48-hour coaching outreach on missed fills; (4) behavioral coaching from Dr. Vann's team throughout; (5) coordination with member's existing prescriber if the member chooses; (6) monthly clinical visits during titration, quarterly during maintenance.

The protocol directly targets the ~47% non-continuation pattern. If Vitality moves twelve-month non-continuation from ~47% to a target zone of ~25% — a conservative target relative to managed-GLP-1 published outcomes — the County retains an additional ~155 members on effective therapy in Year 1, with corresponding compounding outcomes. We deliberately model this as a **target zone, not a guarantee**; the at-risk pricing structure in §9.5 puts our fee at risk against this threshold.

### 9.5 At-risk performance kicker (OUTSIDE the base scored price)

**Important structural note for Criterion V evaluation:** The at-risk performance fee structure below is offered **in addition to** the base PEPM in §9.1, not as a substitute for it. The County's Criterion V cost evaluation should be performed against the base PEPM. The at-risk kicker is a separate value-based pricing instrument that converts a portion of our potential upside into County-aligned risk.

**Structure:**
- **15% of PEPM placed at risk**, tied to three County-named KPIs.
- **Three threshold tests at month 12:**
  1. **Engagement:** ≥70% of enrolled members active in last 30 days at month 12.
  2. **GLP-1 proper use:** ≥30% of the 373-cohort retained on Vitality-managed GLP-1 therapy at month 12 (vs. the ~53% currently still on therapy at month 12 unmanaged; our managed target zone is materially better than the unmanaged trajectory).
  3. **Cost savings:** measured year-over-year medical+pharmacy trend for enrolled cohort vs. matched no-program cohort, target ≥ break-even on program cost.
- **Withhold mechanism:** 15% of PEPM withheld monthly; released annually upon threshold attainment.
- **Outperformance bonus:** an additional **5% PEPM** released if ≥50% of the 373-cohort retained on managed GLP-1 therapy at month 12.
- **Failure-to-perform termination:** if all three thresholds missed in any contract year, County may terminate for non-performance on 30 days' notice (in addition to the standard 30-day termination-for-convenience right under §Contract Period). This is the converse risk to our upside.

**The 30% retention threshold is deliberately conservative.** Published managed-GLP-1 retention sits in the 55–70% range at twelve months. By committing publicly to 30%, the Vitality team is taking on a threshold materially below what the clinical literature supports for an actively managed protocol — a posture intended to communicate operator confidence in the published evidence base. The team is open to negotiating either the withhold percentage or the threshold during contract finalization at the County's preference.

**Why this structure is the right answer to a competitor underbidding us on Criterion V:** A pure-tech vendor priced at $50–$250 PEPM cannot offer this kicker because their delivery model does not include the clinical and behavioral wraparound that drives the KPIs. The kicker is the value-based answer to a price-based comparison.
