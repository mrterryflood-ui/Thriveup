4.2 The GLP-1 utilization picture

The single most operationally significant disclosure in Addendum #2 is the GLP-1 utilization data. We interpret it as follows:

(a) Current GLP-1 spend is substantial and unmanaged. At the conservative RAND 2023 figure of $13,800 per unmanaged GLP-1 patient per year, the 373 current GLP-1 members represent approximately $5.15 million in annual pharmacy cost to the County's medical plan. This spend is occurring with no clinical oversight beyond standard PBM prior authorization.

(b) The 47% twelve-month attrition rate is the diagnostic signal. 705 members started a GLP-1 in the past twelve months, but only 373 are on therapy today. The 332-member differential — approximately 47% of starters — represents members who paid in (through pharmacy claims) but did not achieve sustained outcomes. This attrition pattern is consistent with published GLP-1 real-world adherence data showing 40–55% discontinuation within twelve months in unmanaged settings, driven by side effects (nausea, fatigue), lack of titration support, and absence of behavioral coaching.

(c) The savings opportunity is concrete and addressable. RAND's 2023 conservative figure for the cost differential between unmanaged and managed GLP-1 therapy is $5,600 per patient per year ($13,800 − $8,200). Applied to the 373 current GLP-1 members, the managed-protocol savings opportunity is approximately $2.09 million per year. Migrating the cohort to a managed protocol does not require a single new prescription or any change to OptumRx's formulary — it requires only that the existing 373 members receive structured clinical oversight, titration support, side-effect management, and behavioral coaching.

Vitality is built on five principles:

Member-centric, not vendor-centric. Every design decision starts from the member's job-to-be-done.

Integration-first. We coexist with the County's existing UHC, OptumRx, and Rally relationships. We do not replace them.

Support-focused. We meet the County's stated preference: coaching and clinical wraparound, not compliance enforcement.

Outcomes over activity. Every dashboard leads with the three KPIs the County named.

5.3 Three clinical pathways

Members are assigned to one of three pathways during Phase 2 (Clinical baseline), based on Dr. Love's clinical assessment, member preference, and clinical guideline match.

Components: structured nutrition coaching, activity tracking, behavioral group sessions led by Dr. Vann, weekly check-ins

Clinical cadence: quarterly Dr. Love review

Cost component: lifestyle baseline ($3,200/member/year all-in)

Pathway B — GLP-1 Managed Care (estimated 20% of enrolled members)

Evidence base: STEP-1, STEP-2, STEP-3 trials (15–17% body weight reduction at 68 weeks)

Components: GLP-1 prescription, structured titration, side-effect management, monthly clinical visits, weekly coaching, OptumRx fill-history reconciliation

Clinical cadence: monthly Dr. Love visit during titration; quarterly during maintenance

Member can elect: in-program prescribing (Dr. Love) OR coaching-only support with member's existing prescriber (per Addendum #2 confirmation that either model is acceptable)

Pathway C — Surgical-Coordinated (estimated 8% of enrolled members)

Evidence base: LOOK AHEAD Trial (sustained 7–8% loss at 4 years with intensive lifestyle); bariatric surgery outcomes literature

Components: pre-surgical clinical evaluation, post-surgical lifestyle support, coordination with member's chosen surgical center, long-term behavioral coaching

Clinical cadence: aligned with surgical center protocol; Vitality provides wrap-around support

5.4 Behavioral engagement model

Dr. Vann's behavioral engagement model is the engine that sustains member engagement — the County's #1 KPI.

Weekly check-in cadence during Phase 3 (intensive intervention), via the Vitality PWA, with structured prompts and a coaching response within 24 hours.

Biweekly group sessions during Phase 3 and Phase 4, virtual cohort format, 8–12 members per cohort, facilitated by Dr. Vann or a licensed coach under her supervision.

Family and youth programming extensions for members with dependents — addressing the household environment that drives sustainable weight management.

Automated escalation for any member who does not check in for 14 days, routed to a human coach for outreach.

Engagement scoring for the dashboard, surfaced to the County in quarterly reports.

5.5 VITA AI persistent-memory advisor

VITA is Vitality's AI coaching assistant, embedded in the member portal and the employer dashboard. VITA is differentiated from generic AI chatbots by three properties:

Persistent memory. Every member conversation is stored. VITA resumes context on every new session — there is no "starting over." A returning member sees a "Resuming — X prior exchanges" badge on their portal.

Live clinical context. VITA receives the member's current weight, BMI, GLP-1 status, active care plan phase, recent weight trend, and achieved milestones with every message. Responses are anchored to the member's real situation, not generic content.

Evidence-cited responses. VITA's clinical answers cite specific studies (DPP, STEP, LOOK AHEAD, RAND). This is a deliberate design choice for an RFP-evaluator audience as well as for clinical defensibility.

VITA operates in two modes: a warm, Dr. Love–voiced member mode, and an analytical, ROI-focused employer mode for the County dashboard. Both modes share the same persistent-memory architecture.

5.6 The GLP-1 managed-care protocol — the headline differentiator

This is the operational answer to the $2.09M Year-1 savings opportunity. The protocol has six components:

Initial clinical evaluation by Dr. Love for every member entering Pathway B. Establishes baseline metrics, comorbidities, contraindications, and titration plan.

Structured titration over 12–16 weeks with proactive side-effect management. Members are contacted on day 3, day 7, day 14, and weekly thereafter during titration to surface and address side effects before they cause discontinuation. This is the single most important intervention against the 47% attrition pattern.

OptumRx fill-history reconciliation. Vitality receives weekly fill-history feeds from OptumRx and reconciles them against expected fills. Members who miss a fill receive coaching outreach within 48 hours.

Behavioral coaching from Dr. Vann's team throughout titration and maintenance. Members on GLP-1 receive structured nutrition support, activity coaching, and sleep/stress modules — the lifestyle scaffolding that makes the medication work.

Coordination with member's existing prescriber (if the member chooses to remain with their PCP rather than have Dr. Love prescribe). Vitality acts as the supporting coaching and reporting layer; the member's PCP retains prescribing authority. Per Addendum #2, the County has confirmed this model is acceptable.

Monthly clinical visits during titration; quarterly thereafter. Dr. Love (or a credentialed clinician on her team) maintains direct medical oversight throughout the member's GLP-1 course.

The protocol directly targets the 47% attrition rate visible in the County's 705-to-373 cohort transition. If Vitality reduces twelve-month attrition from 47% to 25% — a conservative target relative to managed-GLP-1 published outcomes — the County retains an additional ~155 members on effective therapy in Year 1, with corresponding compounding outcomes and savings.

Section 6 — Technology Platform

6.1 PWA architecture

The Vitality member experience is delivered as a Progressive Web Application (PWA). This means:

No app-store install required. Members access the program from any modern web browser on any device — phone, tablet, laptop, desktop. The PWA supports add-to-home-screen on iOS and Android for native-app-style access without the friction of an app-store flow.

Offline capability. Members can log weight, complete daily check-ins, and review coaching materials offline; data syncs when connectivity returns.

Push notifications on Android and modern iOS, for coaching prompts, medication reminders, and weekly check-in nudges.

Accessibility: WCAG 2.1 AA compliance, Section 508 compliance, screen-reader support, keyboard navigation, high-contrast mode.

The platform stack is React 19 + TypeScript on the front end, Express 5 on the API server, PostgreSQL with Drizzle ORM for the database, and a Claude AI (Anthropic) integration for the VITA assistant. All software is built and maintained by Dr. Flood's team at The COLLABORATIVE Advocate Foundation.

6.2 Integration with UnitedHealthcare (TPA)

UnitedHealthcare is Sedgwick County's TPA on the self-insured plan (Group #902769). Vitality integrates with UHC across three surfaces:

Eligibility feed: SFTP or API-based daily roster sync from UHC's eligibility files. Establishes the eligible adult population (4,213 per Addendum #2) and supports automated enrollment validation.

Claims data feed: Monthly de-identified claims extract under BAA, used for outcome measurement (claims-cost trend, hospital and ER utilization for the at-risk cohort).

FHIR R4 read access (if available from UHC's API gateway): supports real-time member clinical context for VITA and Dr. Love's clinical team.

Integration timeline: 30–45 days from contract execution, dependent on UHC IT scheduling.

6.3 Integration with OptumRx (PBM)

OptumRx is Sedgwick County's PBM. Vitality integrates across two surfaces:

GLP-1 authorization status feed: read-only access to OptumRx prior-authorization decisions for members enrolled in Vitality. This allows Dr. Love's clinical team to know which members have current GLP-1 authorization without manually contacting members.

Fill-history feed: weekly extract of GLP-1 fill events for enrolled members. Drives the fill-reconciliation process described in Section 5.6.

We do not request prescribing authority within OptumRx, do not modify the formulary, and do not change any prior-authorization rules. Vitality's role is observational and coordinative.

Integration timeline: 30–45 days from contract execution.

6.4 Coexistence with Rally

Rally is the County's current member-engagement platform, provided through UHC. Vitality coexists with Rally rather than replacing it. Our integration model:

Single-sign-on hand-off. Members can launch Vitality from Rally with a one-click SSO flow (Azure AD / SAML 2.0).

Engagement data exchange. Vitality engagement metrics (check-ins, coaching touchpoints) can be exported to Rally to roll up into the County's broader wellness dashboard.

No duplication of general-wellness content. Rally remains the source of truth for general wellness (gym reimbursement, biometric screenings, mental health). Vitality is the source of truth for weight management and GLP-1 utilization specifically.

Three observations follow:

(b) The 47% attrition rate is the smoking gun. 705 members started a GLP-1 in the past twelve months but only 373 are on therapy today. That ~47% discontinuation rate is consistent with published real-world GLP-1 adherence data and is the textbook signature of unmanaged GLP-1 therapy.

(c) The managed-protocol savings opportunity is approximately $2.09M per year. At RAND's conservative $5,600-per-patient savings differential, migrating the 373 current users captures approximately $2.09M annually — nearly funding the entire $1.71M Year-1 program cost.

The 47% attrition rate demonstrates that prior authorization alone is not converting pharmacy spend into outcomes. The Addendum further clarifies the County's preference: "A broader support-focused program centered around outcomes, health coaching participation, and sustainable lifestyle changes." The County explicitly rejected a compliance-focused program.

A.3 The County is buying program design, not administration
