## Executive Summary

HerHealth Network by Sankofa is a clinically rigorous, culturally grounded digital health platform built specifically for Black and minority women. It delivers comprehensive health information, an AI navigator, and 2,100+ verified local resources across 70 women's health conditions organized into 7 condition platforms. It is the anchor platform of the Sankofa Health Network — a three-platform ecosystem that also covers Black men's health (TheHealthyBlkMan.com) and Black maternal health (YourHealthBirthright.net).

The platform is live, production-ready, and currently seeking grant funding for dissemination, community outreach, and ongoing platform maintenance to reach the populations it was designed to serve.

## The Problem

- Black women are 40% more likely to die from breast cancer than white women, despite lower incidence rates
- Black women are 3–4 times more likely to die from pregnancy-related causes than white women
- Lupus is 2–3 times more prevalent in Black women than in white women, with later diagnoses and worse outcomes
- Black women have higher rates of hypertension, Type 2 diabetes, fibroids, and autoimmune conditions
- Existing digital health platforms are built on white, middle-class health data
- Social determinants of health rarely addressed alongside clinical information in consumer platforms

## The Solution: HerHealth Network by Sankofa

### Core Platform Architecture

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| 70 Conditions | Full clinical coverage across 7 domains — Cancer, Cardiovascular, Autoimmune, Mental Health, Reproductive Health, Metabolic, and Maternal Health |
| Nia AI Navigator | AI-powered health navigator trained on minority women's health data, SDOH barriers, and clinical disparities |
| 2,100+ Verified Resources | State-by-state database covering all 50 states, DC, and U.S. territories |
| 5 Guided Decision Wizards | Interactive tools for mammography, genetic testing, clinical trials, treatment navigation, support groups |
| Appointment Preparation Center | Condition-specific question lists, symptom trackers, supplement disclosure tools |
| Find a Provider | ZIP-code-based culturally competent provider matching |
| MAP-GAP Methodology | Every page maps WHERE users are (the gap) and bridges them to where they need to be |

### The 7 Condition Platforms (10 conditions each, 70 total)

1. **Cancer Platform** — Breast cancer (IBC, triple-negative, BRCA), cervical, ovarian, endometrial, colorectal, lung, thyroid, skin
2. **Cardiovascular Platform** — Hypertension, heart disease, stroke, arrhythmia, heart failure, peripheral artery disease
3. **Autoimmune Platform** — Lupus (SLE), rheumatoid arthritis, multiple sclerosis, sarcoidosis, fibromyalgia, Sjögren's
4. **Mental Health Platform** — Depression, anxiety, PTSD, bipolar disorder, postpartum depression, grief, racial trauma
5. **Reproductive Health Platform** — Fibroids, endometriosis, PCOS, fertility, menopause, sexual health
6. **Metabolic Platform** — Type 2 diabetes, obesity, thyroid disorders, vitamin deficiencies
7. **Maternal Health Platform** — Preeclampsia, gestational diabetes, placenta previa, postpartum care

## Population Served

- **Primary:** Black and minority women, ages 18–65+, across all U.S. states and territories
- **Secondary:** Black men, Black pregnant/postpartum women, healthcare providers, CHWs, patient navigators
- **Geographic Reach:** National, with resource depth in Texas, California, New York, Georgia, Illinois
- **SDOH Populations:** Medicaid-eligible, uninsured, underinsured, food insecure, housing unstable, transportation-limited
