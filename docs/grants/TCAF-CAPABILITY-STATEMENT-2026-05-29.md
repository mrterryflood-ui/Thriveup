### SDVOSB — Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (Highest-Leverage Unlocked Advantage)

Dr. Flood is a medically retired U.S. Army veteran with a service-connected disability (MS). This makes TCAF presumptively eligible for SDVOSB certification — the most powerful set-aside preference available in federal contracting.

**What SDVOSB unlocks:**
- **VA Veterans First Contracting Program (38 U.S.C. §§ 8127–8128):** VA is *required by statute* to award contracts to SDVOSBs when two or more qualified SDVOSB firms can perform the work at a fair and reasonable price. VA exhausts SDVOSB set-asides before competing broadly.
- **FAR 19.1405 / VAAR 819.7005:** Mandate SDVOSB set-asides for VA acquisitions above $10,000 where ≥2 SDVOSBs can compete.
- **Government-wide preference:** Named set-aside category across DoD, HHS, DoL, VA, and others.
- **Sole-source authority (FAR 19.1406):** VA may sole-source up to **$5 million (services)** or $7M (manufacturing) to a certified SDVOSB without competition.
- **Federal goal:** 3% of all prime federal contract dollars governmentwide must go to SDVOSBs.

**Certification status:** SBA VetCert application **NOT YET FILED** — filing this is the highest-priority contracting action for TCAF in 2026. Once certified, the SSG Fox award, follow-on VA work, and any VA RFP become significantly more accessible via set-aside authority.
