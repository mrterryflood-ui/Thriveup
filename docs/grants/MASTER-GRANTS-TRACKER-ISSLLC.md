## Why a separate tracker

The TCAF master tracker is sized for 501(c)(3) capability-fit scoring, TCAF UEI/CAGE/SAM, and TCAF signing authority. ARPA-H Other Transactions, SBIR/STTR, and other for-profit-eligible vehicles use different entity, different signing authority, different eligibility rules (especially SBIR's >50% PI-employment rule). Mixing them in the TCAF tracker risks blurring entity discipline — exactly the kind of cross-contamination Iron Rule #2 is designed to prevent.

## 5. Iron Rule reminders for this tracker

- Every dollar amount, deadline, and identifier here must trace to a primary source (solicitation PDF, agency page, direct comms). Concept-paper-stated numbers are NOT primary sources for federal claims — they're working drafts that themselves cite primary sources, which must be re-opened and quoted before any federal submission.
- Entity status (SAM, UEI, CAGE, employee count, ownership, US-performance) is the highest-risk category for ISS-LLC-side pursuits. Verify before claiming.
- Cross-entity claims (e.g., "BirthRight delivered through TCAF ecosystem") must accurately describe the operational relationship without implying TCAF is the submitter when it is not, and vice versa.
