## Synopsis (1 page)

### The Problem

Texas has 30 million residents, the second-largest economy in the nation, and some of the widest digital divides in the country. While Austin's tech corridor drives AI innovation, the vast majority of Texas communities — rural counties, historically marginalized urban neighborhoods, veteran populations, returning citizens, immigrant families, and small businesses — have no pathway to AI literacy, AI-augmented workforce skills, or practical AI adoption. The gap is not just educational; it is economic. Communities that cannot access, understand, and deploy AI tools will be permanently excluded from the AI-driven economy.

Current AI readiness efforts in Texas concentrate in research universities and tech companies. They reach students already enrolled in higher education and workers already employed in technology sectors. They do not reach the 4.3 million Texans living below the poverty line, the 1.5 million veterans transitioning to civilian careers, the 500,000+ returning citizens seeking workforce reentry, or the 2.8 million small businesses that need AI tools to compete but have no access to training or technical assistance.

### The Texas Coordination Hub

The Collaborative Advocate Foundation (TCAF) proposes to serve as the Texas Coordination Hub for NSF's AI-Ready America initiative, deploying a proven operational ecosystem to expand AI readiness across the state — reaching communities, businesses, and public-serving organizations that current efforts miss entirely.

TCAF brings something no university-only proposal can match: a 24-platform AI-powered ecosystem that is already operational, already serving under-resourced communities, and already producing measurable outcomes. This is not a proposal to build — it is a proposal to scale what works.

**The Ecosystem.** TCAF operates 24 interconnected platforms spanning workforce development (ThriveUp Academy, Mission Transition), health equity (SafeCogniCare, Black Maternal Health Network, Sankofa Health), education (WholeMind Learning, Code Canvas), business development (Pinnacle Business Conglomerate), community safety (Emergency Management, SafeReport), implementation science (Better Science Lab / RPLICE), and accessibility (LexiBridge). Each platform deploys AI tools for real-world use by real communities — not as research prototypes, but as operational infrastructure.

**The Curriculum.** ThriveUp Academy's five-level AI Mastery Curriculum — AI Explorer (Grades 3-5), AI Guide (Grades 3-5), AI Architect (Grades 6-8), AI Innovator (Grades 9-10), and AI Master (Grades 11-12 and adult learners) — provides structured AI literacy education from basic awareness through implementation leadership. The curriculum is delivered through WholeMind Learning's adaptive platform with built-in accessibility for neurodiverse learners. Adult and workforce tracks extend the curriculum into practical AI deployment for healthcare workers, small business owners, government agencies, and community organizations.

**The Coalition.** TCAF's existing cross-sector coalition includes municipal governments (Pflugerville, Manor), financial institutions (Frost Bank), higher education (Austin Community College), faith-based organizations (Fountain of Life Ministries), and school districts (Pflugerville ISD). This coalition provides the trusted community access points — libraries, community centers, bank branches, campuses, churches — where AI readiness training reaches populations that never walk into a university computer lab.

**The AI Infrastructure.** TCAF's Collaborative AI Engine runs four AI models in parallel (Gemini 2.0 Flash, Claude, GPT-4o-mini, DeepSeek R1) with RAG knowledge retrieval and implementation science frameworks (CFIR 2.0 / RE-AIM) embedded in every interaction. This is not single-model AI — it is consensus-driven collaborative intelligence designed for accuracy and reliability in high-stakes community applications like benefits enrollment, workforce navigation, and health equity.

**Nationwide Reach, Texas Hub.** TCAF's 24-platform digital ecosystem is already accessible nationwide — anyone, anywhere can access AI literacy training, workforce tools, and community support through thrivingcommunitiesforall.com. The Texas Coordination Hub leverages this existing national infrastructure to deepen in-person deployment across Texas. TCAF's headquarters in Pflugerville (Williamson County) anchors Central Texas operations, with coalition partners spanning Travis, Williamson, Hays, Bastrop, and Caldwell counties providing trusted physical access points. Year 1 establishes intensive in-person Hub operations across Central Texas while the digital ecosystem continues serving communities nationwide. Year 2 deploys in-person regional nodes in Houston, Dallas-Fort Worth, San Antonio, and the Rio Grande Valley. Year 3 achieves full statewide in-person coverage while demonstrating the hybrid digital-plus-physical model as a national replication template.

**Workforce Pipeline.** The Hub connects AI-ready graduates to Texas employers through structured internship, apprenticeship, and project-based work pathways. Frost Bank provides financial sector AI deployment. Healthcare systems provide clinical AI applications. Small businesses receive hands-on AI adoption assistance. The Capital Area Workforce Development Board connects graduates to employment. Every participant who completes the AI Mastery Curriculum earns a verifiable Workforce Readiness Certificate aligned with Texas CTE Employability Skills standards (TEKS §127.15).

**Evaluation.** TCAF's RPLICE implementation science toolkit provides rigorous formative and summative evaluation using the Consolidated Framework for Implementation Research (CFIR 2.0) and RE-AIM outcome measurement. An independent university-based evaluator will provide external validation. All data is publicly accessible through TCAF's evidence chain at thrivingcommunitiesforall.com/sdoh-explorer.

**Broadening Participation.** TCAF is veteran-founded and Black-led. Dr. Flood is an Industrial-Organizational Psychologist (MS, Walden, 4.0 GPA), Implementation Scientist (MS in progress, Dartmouth Geisel School of Medicine), and federal Public Health Social Scientist with 8+ years at VA and DoD. He holds two doctoral degrees (DHA, DBA), three additional master's degrees, federal grants management and COR certifications, and Stanford-certified AI in Healthcare training. He is a retired U.S. Army Warrant Officer and current Veterans Crisis Line trainer. Our ecosystem was designed from inception to serve historically marginalized communities — not as an afterthought, but as the core mission. The populations we reach — returning citizens, veterans, single parents, immigrant families, rural communities, Black and Latino youth — are precisely the populations excluded from current AI readiness efforts. This Hub does not just broaden participation in AI — it ensures that AI readiness is built BY the communities it serves.
