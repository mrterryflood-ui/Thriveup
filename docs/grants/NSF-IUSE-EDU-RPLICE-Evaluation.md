## WHAT THRIVEUP ACTUALLY IS

ThriveUp is a **community infrastructure platform** — a 24-platform interconnected ecosystem across 6 domains (Criminal Justice, Health Equity, Behavioral Health, Workforce & Business, Education & Learning, Community & Advocacy) powered by a 4-engine AI intelligence layer.

It is NOT a learning platform. It is NOT a K-12 platform. It is NOT a workforce tool. It is the operating system for how communities support, engage, connect, and serve their people — across every domain, at every stage of life.

**Education is one domain within the ecosystem.** Within that domain, ThriveUp serves learners from Pre-K through graduate school and professional development — because there is no ceiling on when a person stops needing to learn. The education domain includes:

| Subordinate Element | Platform | Scope |
|---------------------|----------|-------|
| Pre-K through Grade 12 | ThriveUp Academy, WholeMind Learning | Foundational through college-prep, TEKS-aligned |
| Special Education | Perfectly Different | IEP tracking, adaptive learning, family integration |
| Multilingual Access | LexiBridge | Language barrier removal across all levels |
| Undergraduate & Community College | ThriveUp Academy | Nontraditional students — veterans, returning citizens, working parents, first-gen |
| Graduate & Professional | ThriveUp Academy | Advanced credentialing, research pathways, professional development |
| Workforce Transitions | Mission Transition, Ecosystem Nexus | Military-to-civilian, reentry-to-career, career advancement |
| Implementation Science | Better Science Lab / RPLICE | CFIR, RE-AIM, evidence-based practice at every level |
| AI & Emerging Tech Literacy | AI Mastery Academy | 5-level curriculum (Explorer → Master) applicable at any education level |

**But education does not exist in isolation.** The undergraduate student on ThriveUp is ALSO connected to:
- **Health** — if she's diagnosed with breast cancer during finals, Sankofa Health activates
- **Behavioral Health** — if depression hits, Whole-Person Health coordinates with her academic plan
- **Workforce** — if she needs a part-time job, Ecosystem Nexus matches her skills
- **Community** — if her housing is unstable, Collaborative Advocate finds resources
- **Family** — if her child is struggling in school, ISSS wraps supports around the child
- **Criminal Justice** — if her brother comes home from prison, LifeBridge connects to the family file

No undergraduate STEM platform in existence does this. That is the innovation.

## RECOMMENDED APPROACH

**Track 1: Engaged Student Learning, Level 1 (up to $400K, 3 years)**

**Proposed Title:**
"Ecosystem-Integrated STEM Education: Studying Whether Community Infrastructure Improves Undergraduate STEM Outcomes for Underrepresented Populations"

**Proposed Research Question:**
"Does embedding undergraduate STEM education within a 24-platform community infrastructure ecosystem — where health, behavioral health, workforce, family services, and community resources are interconnected — improve STEM learning outcomes, persistence, and degree completion for nontraditional students compared to standalone educational technology?"

**Why This Is a Direct Fit:**
- ThriveUp's education domain already serves undergraduate learners. No reframing needed.
- The innovation is the ecosystem, not the curriculum alone. No other STEM education platform connects a student's organic chemistry grade to her cancer diagnosis to her child's school performance to her housing stability. ThriveUp does.
- The population IS the priority population: veterans on GI Bill, returning citizens entering college, working mothers, first-gen students, Black and Latino students, rural students, immigrants.
- RPLICE provides NSF-grade research methodology: CFIR 2.0, RE-AIM, Three Realities, MAP-GAP CQI.
- Dr. Flood's credentials — DHA, DBA, MS Implementation Science (Dartmouth), MS I-O Psychology, MS Criminal Justice — position him as the exact PI profile NSF wants: practitioner-researcher with both academic rigor and field experience.

**The Ecosystem Argument:**
Traditional STEM education assumes the only variable that matters is what happens in the classroom. ThriveUp knows that's false. When a nontraditional undergraduate fails organic chemistry, the root cause might be depression, job loss, childcare collapse, housing instability, family crisis, or all of the above. The AI intelligence layer sees across all 24 platforms and identifies root causes that no standalone STEM tool can detect.

The research question is: does seeing the whole person — not just the student — produce better STEM outcomes?
