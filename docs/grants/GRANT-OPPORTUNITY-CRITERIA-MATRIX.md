  6. Organizational authority/governance (board governance, consumer input)

- [ ] Must provide crisis intervention services (24/7 or via partnership)
- [ ] SAM.gov registration required
- [ ] Letters of support from local health systems, law enforcement, social services

**YOUR FIT**: Whole-Person Health's clinical assessment engine (C-SSRS, PHQ-9, GAD-7) + Sankofa's health equity navigation + LifeBridge's SDOH resource connection = foundation for CCBHC model. Would need to add or partner for 24/7 crisis services and licensed clinical staffing.
