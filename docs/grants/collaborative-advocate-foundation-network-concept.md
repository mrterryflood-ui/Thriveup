### What We Are

The Collaborative Advocate Foundation Network is a **backbone infrastructure organization** — not a service provider that competes with local nonprofits, but the shared operating system that makes every affiliated partner more effective. We provide the coordination layer, technology platform, grant infrastructure, and measurement framework. Affiliated partners — churches, CHW organizations, school districts, reentry nonprofits, workforce programs, media partners — continue doing what they do best, now connected to a network that amplifies their impact and funds it.

This model follows the **collective impact backbone framework**: a central entity that coordinates aligned, independent partners working toward shared community outcomes, aggregates outcome data, and secures funding at a scale no single partner could reach alone.

### How Affiliation Works

A local organization affiliates with the Foundation Network and immediately gains:

- **Platform access** — connect their participants to any of the 25 platforms relevant to their population
- **Shared referral pathways** — warm handoffs, not cold lists
- **Outcome measurement** — standardized metrics flow into the shared network dashboard; their impact becomes part of the aggregate grant report
- **Grant support** — Foundation staff assists with grant readiness, proposal development, and funder matchmaking
- **Training and credentialing** — staff access ThriveUp Academy, CHW training, AI literacy, and digital credentials
- **Co-branded intake** — participants enter through the affiliate's trusted identity, not a generic portal

**What affiliates give:** outcomes data (standardized, aggregated), co-branding rights, referral participation, and willingness to be named in the network's collective impact reporting.

**What affiliates do not give up:** their autonomy, their identity, their existing funder relationships, or their programs.

### The Cycle That Makes It Self-Sustaining

```
A resident hears on Morning with Meredith that a local nonprofit
is helping justice-involved individuals find employment.

The episode links to the platform.

The resident discovers: workforce training, transportation assistance,
benefits screening, career coaching, digital credentials, employer partners.

They enroll.

Their progress is tracked.

Outcome data flows to the affiliate nonprofit's grant report.

The funder sees measurable results.

The nonprofit secures additional funding.

Meredith later interviews the participant about their success.

That story inspires others.

The cycle repeats.
```

The podcast isn't reporting on change — it is producing it.
