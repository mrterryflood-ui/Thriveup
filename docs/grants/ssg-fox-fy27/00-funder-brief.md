## Scope of covered services (per NOFO)
The program is **primarily non-clinical** — extends suicide prevention into financial, occupational, social, and spiritual supports — with linkage to VA clinical care.

Eligible activities:
- Outreach to identify and engage at-risk veterans + service members + families
- Case management
- Peer support
- Validated screening using **Columbia-Suicide Severity Rating Scale (C-SSRS)** — required
- Linkage / warm handoff to VA clinical care + community resources

**Risk-level gate:** participant must screen at least low-risk on C-SSRS. This is NOT an acute high-risk crisis program (those route to 988 / Veterans Crisis Line).

## Geographic priorities (VA's stated priorities)
VA may prioritize awards in:
- Areas with limited access to medical services
- Rural communities
- Tribal lands
- US territories
- Areas with **high number or percentage of minority Veterans or women Veterans**
- Areas with high Veterans Crisis Line call volume
