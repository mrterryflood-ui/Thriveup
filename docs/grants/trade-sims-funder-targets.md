# ThriveUp Trade Sims — Funder Target List

**Scope:** funding sources for the certification + apprenticeship + multilingual game-based trade-learning portion of ThriveUp Academy.
**Prepared:** 2026-05-17.
**Status:** working list. Fit ratings are TCAF's internal assessment, not funder confirmations. **Every amount, deadline, and program scope below must be verified at the funder's website before any LOI or proposal goes out — Iron Rule.**

---

## Tier 1 — Direct trade/apprenticeship fit, high probability

| Funder | Program | Why it fits | Verification URL |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lowe's Foundation** | Gable Grants for Community and Technical Colleges (Skilled Trades Expansion) | Explicitly funds trade-skills capacity at $1M–$2.5M scale. Free multilingual trade content + credential routing is exactly the "remove barriers to trade entry" thesis they fund. | https://www.lowes.com/l/about/lowes-foundation.html |
| **The Home Depot Foundation** | Path to Pro Skills Program | Funds trade-skills training and certification, with priority on Veterans and underrepresented populations. M2 alignment is a natural pairing. | https://corporate.homedepot.com/foundation |
| **USDOL ETA** | Apprenticeship Building America (ABA) | Federal grant program explicitly funding apprenticeship expansion, modernization, and pre-apprenticeship pipelines. TCAF as pre-apprenticeship content provider is an in-scope sub-award role. | https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/apprenticeship/grants |
| **USDOL ETA** | H-1B Skills Training Grants | Funds workforce training in high-demand occupations. Skilled trades (electrical, HVAC, welding) are explicitly named in recent NOFAs. | https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/skills-training-grants |
| **USDOL VETS** | Homeless Veterans' Reintegration Program (HVRP) / SSG Fox | Already in pipeline via vetmissiontransition.com (separate codebase). Trade Sims = content-layer cite-in for the HVRP and SSG Fox narratives. | https://www.dol.gov/agencies/vets |
| **Texas Workforce Commission** | Skills Development Fund | TX-specific grant funding custom training partnerships between community colleges and employers. Multilingual + credential-pathway content is differentiated. | https://www.twc.texas.gov/programs/skills-development |
| **Texas Workforce Commission** | Jobs and Education for Texans (JET) Grant | TX grant for CTE equipment + curriculum at high schools and community colleges. Trade Sims as the digital curriculum overlay. | https://comptroller.texas.gov/programs/education/jet/ |

## What this list does not include

- Funders we know have declined or paused for FY26/FY27 cycles (e.g., St. David's Foundation WAB2 LOI declined 2026-05-15 — see `replit.md` Gotchas).
- Funders requiring a research PI or a 5-year research outcome design — those go to the BetterScience/RPLICE pipeline, not the Trade Sims content layer.
- "Aspirational" national funders we don't yet have a credible touchpoint or alignment story with — listed elsewhere as long-horizon.

This list is the working set for the cert + apprenticeship + multilingual trade-content scope, and the right place to start the next 6–12 months of outreach.
