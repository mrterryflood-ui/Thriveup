## Cost Per Unit of Service

| Metric | Count | Cost per unit |
|---|---|---|
| Unique residents reached | ~1,500 | $100 / resident |
| Validated BH screenings completed | ~1,500 | $100 / screening |
| Crisis events routed (multilingual) | ~400 | $375 / event |
| Clinical Decision Support sessions (HITL-reviewed) | ~250 | $600 / session |
| Veteran transition crisis screenings | ~200 | $750 / screening |
| SDOH-linked navigations | ~500 | $300 / navigation |

These per-unit numbers compare favorably to public-system equivalents (a single ED behavioral-health visit averages $1,200–$2,400; an inpatient BH admission averages $7,100/day). Crisis-routing prevention work at $100–$750 per touch produces strong cost-offset arithmetic for the payer.

| Source | Status | Role |

| Cigna Youth Mental Health 2026 | Targeted June 2026 cycle reopen ($150K) | Family-school-clinical youth BH wedge |

| Hogg Foundation for Mental Health | Strategic outreach planned | Statewide BH workforce + peer support |
| St. David's Foundation | Actively evaluating (status framing per their request) | Community health overlay |
| In-kind platform infrastructure | TCAF self-funded | Server/AI/cloud costs absorbed by TCAF |

The Centene Foundation award would underwrite the **demonstration-quality evaluation and clinical-decision-support layer** that the other foundations do not specifically fund, making this the highest-leverage piece of the portfolio.
