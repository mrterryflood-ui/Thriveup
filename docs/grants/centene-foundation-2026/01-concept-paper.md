## Executive Summary

Most community behavioral-health programs do one of two things well: they serve a single language community with deep cultural fluency, or they integrate with payer/clinical systems with technical rigor. Very few do both. TCAF operates a 15-platform national community-infrastructure model — Kansas-piloted in Sedgwick County (Wichita), with on-the-ground clinical and faith partners already in place — that closes that gap in production today.

We are requesting **$150,000 over 12 months** to expand behavioral-health crisis-routing capacity for ~1,500 Medicaid-eligible residents across three cohorts that Centene's Kansas plan (**Sunflower Health Plan**, the state's largest KanCare managed-care organization) already covers but often struggles to reach: Limited-English-Proficient (LEP) families across 89 spoken and 18 signed languages, Black and Brown communities, and recently-separated veterans (Wichita is home to McConnell Air Force Base and a significant Kansas Army National Guard cohort). The work uses already-live TCAF platforms with documented healthcare-IT interoperability (FHIR + CDS Hooks), zero PHI egressed by design, human-in-the-loop default-on for every AI recommendation, and implementation-science evaluation through CFIR / RE-AIM frameworks.

We are a newly-determined 501(c)(3) (eff. 01/14/2026) with SAM.gov ACTIVE status and zero prior federal awards. We disclose that plainly. Our platforms, however, are not new — they have been operating as community-delivery infrastructure for multiple cycles, with measurable outputs (50 states covered for mandatory-reporter compliance, 20,670+ resources indexed in our virtual 211, 107 languages live in crisis routing). This grant funds the connective tissue and evaluation rigor required to take what's already running and prove it at the depth a Medicaid managed-care funder requires.

## The Problem

In Sedgwick County (Wichita is Kansas's largest city, ~395,000 residents, ~38% non-white), Medicaid-eligible Black, Brown, LEP, and veteran residents face three compounding barriers to behavioral-health care:

1. **Language access.** Wichita has a substantial Vietnamese, Latino, Burmese, Sudanese, and Somali refugee/immigrant population. Crisis-line capacity in non-English / non-Spanish languages is effectively nonexistent in the public system. Sign-language access in BH is rarer still.
2. **Cultural fluency gaps.** Black maternal-mental-health outcomes, Black men's depression screening rates, and refugee trauma-informed care all show measurable equity gaps in standard-of-care settings — a gap **Sistahs Can We Talk Inc.** (Kansas 501(c)(3) since 2015, serving the 29th & Grove community of Wichita) was founded explicitly to close on the women's-health side.
3. **System fragmentation.** A resident with a BH need typically faces 4–6 disconnected referral hops (211 → clinic → MH provider → SDOH support → benefits navigator → follow-up). Each handoff is a leak point. KanCare managed-care plans absorb the cost of every leak.

The cost is not abstract. Untreated BH conditions drive higher ED utilization, longer hospital LOS, and worse downstream chronic-disease outcomes — directly impacting Centene's Kansas (Sunflower Health Plan) medical-loss ratio on members the plan is already responsible for.

## Outcomes & Evaluation

Evaluation runs through **RPLICE**, our implementation-science engine, using two frameworks Centene's program officers will recognize:

**CFIR (Consolidated Framework for Implementation Research)** for fidelity and adaptation tracking across the five domains: intervention characteristics, outer setting (Medicaid policy environment), inner setting (TCAF platform delivery), characteristics of individuals, and process.

**RE-AIM** for population-level reporting:
- **Reach** — unique residents served, demographic distribution
- **Effectiveness** — screening-to-treatment-engagement rate; PHQ-9 / GAD-7 / C-SSRS score trajectories over time
- **Adoption** — number of referral partners actively using TCAF crisis routing
- **Implementation** — protocol adherence, HITL clinician review rate (target: 100%), citation coverage (target: 100%)
- **Maintenance** — 6-month re-engagement rates

We do not promise pre-post randomized-trial-grade causal inference at this funding level. We do promise transparent, audited, framework-aligned reporting that maps directly to Centene's evidence requirements for renewal consideration.
