## The Problem

In Travis County, Medicaid-eligible Black, Brown, LEP, and veteran residents face three compounding barriers to behavioral-health care:

1. **Language access.** Travis County has ≥35% household-language diversity. Crisis-line capacity in non-English / non-Spanish languages is effectively nonexistent in the public system. Sign-language access in BH is rarer still.
2. **Cultural fluency gaps.** Black maternal-mental-health outcomes, Black men's depression screening rates, and Hispanic-immigrant trauma-informed care all show measurable equity gaps in standard-of-care settings.
3. **System fragmentation.** A resident with a BH need typically faces 4–6 disconnected referral hops (211 → clinic → MH provider → SDOH support → benefits navigator → follow-up). Each handoff is a leak point. Medicaid managed-care plans absorb the cost of every leak.

The cost is not abstract. Untreated BH conditions drive higher ED utilization, longer hospital LOS, and worse downstream chronic-disease outcomes — directly impacting Centene's Texas medical-loss ratio on members the plan is already responsible for.

## Outcomes & Evaluation

Evaluation runs through **RPLICE**, our implementation-science engine, using two frameworks Centene's program officers will recognize:

**CFIR (Consolidated Framework for Implementation Research)** for fidelity and adaptation tracking across the five domains: intervention characteristics, outer setting (Medicaid policy environment), inner setting (TCAF platform delivery), characteristics of individuals, and process.

**RE-AIM** for population-level reporting:
- **Reach** — unique residents served, demographic distribution
- **Effectiveness** — screening-to-treatment-engagement rate; PHQ-9 / GAD-7 / C-SSRS score trajectories over time
- **Adoption** — number of referral partners actively using TCAF crisis routing
- **Implementation** — protocol adherence, HITL clinician review rate (target: 100%), citation coverage (target: 100%)
- **Maintenance** — 6-month re-engagement rates

We do not promise pre-post randomized-trial-grade causal inference at this funding level. We do promise transparent, audited, framework-aligned reporting that maps directly to Centene's evidence requirements for renewal consideration.
