# NSF Advancing Quantum Education & Workforce Development — RPLICE Evaluation Sheet
## Dear Colleague Letter: NSF 21-033

---

## PROGRAM OVERVIEW

This is NOT a standalone funding program. It is a Dear Colleague Letter (DCL) — an official signal from NSF that they want to see quantum education proposals submitted through EXISTING programs. You submit to a regular NSF program (like STEM K-12 or IUSE:EDU) and reference DCL NSF 21-033.

---

## WHAT NSF WANTS

Projects that motivate and prepare students for quantum information science and engineering (QISE) careers at ALL levels — K-12 through graduate — in formal and informal settings.

Successful projects should:
1. Study, inspire, and support students' interest in QISE
2. Promote awareness and inform preparation for QISE jobs
3. Build the STEM skills and practices needed for quantum careers
4. Address curricula, educational approaches, and educator professional development
5. Broaden participation — explicitly calls out women, African Americans, Hispanics, persons with disabilities

---

## HOW TO APPLY

Submit through an existing NSF program and reference DCL NSF 21-033. Eligible host programs include:
- **STEM K-12 (NSF 25-545)** — ThriveUp's primary target
- **IUSE:EDU (NSF 23-510)** — ThriveUp's secondary target
- **Advanced Technological Education (ATE)**
- **Robert Noyce Teacher Scholarship Program**
- **INCLUDES** (broadening participation)

The proposal must meet ALL requirements of the host program. The quantum angle is an enhancement, not a replacement.

---

## AWARD AMOUNTS

Determined by the host program:
- Through STEM K-12: $350,000 - $750,000 (2-3 years)
- Through IUSE:EDU Level 1: Up to $400,000 (3 years)
- No separate quantum budget

## WHAT THRIVEUP ACTUALLY IS

ThriveUp is a **community infrastructure platform** — 24 interconnected platforms across 6 domains (Criminal Justice, Health Equity, Behavioral Health, Workforce & Business, Education & Learning, Community & Advocacy) powered by a 4-engine AI intelligence layer. It is the operating system for how communities support, engage, connect, and serve their people across every domain, at every stage of life.

Education is one domain. Within that domain, ThriveUp serves learners from Pre-K through graduate and professional development — with subordinate elements at every level:

| Level | Platform(s) | Capability |
|-------|------------|------------|
| Pre-K – Grade 12 | ThriveUp Academy, WholeMind Learning | Foundational STEM, TEKS-aligned, AI-powered |
| Special Education | Perfectly Different | IEP tracking, adaptive learning |
| Multilingual | LexiBridge | Language access at every level |
| Undergraduate | ThriveUp Academy | Nontraditional students, workforce-connected |
| Graduate & Professional | ThriveUp Academy | Advanced credentialing, research pathways |
| Workforce Transitions | Mission Transition, Ecosystem Nexus | Military-to-civilian, reentry-to-career |
| AI & Emerging Tech | AI Mastery Academy | 5 levels: Explorer → Master |
| Implementation Science | Better Science Lab / RPLICE | CFIR, RE-AIM, evidence-based practice |

**Quantum would become another subordinate element within the education domain** — a natural extension of the AI Mastery Academy pathway into next-generation computing.

## HONEST ASSESSMENT

### What Would Need to Be Built (This Is What the Grant Funds)
1. **Quantum Literacy Curriculum** — Modules introducing quantum concepts at age-appropriate levels across the full education continuum:
   - K-8: Superposition, entanglement as concepts (games, simulations, visual learning via WholeMind)
   - High School: Qubits, quantum gates, basic quantum circuits
   - Undergraduate: Quantum algorithms, quantum ML, quantum optimization
   - Graduate/Professional: Applied QISE, quantum sensing, quantum networking
   - Workforce: Quantum industry awareness, quantum career pathways via Mission Transition + Ecosystem Nexus

2. **AI → Quantum Bridge Content** — Natural extension of AI Mastery Academy:
   - Level 5 (current top): AI Master
   - Level 6 (new): Quantum-Ready — "You understand AI. Now understand the computing paradigm that will transform it."

3. **Educator PD for Quantum** — Professional development track for teachers, leveraging WholeMind Learning's visual-first approach

4. **Assessment Instruments** — Pre/post measures for quantum literacy adapted for each education level. Literature exists for some levels.

### What You Already Have That's Valuable
1. **The community infrastructure platform** — Not a learning app. An operating system for community support. Quantum education sits within a platform that also provides health, behavioral health, workforce, family, and community services. A student learning quantum who gets diagnosed with cancer doesn't lose access to education support — Sankofa Health and ThriveUp Academy are in the same ecosystem.
2. **The population** — The populations currently EXCLUDED from quantum education are the populations ThriveUp was built to serve. Black students, rural students, justice-involved youth, veterans' children, first-gen college students, immigrants. The DCL explicitly prioritizes broadening participation.
3. **The research framework** — RPLICE can study whether quantum literacy interventions work for underserved populations across the full education continuum. This is a gap in the quantum education literature.
4. **The ecosystem** — A student learning quantum is connected to workforce (quantum careers through Ecosystem Nexus and Mission Transition), behavioral health (STEM anxiety, imposter syndrome), family services (if the family is in crisis), community resources (housing, legal, benefits). No quantum education program in the country does this.

### The Credible Angle
ThriveUp is not a quantum physics lab. It is the broadening participation and community infrastructure engine.

"Quantum education currently reaches students at elite universities and well-funded suburban school districts. It does NOT reach the Black mother in Pflugerville, the veteran's child in Killeen, the returning citizen's daughter in East Austin, or the rural student in the Rio Grande Valley. We built the platform that reaches those populations. We built the research framework to study what works. We are proposing to develop, deploy, and study quantum literacy curriculum within a community infrastructure ecosystem that wraps health, behavioral health, workforce, and family support around every learner. NSF funds the curriculum development and the research. We provide the platform, the population, and the methodology."

### RPLICE VERDICT: TWO VIABLE PATHS

**Path A (Lower Risk): Embed in STEM K-12 Proposal**
Add a quantum education aim to the NSF STEM K-12 proposal. Primary research: AI-powered ecosystem education for underserved populations. Secondary aim: develop and pilot quantum literacy modules as the next frontier of AI education. References DCL NSF 21-033. No additional application. Low risk, high optionality.

**Path B (Higher Reward): Standalone IUSE:EDU Level 1**
Submit a separate IUSE:EDU Level 1 proposal ($400K, 3 years) focused specifically on developing and studying quantum literacy for nontraditional undergraduate students within the ThriveUp ecosystem. Deadline July 15, 2026. Positions for Level 2/3 follow-on ($750K-$2M).

**Path A is recommended if capacity is limited.**
**Path B is recommended if both STEM K-12 and IUSE:EDU are being pursued anyway** — the quantum angle differentiates the IUSE:EDU proposal from the STEM K-12 proposal and prevents reviewers from seeing them as duplicative.
