I am writing to express Pflugerville Independent School District's support for The Collaborative Advocate's application to [TWC RFA 32026-00162 / Rare Impact Fund] for the purpose of delivering workforce readiness training to Career and Technical Education (CTE) students.

The Collaborative Advocate, led by Dr. Terry Flood, has an established relationship with Pflugerville ISD. Dr. Flood currently serves as a member of our School Health Advisory Council (SHAC), demonstrating an ongoing commitment to student wellness and development in our community.

Pflugerville ISD serves a diverse student population in the greater Austin metropolitan area. We recognize the importance of equipping our CTE students with employability skills that complement their technical training. The Collaborative Advocate's ThriveUp Academy platform offers a comprehensive, technology-enhanced approach to workforce readiness that aligns with Texas TEKS §127.15 CTE Employability Skills standards.

We are interested in partnering with The Collaborative Advocate to:

- Provide access to CTE students for workforce readiness programming
- Coordinate with our CTE department on curriculum alignment and scheduling
- Support student credential attainment through the ThriveUp Workforce Readiness Certificate
- Share outcome data for program evaluation purposes (in compliance with FERPA)

We believe this partnership has the potential to strengthen our students' transition from education to employment and look forward to working with The Collaborative Advocate on this initiative.
