**Re: Letter of Support for The Collaborative Advocate — ThriveUp Workforce Readiness Academy**

Dear [Selection Committee / Program Officer],

I am writing to express Pflugerville Independent School District's support for The Collaborative Advocate's application to [TWC RFA 32026-00162 / Rare Impact Fund / Agency Fund] for the purpose of delivering workforce readiness training to Career and Technical Education (CTE) students.

The Collaborative Advocate, led by Dr. Terry Flood, has an established relationship with Pflugerville ISD. Dr. Flood currently serves as a member of our School Health Advisory Council (SHAC), demonstrating an ongoing commitment to student wellness and development in our community.

Pflugerville ISD serves a diverse student population in the greater Austin metropolitan area, with approximately 25% Black and 35% Hispanic enrollment. We recognize the importance of equipping our CTE students with employability skills that complement their technical training. The Collaborative Advocate's ThriveUp Workforce Readiness Academy offers a comprehensive, culturally responsive, technology-enhanced approach to workforce readiness that is fully aligned with Texas TEKS §127.15 CTE Employability Skills standards.

The program's design is distinctive in several ways that we believe will benefit our students:

- **Culturally responsive curriculum** featuring relatable student characters navigating real workplace scenarios in retail, food service, warehouse, and construction — industries where our students actually work
- **Phone-first delivery** via Progressive Web App, recognizing that many of our students access the internet primarily through mobile devices
- **AI-powered mock interview practice and professional communication coaching** available 24/7 for private, judgment-free skill building
- **A progressive resume builder** threaded across all 5 modules, so every student graduates with a complete professional resume built from their real experiences
- **Weekly live virtual check-ins** connecting students with neighborhood champions — local professionals and mentors from their own communities
- **"Real Talk" sections** addressing barriers our students face, including access to transportation, professional clothing, and technology

We are committed to partnering with The Collaborative Advocate to:

- Provide access to CTE students in grades 9–12 for workforce readiness programming
- Coordinate with our CTE department on curriculum alignment, scheduling, and pathway integration
- Provide classroom or lab space for instructor-led sessions as available and appropriate
- Support student participation in weekly live virtual check-ins
- Support student credential attainment through the ThriveUp Workforce Readiness Certificate
- Share outcome data for program evaluation purposes (in compliance with FERPA)

We believe this partnership will meaningfully strengthen our students' transition from education to employment and complement our existing CTE offerings. The program's culturally responsive design and community integration model align with our district's commitment to equity and student success. We look forward to working with The Collaborative Advocate on this initiative.
