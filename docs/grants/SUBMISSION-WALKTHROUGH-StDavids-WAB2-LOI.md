GivingData is a third-party grants portal that St. David's uses. The exact field labels may shift over time, but the flow is consistent.

1. **Go to** the St. David's Foundation grants page: https://stdavidsfoundation.org/funding-opportunities — click the "We All Benefit 2.0" link or "Apply Now."
2. **Sign in to GivingData.** If TCAF already has a GivingData account from prior cycles, use those credentials. If not: click "Register," use a TCAF organizational email (not a personal one if you have one), verify, return.
3. **Find the "We All Benefit 2.0 LOI"** opportunity in your dashboard. Click "Start Application."
4. **Organization Profile section** — confirm:
   - Legal name, EIN, address all match IRS records
   - 501(c)(3) status box is checked
   - Mission statement is current
   - Board roster is current (add/remove members as needed)
   - Most recent 990 and audited financials are uploaded (re-upload if older than 12 months)

   - **Geographic impact:** Regional (all 5 counties)

6. **Narrative section:** This is where you paste the LOI text.
