## I. Indirect Costs

TCAF will use the **10% de minimis rate** on Modified Total Direct Costs (MTDC) per 2 CFR 200.414(f).

**MTDC excludes:**
- Equipment over $5,000/unit
- Participant support costs
- Sub-award amounts exceeding $25,000 per sub-awardee
- Rental costs of equipment

**Year 1 MTDC calculation:**
- Total direct costs: $916,250
- Less participant support: ($50,000)
- Less equipment >$5K: ($0)
- Less sub-award excess (over $25K each): ($35,000 university partner excess)
- Adjusted MTDC: ~$831,250
- 10% indirect: $83,125 (rounded to $83,750 in budget)

## Budget Justification Notes

### Why 10% De Minimis?
TCAF does not currently have a federally negotiated indirect cost rate agreement (NICRA). Per 2 CFR 200.414(f), organizations without a NICRA may elect the 10% de minimis rate on MTDC. This maximizes direct program spending — 90 cents of every dollar goes directly to Hub activities.

**Action item:** TCAF may negotiate a rate with its cognizant federal agency after the first year of the award. This could increase the indirect rate in future awards but is not required.

### Cost Share / Match
NSF does not require cost sharing for TechAccess proposals unless specifically stated. TCAF will provide voluntary cost share through:
- Existing 24-platform ecosystem infrastructure (valued at $500K+/year in development and hosting)
- PI and staff time beyond the funded effort percentage
- Community partner in-kind contributions (facilities, staff time)
- CIP LLC implementation consulting (in-kind)

### Single Audit Requirement
Awards exceeding $750,000 in federal expenditures require a Single Audit per 2 CFR 200.501. Budget includes $5,000/year for audit compliance.

### Sustainability Beyond NSF
Year 3 budget includes sustainability transition:
- Workforce board integration for ongoing funding
- Employer partnership revenue for AI training delivery
- State appropriations through HHSC CPP and Texas Workforce Commission
- Fee-for-service AI readiness consulting for businesses
