## #1 — HRSA RCORP-Planning (HRSA-26-036)
**Rural Communities Opioid Response Program — Planning track**

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Status** | ✅ **OPEN** — Posted **April 29, 2026** (8 days ago) |
| **Funder** | HRSA / Federal Office of Rural Health Policy |
| **Award** | ~$200K, 1 year planning grant |
| **Fit Score** | **94/100** |
| **Submission window** | Wide (typically 60-day NOFO) — verify exact deadline on Grants.gov |

### Why this one (the strategic case)
This is the cleanest open opportunity in the entire ecosystem right now. RCORP-Planning is *built* for organizations like TCAF: nonprofits with strong service infrastructure but no formal SUD/opioid track record yet. The Planning grant funds you to build the consortium and infrastructure — then RCORP-Implementation is the natural follow-on at 5–10× the dollars.

It also lets TCAF formally enter the federal substance use prevention pipeline, which becomes a credentialing event for SAMHSA SPF-PFS and CDC OD2A in future cycles.

### Platform combination (this is where TCAF wins)
| Platform | Role in proposal |
|---|---|
| **LifeBridge** | The 24/7 virtual 211 + CHW dispatch = the **service navigation backbone** for opioid resource connection (treatment, MAT, harm reduction supplies, recovery housing). Already built. |
| **Whole-Person Mental Health Ecosystem** | C-SSRS suicidality + PHQ-9 + GAD-7 screening = the **clinical assessment layer** consortium members lack. Co-occurring SUD/MH is the standard of care. |
| **PillScheduler** | Medication adherence for MAT (buprenorphine, methadone) — direct opioid relevance. Bring online for this. |
| **Talk Your Talk (LexiBridge)** | 107 languages + crisis detection = the **language access layer** rural Texas communities cannot afford to staff. Differentiator. |
| **SafeCogniCare** | TBI–opioid overlap (TBI patients have 5–8× opioid disorder risk). Connects veteran/justice populations into the model. |
| **M2C** | Veterans are an opioid-disparity population; M2C provides the transition pathway. |

### Honest gating
- "Rural" definition: Travis County is technically MSA, but **Bastrop, Caldwell, Lee, Burnet** counties (TCAF's natural Eastern Crescent extension) qualify. Frame as Central Texas multi-county consortium with Austin as the anchor metro.
- Need 4+ consortium members documented. Foundation Communities, AVANCE, and Catholic Charities (already on AEI list) extend cleanly.
