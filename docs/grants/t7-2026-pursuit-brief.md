# T7 Social Impact Grant Challenge — Pursuit Brief

**Decision:** Pursue, subject to a short eligibility and evidence check before submission.

**Primary source reviewed:** `attached_assets/Social_Impact_Grant_Challenge_-_T7_Summit_2026_1787589751748.pdf`

## Why this is worth pursuing

- The packet anticipates **$1.5 million total** across one $500,000 award, one $250,000 award, two $100,000 awards, and eleven $50,000 awards.
- The deadline is **August 30, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. ET**. Applications cannot be edited after submission.
- The four evaluation criteria are equally weighted: organizational impact/results, alignment, feasibility of use of funds, and capacity/sustainability.
- TCAF’s strongest truthful alignment is **Community Transformation and Well-Being**, with a credible secondary connection to **Educational Access and Workforce Readiness** through technology-enabled navigation and workforce pathways.
- The application is worth submitting even if the award is competitive: the finalist pathway includes national visibility and a T7 Summit presentation opportunity.

## Eligibility gates to confirm before submitting

Do not guess or reuse a stale value. Confirm each item from current organizational records:

1. TCAF can provide current documentation showing 501(c)(3) public-charity status in good standing.
2. The organization is headquartered and operating in the United States.
3. The authorized representative, mailing address, phone, EIN, and email are correct.
4. Audited financials and/or financial statements for the past two years can be provided if requested.
5. The active board, financial controls, recent funders, annual budget, years in operation, direct-service count, and comparable grant history are documented.
6. TCAF can attend Atlanta finalist activities November 12–14, 2026 and cover finalist travel if selected.
7. The proposed program is open to participants without unlawful discrimination and TCAF accepts the reporting, evaluation, grant-agreement, and automatic-email-list terms.

## Recommended application frame

**Working title:** Community Bridge: Technology-Enabled Navigation from Resident Voice to Measurable Opportunity

Describe one fundable, bounded program—not the entire platform. The strongest frame is a resident-centered navigation and workforce-readiness pilot that:

- listens to resident priorities;
- connects residents to benefits, health, education, workforce, and community resources;
- uses responsible AI as decision support with human oversight;
- protects privacy and discloses observed data versus modeled outputs;
- tracks navigation, referral, implementation, and outcome measures; and
- produces a replicable learning model for additional communities.

Avoid claiming that the platform caused outcomes, that every listed capability is deployed nationwide, or that partner relationships/awards exist unless current records support those claims.

## Evidence to assemble

- Current IRS determination/good-standing documentation and organizational profile.
- Two years of financial statements and a one-page financial-control description.
- Board roster and governance/oversight summary.
- Verified direct-service count for the last 12 months.
- Verified funder list for the last 24 months.
- Verified impact measures and examples, with privacy-safe aggregation.
- Six-month launch plan, staffing, procurement, and partnership assumptions.
- A grant-specific budget tied to the selected request range; do not select a dollar amount until the budget is built.

## Recommendation

**Proceed to application drafting and eligibility confirmation.** Do not submit until the seven gates above are checked and the final narrative is reviewed against the official rules. The opportunity is aligned enough to justify the effort, while the packet’s equal 25% scoring weights mean a disciplined capacity, budget, and evidence package matters as much as the idea.
