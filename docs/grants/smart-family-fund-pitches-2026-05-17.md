## Pitch A-FULL — Long-form (for reference / follow-up package)

**The problem.** America needs ~3.2M new skilled-trades workers this decade. The pipelines that exist — community colleges, registered apprenticeships, OEM bootcamps — assume English fluency, daytime availability, and a learner who already knows the trade exists. They lose the people who would benefit most: foster-youth alumni, justice-involved adults, returning veterans, immigrant workers, monolingual Spanish speakers, and rural learners without a shop teacher.

**Our intervention.** ThriveUp Trade Sims is a game-based learning platform that takes a learner from "I've never held a multimeter" to "I'm ready to test for OSHA 10 / NCCER Core / EPA 608" entirely online, free, in their language. Five trades are live today — electrical, plumbing, HVAC, welding, automotive — with 75 lessons running on real physics engines (Modified Nodal Analysis for circuits, Hardy-Cross for pipe flow, etc.). Every lesson uses the same 5-loop pattern: concept → guided practice → solo challenge → sandbox → AI debrief.

**Why this works where others don't.** Four built-in differentiators, all live today:
1. **5-loop pedagogy** — not video-then-quiz. Learners build, break, and debrief inside a real simulator.
2. **4-engine AI tutor** (Gemini, Claude, GPT, DeepSeek) — gives hints without giving away answers, in the learner's language.
3. **Multilingual at launch** — 10 languages (EN, ES, VI, ZH, AR, KO, FR, TL, HI, MY), AI-translated + cached.
4. **Credential-pathway routing** — each trade unlocks a vetted prep surface (OSHA 10, NCCER L1, EPA 608/609, ASE G1, AWS SENSE) at 80% lesson completion. We route to sponsors; we never charge for test fees.

**The quiet differentiator.** Every Trade Sims design decision was made against an implementation-science checklist:
- **CFIR** to identify which contexts (rural ISDs, reentry programs, refugee resettlement orgs) will adopt fastest.
- **RE-AIM** to measure Reach, Effectiveness, Adoption, Implementation, Maintenance — not just enrollments.
- **RPLICE** to plan replication into trades 6–N (solar PV, masonry, diesel mech) without rebuilding the engine.

**Demonstrating efficacy.** Our 200-learner pilot launches July 1, 2026. We measure: lesson completion rate, time-to-credential-readiness, language-of-instruction effect on completion, credential-test pass rate at sponsor sites. Pflugerville ISD is our K-12 anchor (signed). Austin Community College is our post-secondary partner (in outreach). Mission Transition (M2) routes veterans.

**What angel funding unlocks.** Outcomes-capture instrumentation, credential-test pass-rate tracking, and partner cohort recruitment for trades 6–8. Smart Family Fund's investment becomes the evidence base we use to compete for Promise Neighborhoods (Aug 2026) and DOL Strengthening Community Colleges Round 7.

> **Foster-Youth Transition Engine: meeting reintegration where it actually happens.**
>
> Each year roughly 20,000 young people age out of U.S. foster care. Within four years, one in five is homeless. Fewer than three percent earn a four-year degree. The reason isn't the youth — it's that benefits, housing, identity documents, education, and employment live in fifty different state systems with fifty different rules, and the youth must navigate every one of them at the moment of maximum disruption.
