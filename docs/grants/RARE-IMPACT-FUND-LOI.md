## Organization Overview

The Collaborative Advocate is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization headquartered in Austin, Texas. We are veteran-founded, Black-led, and registered on SAM.gov. Our mission is to empower under-resourced communities — particularly youth, veterans, returning citizens, and families navigating systemic barriers — through technology-driven workforce development, education, and wraparound support services.

We operate ThriveUp Academy, an AI-powered workforce development platform serving as the anchor of a 24-platform interdependent ecosystem. This ecosystem connects workforce training, health equity, community resources, case management, mentorship, and career pathways into a single coordinated system — because the communities we serve don't face single problems, they face interconnected challenges that require interconnected solutions.

## The Problem

Young people in under-resourced communities — particularly Black and brown youth, youth in rural and suburban areas surrounding Austin, and first-generation college and career seekers — face a compounding gap between classroom learning and workforce readiness. They graduate without the employability skills employers demand: professional communication, time management, workplace safety awareness, understanding their rights, and the work ethic that sustains careers.

In the Pflugerville, Manor, and East Austin communities we serve:
- Youth unemployment rates for Black teens are 2-3x the state average
- CTE enrollment is growing, but employability skills training has not kept pace with industry demands
- Mental health challenges, housing instability, and food insecurity create barriers that traditional workforce programs ignore
- Employers report that entry-level hires lack basic professional conduct, punctuality, and communication skills

These are not character deficiencies — they are training gaps that no one has filled. The school system teaches content. Employers expect readiness. The gap between the two is where young people fall.

## Our Solution: ThriveUp Workforce Readiness Program

ThriveUp Academy delivers a comprehensive, AI-powered workforce readiness curriculum aligned to Texas TEKS §127.15 (CTE Employability Skills) standards. Our program covers all 20 employability standards across two levels:

**Core Modules (15 weeks total):**
1. **Professional Presence** — Workplace conduct, dress codes across industries, interview skills, professional communication, personal branding
2. **Workplace Rights & Responsibilities** — Discrimination and harassment awareness, Title VII basics, reporting procedures, bystander responsibilities, employee rights
3. **Workplace Safety Essentials** — OSHA fundamentals, hazard identification, PPE, emergency procedures, safety plan development
4. **Time & Priority Management** — Prioritization frameworks, calendar skills, deadline management, group coordination, productivity systems
5. **Work Ethic & Career Leadership** — Punctuality, dependability, meritocracy, organizational structures, manager vs. leader comparison, career advancement through character

**What makes ThriveUp different:**
- **AI-powered personalization** — Each student's learning path adapts to their pace, interests, and career goals using AI companions that provide culturally responsive, age-appropriate guidance
- **Wraparound ecosystem** — Students don't just learn employability skills in isolation. ThriveUp connects to mental health support (Whole-Person Health), community resources (ISSS), career exploration (Career Pathways), financial literacy (Financial Literacy Hub), and mentorship (Mentor-to-Career) — because you can't focus on interview skills when you haven't eaten
- **Digital credential** — Completers earn the ThriveUp Workforce Readiness Certificate, a verifiable digital credential mapped to all 20 TEKS §127.15 standards
- **Real partnerships** — Our founder, Dr. Terry Flood, serves on the School Health Advisory Council (SHAC) for Pflugerville ISD, providing direct access to CTE students and district leadership
