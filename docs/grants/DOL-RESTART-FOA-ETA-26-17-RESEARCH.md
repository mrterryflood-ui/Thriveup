## PROGRAM PURPOSE

RESTART connects formerly incarcerated individuals and those with prior criminal justice involvement to training and employment pathways in skilled trades and high-demand sectors. The initiative builds on prior DOL reentry programming (Reentry Employment Opportunities / REO program).

Funds support organizations delivering:
- **Pre-apprenticeships**
- **Work-based learning**
- **Credential attainment**
- **Paid work experience**
- **AI and digital literacy training**

Training is prioritized in **skilled trades, advanced manufacturing, and Registered Apprenticeship programs**.

| Faith-based organizations | Yes (encouraged) |

### Intermediary vs. Direct Grantee Tracks

**Track 1 — National/Regional Intermediaries (~$30M reserved):**
- Must operate across **3+ non-contiguous metropolitan areas or rural regions**
- Can be nonprofits with or without 501(c)(3)
- Must identify sub-grantees
- Serving youth and young adults
- Cap: ~$5.1M per award

**Track 2 — State/Territory/Tribal Projects (remaining ~$51M):**
- Must integrate RESTART activities with existing public workforce system under WIOA
- Leverages existing workforce infrastructure
- Focused on sustainability after grant period

## PROJECT NARRATIVE REQUIREMENTS

### Scored Sections (Standard DOL/ETA Framework):

### 1. STATEMENT OF NEED
- Labor market data for target area
- Community need documentation
- Target population justification
- Criminal justice system data (incarceration rates, recidivism, unemployment among formerly incarcerated)
- Gap analysis — what services currently exist vs. what's missing

### 2. PROGRAM DESIGN
- Participant journey: intake → assessment → training → placement → retention
- Pre-release services (for those still incarcerated):
  - Job preparation
  - Individual Development Plans (IDPs) identifying barriers to employment
  - Career exploration and planning
  - Experience and skill matching
  - Counseling
  - Assistance obtaining state identification
  - Linkage to social services for community transition
- Post-release services:
  - Pre-apprenticeships
  - Work-based learning
  - AI and digital literacy training
  - Credential attainment
  - Paid work experience
- Must name **specific trade sectors and credentials** (e.g., NCCER, OSHA-10/30, AWS welding cert, CompTIA, etc.)
- Comprehensive work plan for entire period of performance with feasible dates
- Evidence-based approaches

### 3. ORGANIZATIONAL CAPACITY
- Staff qualifications
- Management structure
- Past performance in similar programs
- Financial management capability
- Data collection and reporting systems

### 4. PARTNERSHIPS
- Required partner categories with documentation:

| Partner Type | Requirement | Documentation |
|---|---|---|
| Local Workforce Development Board (LWDB) | **Required** | MOU or Letter of Commitment |
| American Job Center (AJC) | Required (WIOA-aligned) | MOU |
| Employer Partners | **Required** | Commitment Letters |
| Training Providers | Often required | MOU or subcontract agreement |
| Community-Based Organizations | Encouraged | Letter of Support |
| Registered Apprenticeship Sponsors | **Priority consideration** | MOU |
| Correctional Facilities | Expected | MOU or agreement |

**LWDB MOU must include:**
- Each party's roles and responsibilities
- How LWDB will co-enroll or refer participants
- Data sharing arrangements
- Resource/cost-sharing commitments
- Signatures from authorized representatives

### 5. OUTCOMES / PERFORMANCE MEASURES
(See Performance Measures section below)

## PERFORMANCE MEASURES & OUTCOMES

### Primary Employment Outcomes

| Measure | Definition | Historical Benchmark (REO) |
|---|---|---|
| Employment Rate — Q2 After Exit | % of exiters employed in 2nd quarter after program exit | 68% (adults), 66% (youth) |
| Employment Rate — Q4 After Exit | % of exiters employed in 4th quarter after program exit | Tracked separately |
| Median Earnings — Q2 After Exit | Median wages of employed exiters in Q2 post-exit | $6,400 (adults) |
| Employment Retention | Sustained employment across measurement periods | Tracked |

### Credential & Training Outcomes

| Measure | Definition |
|---|---|
| Credential Attainment Rate | % of participants earning industry-recognized credential |
| Measurable Skill Gains | % achieving education/training milestones during participation |
| Apprenticeship Enrollment | # placed into Registered Apprenticeship programs |

### Recidivism Outcomes

| Measure | Definition |
|---|---|
| Recidivism Rate | % of participants re-arrested or re-incarcerated during/after program |
| Recidivism Reduction | Comparison to baseline recidivism in service area |

## BUDGET REQUIREMENTS

### Standard Budget Categories (SF-424A)

| # | Category | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Personnel** | List all positions by title; annual salary, % time on project, grant-funded amount |
| 2 | **Fringe Benefits** | Itemize FICA, health, retirement, workers' comp, SUTA; can use composite rate |
| 3 | **Travel** | Domestic & foreign separate; necessary, reasonable, Fly America Act |
| 4 | **Equipment** | Unit cost ≥$5,000 + useful life >1 year; requires prior written Grant Officer approval |
| 5 | **Supplies** | Items <$5,000/unit; identify categories with quantity and unit cost |
| 6 | **Contractual** | Subcontracts, consultants; procurement rules apply |
| 7 | **Other** | Rent, meeting costs, other items — itemize sufficiently |
| 8 | **Indirect Costs** | Must have NICRA or use 10% de minimis rate |
| 9 | **Participant Support Costs** | Separate, protected category (stipends, transportation, tools, work gear, emergency assistance) |

### Allowable Cost Principles (2 CFR 200 + 2 CFR 2900)
All costs must be:
- **Necessary & Reasonable** for the project
- **Allowable** under statute, 2 CFR 200, and 2 CFR 2900
- **Allocable** to the award
- **Consistently treated** per organization's policies

### Cost Sharing / Match
- DOL reentry grants historically **do not require cost sharing or matching funds**
- Verify in FOA Section III — some FOAs encourage but do not require match
- Voluntary cost sharing, if included, becomes binding once awarded
