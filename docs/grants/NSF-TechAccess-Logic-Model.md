## ACTIVITIES

### Activity 1: AI Literacy Training Delivery
- Deploy 5-level AI Mastery Curriculum at community partner sites
- AI Explorer → Guide → Architect → Innovator → Master progression
- Deliver through WholeMind Learning adaptive platform
- Bilingual delivery (English/Spanish)
- Mobile deployment via tablets at libraries, churches, community centers, bank branches
- Adult/workforce tracks: Healthcare AI, Business AI, Government AI, Community AI

### Activity 2: Workforce AI Upskilling
- Develop AI-augmented job readiness programs
- Create internship placements with employer partners
- Establish DOL-registered AI apprenticeship pathways
- Deliver project-based community AI work (capstone projects)
- Issue Workforce Readiness Certificates aligned with TEKS §127.15

### Activity 3: Small Business AI Adoption Assistance
- Provide hands-on AI tool training for small business owners
- Deploy AI marketing, operations, and customer analytics tools
- Offer 1-on-1 AI adoption consulting through Hub staff
- Connect businesses with AI-ready graduates for project work

### Activity 4: Coalition Building and Statewide Scaling
- Year 1: Solidify Central Texas coalition operations
- Year 2: Launch regional nodes in Houston, DFW, San Antonio, Rio Grande Valley
- Year 3: Achieve statewide coverage, document replication methodology
- Recruit employer partners across financial, healthcare, technology, manufacturing sectors
- Coordinate with NSF National Coordination Lead

### Activity 5: Implementation Science Evaluation
- Conduct CFIR 2.0 readiness assessments at each deployment site
- Measure RE-AIM outcomes continuously
- Collect pre/post assessment data on AI literacy and skills
- Track workforce placement and business AI adoption outcomes
- Publish findings in peer-reviewed journals
- Annual external evaluation by independent university-based evaluator

### Activity 6: Technology Platform Scaling
- Scale 24-platform ecosystem for increased Hub traffic nationwide
- Enhance Collaborative AI Engine for Hub-specific applications
- Build data dashboards for NSF reporting and public transparency
- Maintain and update AI Mastery Curriculum content

## EXTERNAL FACTORS / ASSUMPTIONS

| Assumption | Risk if Invalid | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| University partner secured as Co-PI | Weak research credibility | Multiple university outreach in parallel |
| Employer partners willing to hire AI-ready graduates | No workforce pipeline destination | Start employer recruitment immediately, Frost Bank as anchor |
| Community members willing to participate in AI training | Low enrollment | CHW outreach, trusted community partner sites, participant stipends |
| AI technology remains accessible and affordable | API costs increase | Multi-model architecture provides alternatives; budget includes API costs |
| NSF National Coordination Lead provides effective cross-hub support | Isolated hub operation | Build independent interstate relationships |
| State/local government supportive of AI readiness initiatives | Policy resistance | HHSC CPP pathway already established; municipal partners confirmed |
| Broadband access sufficient for digital AI training | Rural connectivity gaps | Mobile-responsive design, offline-capable content, CHW in-person delivery |
