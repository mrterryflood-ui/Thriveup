## WAB 1.0 Insights → How TCAF Addresses Each

### Insight #1: Effective outreach enrolled 27,000+ despite policy changes
**TCAF Response:** Our coalition model distributes enrollment across multiple partner sites (libraries, campuses, banks, community centers) so when one channel faces policy disruption, others continue. Mission command: decentralized execution means no single point of failure.

### Insight #2: Policy expertise + peer connections sustain capacity
**TCAF Response:** Our digital ecosystem connects every partner to shared tools, updated policy guidance, and real-time enrollment data. Partners learn from each other's results — what's working in Pflugerville can be replicated in Manor within days. BetterScience Lab / RPLICE provides implementation science framework for continuous learning.

### Insight #3: Trust-building over years, culturally responsive practices
**TCAF Response:** Bilingual CHWs embedded in communities they already belong to. Mixed-status family approach: screen children's eligibility without requiring parent immigration status. Our approach recognizes trust cannot be manufactured or rushed — it's built by showing up consistently, year after year.

## Mission Command Operating Model

### Doctrine: Decentralized Execution, Centralized Intent

**Commander's Intent (TCAF as Backbone):**
- Every eligible family in the 5-county region is screened, enrolled, and retained in benefits
- Data is transparent — every partner sees outcomes in real time
- No family hits a dead end — if one pathway is blocked, another opens

**Decentralized Execution (Coalition Partners):**
- Each partner has autonomy to execute in their community using their strengths
- Pflugerville operates through its library and municipal channels
- Manor operates through community centers and local networks
- ACC operates through campus-based student services
- Frost Bank operates through financial wellness programming
- Faith-based partners operate through congregational trust

**Reporting, Communication, Accountability:**
- Real-time enrollment dashboards shared across all partners
- Monthly coalition meetings for cross-learning and adaptation
- Public evidence chain at thrivingcommunitiesforall.com/sdoh-explorer
- HHSC CPP reporting feeds state-level accountability
- Implementation science measurement (CFIR/RE-AIM) for fidelity

**Auditing & Quality:**
- Every enrollment tracked from screening through application through confirmation
- Annual renewal tracking ensures retention, not just initial enrollment
- Census tract-level gap analysis identifies where the coalition needs to grow
- Public, verifiable data — any stakeholder can audit the evidence chain

**No Single Point of Failure:**
- If TCAF's digital platform is down, CHWs continue with paper screeners
- If one partner exits, their community connections transfer to remaining partners
- If federal policy changes (as WAB 1.0 grantees experienced), the coalition adapts collectively
- HHSC CPP certification means enrollment infrastructure persists beyond any single grant
