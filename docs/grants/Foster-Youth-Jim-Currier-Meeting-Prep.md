### Minute 22–24 — National Agency Portal (the new ask)
- Navigate to **`/foster-youth/state-portal`**.
- "This is what a state or county child-welfare agency would log into. They upload de-identified caseload data, the system stratifies it by documented research factors — Midwest Study, NYTD, Casey, Vera — and surfaces who needs coordinated stakeholder attention before they age out."
- Click the **Method & Sources** tab. "Every factor cites a source. No black box. The youth is never 'high risk' — the system response is."
- Click a case → show the ISS-style stakeholder coordination panel. "Caseworker, school counselor, healthcare PCP, mental-health clinician, CASA/GAL, court, PHA — who needs to be in the room. Modeled on the integrated student-support pattern at implementationineducatio.com."
- Honest disclosure card: "What's live is the engine. What's roadmap is the CCWIS integration and FERPA/HIPAA MOU."

### Minute 24–26 — 50-State Policy Comparison
- Navigate to **`/foster-youth/policy-comparison`**.
- "Non-adversarial. We surface what's working in each state so we can lift the floor everywhere. Pick TX and CA. Show extended foster care to 21, tuition waiver, transitional housing, ID waiver."

### Minute 26–28 — The bigger picture (loud and proud)
- "This is one experience inside a 5-platform ecosystem. Talk Your Talk gives us 89 spoken + 18 sign = 107 languages — meaningful access for LEP foster youth. Civic Signal handles community advocacy. LifeBridge is the 20,670-resource navigator backbone. ThriveUp Academy is the AI-enabled workforce/education arm. Whole-Person Health is the behavioral-health safety floor underneath all of it. **Crisis events from any platform route INTO Whole-Person Health.**"

- "Federal envelope in this lane: ~$500M+ annually — Chafee · ETV · FYI · YHDP · RHYA · WIOA · OJJDP. Texas's slice is ~$15M+. We are asking for nothing today; we are showing readiness."

### Minute 28–30 — The ask
> "Three specific things, then I'll listen.
> 1. **Tell me the one thing on the FYI rubric that disqualifies most applicants** — so we don't accidentally make ourselves disqualifiable.
> 2. **Introduce us to the Austin/Travis County PHA(s)** you trust most for an FYI conversation. We can stand up the supportive-services side; we need a PHA partner for the voucher side.
> 3. **What evaluation evidence does HUD want** to see from a non-traditional FYI partner before they're considered fundable? We will instrument and report whatever you tell us matters."

## Anticipated Q&A

| Question Jim might ask | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Are you a Title IV-E placing agency?" | "No. We are an IRS-determined 501(c)(3) (Letter 947, eff. 01/14/2026), SAM Active, CAGE 209N1, operating community infrastructure. We are designed to wrap around placing agencies and PHAs, not replace them." |
| "Do you have a current Texas DFPS contract?" | "Not today. Conversations are at the early stage. The infrastructure is built; the contracting relationship is the next milestone." |
| "How do I know your tools work for foster youth specifically vs. the general population?" | "Honest answer: today, our LifeBridge analytics aren't segmented by foster-youth user. We have a 30-day plan to fix that — see `Foster-Youth-Outcome-Tracking-Plan.md`. We can be reporting foster-youth-specific outcome numbers within 30 days of starting that work." |
| "Where's the data going?" | "Wellbeing check-in: nowhere — local browser only. Toolkit + Transition Plan: localStorage only. We do not collect PII on these tools. Authenticated tools (FAFSA navigator, etc.) run on our PostgreSQL stack with Replit Auth." |
| "What about LGBTQ+ foster youth, who have higher homelessness risk?" | "Our resource navigator (LifeBridge `/resources`) tags LGBTQ-affirming providers; the Wellbeing screen does not assume a gender or family structure; and we lead with culturally responsive language. We treat that as table stakes, not a feature." |
| "How is this different from 211?" | "211 is information referral. We are a structured transition plan, a screening pathway, a rights-citation library, a multilingual access layer, and a 50-state benefits navigator — built around the specific federal entitlements that apply to this population. 211 is the door; this is what's behind it." |
| "Why should HUD care about TCAF?" | "Because FYI's success metric is youth housed and stable at exit, and the limiting reagent in most FYI sites is the supportive-services partner that wraps around the voucher. We are designed to be that partner, at scale, multilingual, with no per-unit cost to the youth." |
| "What's your competition?" | "Honestly, no one else is building the integrated 5-platform stack. The closest analogs are siloed: iFoster does platform; Foster Care Alumni of America does community; FC2S does ETV; Casey does research. We integrate." |
