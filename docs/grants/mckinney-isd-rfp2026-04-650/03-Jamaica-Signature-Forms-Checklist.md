## Standard Forms (typically required — check zip for actual forms)

### 1. Conflict of Interest Questionnaire (Form CIQ)
**Authority:** Texas Government Code §176.006
**What it is:** Discloses any relationships between the vendor (or its officers/employees) and McKinney ISD officers or employees.
**Sign:** Authorized representative of Hargrave Innovative Solutions
**Notes:** If no conflict exists, still file — check "no" and sign. Must be filed with the McKinney ISD Records Administrator.

### 2. HB 1295 Certificate of Interested Parties
**Authority:** Texas Government Code §2252.908 / Texas Ethics Commission
**What it is:** Lists all "interested parties" (5%+ ownership interest, board members, etc.) in the contracting entity. Must be filed electronically through the Texas Ethics Commission portal AND a paper copy submitted to the district.
**Process:**
1. Go to https://www.ethics.state.tx.us/whatsnew/elf_info_form1295.htm
2. Complete Form 1295 online for Hargrave Innovative Solutions
3. Print the certificate (includes a certification number from TEC)
4. Have the authorized representative sign the printed form
5. Submit signed copy to McKinney ISD Purchasing AND electronically confirm on the TEC portal
**Sign:** Authorized representative of Hargrave Innovative Solutions

### 3. Felony Conviction Notification
**Authority:** Texas Education Code §44.034
**What it is:** Vendor must state whether any principals (officers, owners, operators) have been convicted of a felony. Applies to business entities and sole proprietors.
**Sign:** Authorized representative
**Notes:** Simply check the applicable box (no conviction, or disclosure) and sign. Failure to disclose is a Class A misdemeanor.

### 4. Debarment / Suspension Certification
**Authority:** 2 CFR Part 200 (EDGAR) / Federal Executive Order
**What it is:** Certifies that the vendor is not currently debarred, suspended, proposed for debarment, or otherwise excluded from federal procurement programs.
**Sign:** Authorized representative
**Notes:** If McKinney ISD uses any federal funds to pay vendors on this contract (Title I, IDEA, ESSER, E-Rate, etc.), this certification is required.

### 5. Vendor Acknowledgment / Bid Certification Form
**What it is:** Acknowledges that the vendor has read the RFP, agrees to the terms, and certifies the accuracy of the response.
**Sign:** Authorized representative of Hargrave Innovative Solutions
**Notes:** This is typically the first or last page of the Ion Wave response and may be signed electronically inside the portal rather than on a separate form — check with Eric.

### 6. Non-Collusion Affidavit (if included)
**What it is:** Certifies that the submitted proposal was prepared independently without collusion with other vendors.
**Sign:** Authorized representative, sometimes requires notarization
**Notes:** Some Texas ISDs require notarization on this form. If the zip version has a notary block, Eric needs to arrange notarization before submission (same-day notary services available at UPS Stores, many banks, and notary apps like Notarize.com if remote signature is acceptable).

### 7. W-9 (Request for Taxpayer Identification Number)
**What it is:** Provides Hargrave's EIN to McKinney ISD for payment processing.
**Sign:** Authorized representative
**Notes:** Must match the entity name exactly as registered with IRS. If Hargrave is a sole prop vs. LLC vs. Corp, the entity type box must match the IRS registration.

### 8. EDGAR / Federal Funding Certifications (if included)
**What it is:** Package of federal certifications required when any federal funds may be used: Buy American preference, lobbying certification, environmental compliance, etc.
**Sign:** Authorized representative
**Notes:** Required if McKinney ISD uses Title I, IDEA, ESSER, or any other federal program funds to pay vendors on this contract — which is common for PD services at Title I campuses.

## If a form requires notarization
