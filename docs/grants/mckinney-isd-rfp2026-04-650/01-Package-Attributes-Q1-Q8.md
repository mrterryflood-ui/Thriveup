## Q1 — PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT, TRAINING, & CONSULTANT SERVICES
*Provide a description of professional development, training, and consultant services your company provides and for what department it may be useful.*
**Limit: 4,000 characters · Estimated: ~2,800 characters**

---

Hargrave Innovative Solutions (HIS), in partnership with ThriveUp Academy (The Collaborative Advocate Foundation, EIN 41-3618003), provides a comprehensive suite of professional development, training, and consulting services designed to serve every department within McKinney ISD — from campus instructional staff to district leadership to non-instructional operations teams.

**Instructional Professional Development Services**
We deliver evidence-based, job-embedded professional learning aligned to Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills (TEKS) and the Texas Teacher Evaluation and Support System (T-TESS). Offerings include: culturally responsive and sustaining pedagogy, trauma-informed classroom practices, differentiated instruction for diverse learners (ELL, Special Education, Gifted & Talented), inquiry-based learning design, and AI literacy for educators. All sessions are available as half-day (3 hr), full-day (6 hr), or multi-session learning series formats. Useful for: Curriculum & Instruction, campus principals, instructional coaches, classroom teachers (all grade levels and content areas).

**Leadership Development**
We provide executive coaching, instructional leadership academies, and facilitated learning communities for campus leaders and district administrators. Services include: data-driven decision-making coaching, strategic planning facilitation, equity-centered leadership workshops, and superintendent/cabinet leadership intensives. Useful for: Campus principals, assistant principals, district administration, department directors.

**Non-Instructional Professional Development**
We train operations, facilities, food service, transportation, and clerical staff on customer-service excellence, compliance requirements, workplace safety, technology fluency, and inter-department communication. Useful for: Operations, facilities management, transportation, nutrition services, district clerical and support staff.

**Technology Training**
We deliver hands-on technology training for both instructional and non-instructional staff, including: Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace administration and integration, learning management system (LMS) training, AI tool orientation and responsible-use frameworks, data privacy and FERPA compliance, and educational technology integration strategies. Useful for: Technology Services, instructional coaches, campus staff, district administration.

**Specialized Consulting Services**
Our consulting practice brings implementation-science methodology to district improvement efforts: Multi-Tiered System of Supports (MTSS) design, equity audit and gap analysis, strategic plan development, grant program design and evaluation, community engagement strategy, and continuous quality improvement using the Consolidated Framework for Implementation Research (CFIR). Useful for: District leadership, Special Programs, Equity & Inclusion, Grant Administration.

**Speaking and Presentation Services**
We provide keynote addresses, panel facilitation, and professional learning community (PLC) facilitation for district-wide professional development days, leadership retreats, board meetings, and community events. Useful for: All departments.

## Q5 — VIRTUAL TRAINING
*Does your company offer optional virtual professional development and training services? If so, please explain process.*
**Limit: 1,000 characters**

---

Yes. All services offered in-person are also available in virtual and hybrid formats. Our virtual delivery process:

**Platform:** Zoom, Microsoft Teams, or Google Meet — we adapt to whichever platform McKinney ISD uses. Sessions are hosted by HIS/TCAF with a co-facilitator managing participant engagement and Q&A.

**Scheduling:** Virtual sessions are scheduled via our booking portal with a minimum 72-hour advance notice. Recordings are available upon request with written District permission.

**Format options:** (1) Synchronous live — real-time facilitation with breakout rooms, polls, shared whiteboards, and chat. (2) Asynchronous — pre-recorded modules with embedded knowledge checks, delivered through our LMS or the District's platform. (3) Hybrid — in-room presenter with simultaneous Zoom link for remote participants.

**Technical requirements:** Stable internet and webcam required. HIS/TCAF provides all digital materials in advance. A pre-session tech check is included for groups of 20 or more.

## Q6 — VIRTUAL TRAINING COST
*Please explain if your company's virtual cost would differ from in person training? If it does not, enter N/A*
**Limit: 1,000 characters**

---

Virtual workshops are offered at a 10% discount from the equivalent in-person workshop rate. This reflects the elimination of travel time, onsite logistics, and related costs. Hourly consulting rates remain the same for virtual and in-person delivery, as consultant time and preparation are equivalent regardless of format.

For asynchronous module development (custom-recorded content built to District specifications), a one-time content development fee will be quoted separately based on scope and number of modules. Once developed, asynchronous modules may be reused by the District at no additional license fee for the duration of the contract.

Hybrid delivery (simultaneous in-person and virtual facilitation) is billed at the in-person rate; no additional charge for the virtual component when delivered concurrently. No travel-related costs (mileage, lodging, per diem) are charged for virtual or hybrid engagements unless an onsite component requires consultant travel beyond 50 miles from the McKinney ISD service area.
