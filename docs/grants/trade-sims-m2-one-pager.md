## What we built

**ThriveUp Trade Sims** is a free, no-login, multilingual, game-based learning platform that takes a learner from "I have never touched this trade" to "I am ready to register for an apprenticeship and an industry credential." It is a national platform, Texas-piloted.

**Five trades live today:** Electrical · Plumbing · Welding · Automotive · HVAC.
**Per trade:** 15 lessons (Day 1 → Day 15 capstone) = **75 lessons total**.
**Per lesson:** five learning loops — Concept → Guided → Solo → Sandbox → AI Debrief.
**Engagement integrity:** "Mark Complete" requires either running the interactive simulator or writing a ≥40-word reflection. Tab-clicking does not count.
**Languages:** EN + ES human-translated, 8 more (VI, ZH, AR, KO, FR, TL, HI, MY) opt-in AI translation.

## What just shipped (May 2026)

1. **Official-certification test prep, gated by course completion.**
   When a learner finishes ≥80% of a trade's lessons, the credential tab unlocks practice question banks for each industry credential associated with that trade. Practice questions are written from publicly documented exam objectives and are explicitly labeled "study questions, not official exam content." Every credential card links the learner to the sponsor for fee verification and scheduling.

2. **Apprenticeship route + locator information per trade.**
   For each trade, the platform surfaces the real Registered Apprenticeship pathways — IBEW/NECA for electrical, UA for plumbing, Iron Workers / SMART for welding, OEM tech programs for automotive, SMART/UA for HVAC — plus universal locators (apprenticeship.gov, TWC, WorkInTexas). Learners see typical program length, what they earn during the program, and how to apply, with direct links to each sponsor's "find a sponsor near you" page.

## Why this matters for M2 / Mission Transition

M2's mission is to move transitioning service members and Veterans from uniform into stable civilian work. ThriveUp Trade Sims is a **drop-in, no-cost, no-MOU-required tool** that M2 can hand to a Veteran today.

It complements (does not compete with) the SSG Fox FY27 work happening on `vetmissiontransition.com` — Trade Sims is content + practice + credential routing; the M2 site remains the case-management and program-of-record front door.

### Specifically, M2 Veterans can:

- **Try a skilled trade before committing to a school.** Build a working DC circuit in a browser before paying community-college tuition. Lay a virtual fillet weld before buying boots and a hood.
- **Find a paid apprenticeship pathway in their city.** Every pathway card links to the sponsor's own locator. We do not gatekeep referrals.
- **Earn a credential roadmap.** The platform tells a Veteran exactly which credentials matter for the trade they're trying (OSHA 10, NCCER, AWS, ASE, EPA 608, NATE, etc.), what each one proves, and where to schedule the exam.
- **Practice for a real exam.** The practice banks include rationales for every answer — Veterans get the "why," not just the "what."
- **Do all of this in their preferred language**, login-optional, on a phone, for free.

### How M2 can use it operationally

- **Direct learner referral:** point a Veteran to `https://[trade-sims-url]/academy/trade-sims` with no account creation required.
- **Co-branded cohort:** if M2 wants a Veteran cohort tracked together, TCAF can stand up an M2 referral code that tags those learners' anonymous progress for a quarterly engagement report (no PII, just lesson completion + practice attempts).
- **Embed:** the platform is built on stable URLs — M2 can deep-link from program emails or counselor portals straight into Day 1 of any trade.

## What we are NOT claiming

- We are **not** an apprenticeship program. We do not place Veterans into paid OJT. We route them to the sponsors who do.
- We are **not** the official exam. Practice questions are study aids. The sponsor's own prep materials are authoritative.
- We are **not** counting Trade Sims hours toward USDOL apprenticeship classroom requirements. A sponsoring JATC or chapter has to formally accredit hours for them to count.
