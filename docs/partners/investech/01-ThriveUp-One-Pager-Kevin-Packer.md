## Six domains we serve
1. **Criminal Justice & Reentry** — RNR (Risk-Need-Responsivity), CBI (Cognitive-Behavioral Intervention), NRRC outcome reporting, family-visitation tracking
2. **Health Equity** — Whole-Person Health, maternal health, behavioral health
3. **Behavioral Health** — SafeReport (compliance-grade clinical AI), longitudinal screening (PHQ-9, GAD-7, C-SSRS, PCL-5, ACES)
4. **Workforce & Business** — Mission Transition (veteran), Trade Sims, Minority Center of Excellence
5. **Education & Learning** — ThriveUp: AI literacy, financial literacy, STEM, FAFSA, foster-youth transition planning
6. **Community & Advocacy** — Civic Signal, Community Voice (map-pinned community input → AI clustering → ecosystem routing → #DATA storytelling)

- **Foster-Youth Transition Engine.** 50-state policy comparator with statute citations, 4-domain AI risk engine, capability-token security on the public no-auth wizard.
- **Justice & Reentry stack** — RNR + CBI + recidivism baselines + NRRC reporting in one production case-management surface.
- **We already find funders again.** The Grant Intelligence Engine scans daily and tier-scores fit. Funders evaluating a young org care whether the org can find them again next year. We can.
