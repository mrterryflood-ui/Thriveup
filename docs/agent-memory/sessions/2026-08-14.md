# Session — 2026-08-14

## Community Brief evidence-integrity contract

- Established `community-evidence/v1` as the required public disclosure contract for Community Brief data.
- ZIP and city inputs now disclose and render the resolved Census ZCTA rather than retaining citywide wording; county and multi-county inputs retain their true Census geography grain.
- Missing or malformed ACS fields fail closed. No Census, scenario, population, or narrative output may be populated from numeric defaults.
- TCAF-derived scores, scenario outputs, and AI narrative synthesis are explicitly labeled across the first-party brief, share, story, partner, PDF, presentation, and widget surfaces.
- Public sharing and exporting now require the complete v1 contract; legacy/invalid shares fail visibly instead of rendering unverified claims.
- Validation: live ZIP/city/county/multi-county route probes, share rejection/preservation probes, PDF/presentation content checks, `preflight`, and the Community Brief E2E workflow.

## Lesson

Public-data labels must identify the actual resolved geography and claim class. A plausible number is not evidence when the source field, denominator, or geography is absent.
