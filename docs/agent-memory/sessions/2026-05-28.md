### SNAP Focus Group — Full Prep Brief

**What it is:** Texas HHSC Community Partner Program focus group on SNAP application accuracy and payment error reduction. Dr. Flood selected as a community partner. Goal: share insights on confusion points, verification challenges, and simplification opportunities.

**Strategic framing:** Two purposes — (1) be the most substantive voice in the room by grounding every answer in TCAF's actual platform capabilities; (2) position for HHSC partnership / state contract / USDA-FNS grant pathway.

**The five topics and what to say:**

**Topic 1 — Areas of most confusion:**
> The problem is branching logic and question intent. Clients guess rather than understand. LifeBridge adds a plain-language explanation of *why* each question is being asked before the client answers. When clients understand the purpose, accuracy improves. "The confusion isn't reading — it's intent."

**Topic 2 — Reporting/verification challenges:**
> Verification failures are logistics failures, not compliance failures. Clients who can't upload a pay stub in 10 days aren't hiding income — they work multiple jobs and don't have a printer. TCAF built mobile document capture with warm hand-off to caseworkers. The question is whether HHSC's system can receive what we send.

**Topic 3 — Greatest-impact process:**
> Recertification. Every partner in the room will say this. SNAP lapses not from ineligibility but from timing failures. TCAF has proactive re-engagement: automated outreach at 60 and 30 days pre-recertification, guided workflows, peer navigator handoff for complex households.

**Topic 4 — Information clients find hard to understand:**
> Deductions — earned income deduction, excess shelter deduction, standard utility allowance. Clients skip these because they don't know they exist. TCAF's AI-guided intake proactively asks about each deduction and explains it. "Accuracy errors run both directions — under-claimed deductions are also a payment accuracy problem."

**Topic 5 — Opportunities to simplify:**
> Two things: (1) Pre-population — HHSC already has wage/SSA/case data; recertification should be verification of a pre-populated form, not starting over. (2) Standardized community partner submission API — give CBOs an authenticated submission channel so we can guarantee accuracy on what we touch, instead of every org building their own workaround.

**The positioning line (say this naturally, not as a pitch):**
> "We've built a benefits navigation platform that addresses most of what we're discussing. We're not here to pitch it — but if HHSC is looking at technology solutions, we'd welcome a conversation after today."

**Downstream pathways to flag internally after the session:**

- USDA-FNS SNAP Innovative Strategies grant (FNS funds 501(c)(3)s directly for SNAP access/accuracy demonstrations — LifeBridge is demonstration-ready)
