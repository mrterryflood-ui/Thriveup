## Lessons deposited

- **P-L*N* (proposal-audience discipline).** Iron Rule #5 RFP Fidelity Doctrine: write to the reviewer, not the end user. Drifted in §5.4-§5.8 first draft (explanatory tone to Dr. Flood himself); rewrite required when PI flagged the drift. Always lead each section with `"In response to Section 5.2 Criterion [N]'s requirement that…"` framing in submission-grade documents.
- **P-L*N* (clinician-only HITL is insufficient for in-moment crises).** A clinician on-call cannot stop a parent's hand at 2 AM. Detection-and-intervention platforms MUST include a proximate human layer (Warm Circle / 3am list / peer-mentor channel) between the AI detector and the clinical/state backstop. Front-loaded competency acquisition is a fidelity prerequisite, not optional content.
- **P-L*N* (reviewer intelligence is a discipline, not an optional polish).** Tabbara prior-award discipline (STRATEGIC-INTELLIGENCE-PLAYBOOK gotcha #19) is mandatory: identify funder office + authority, document reviewer scoring posture, inventory likely reviewers, name their published frameworks, mirror their vocabulary. Web-search + PI recognition triangulates fast; do this BEFORE writing submission-grade prose, not after.
- **Five-lens thinking** is now constitutional in `replit.md` — every proposal and design decision must hold implementation science + psychology + data engineering + community health work + UX simultaneously while still mirroring the RFP rubric. Never sacrifice one to optimize another.
