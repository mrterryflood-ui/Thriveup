## Addendum 2 — SDOH / SES gradient + DID income-maltreatment literature added

Dr. Flood hypothesized that SDOH/SES drove a measurable portion of the maltreatment numbers and that a difference-in-differences literature should exist. Confirmed via parallel web-search against primary sources:

**Verified figures now in the Solution Summary I&I table (line 87–90):**
- **NIS-4 (Sedlak et al. 2010) Table 5-2:** Low-SES physical neglect 27.0/1k vs not-low-SES 3.1/1k (~9× elevated); overall maltreatment ~5× elevated. Low-SES = income <$15k OR parental education <HS OR any poverty-program participation.
- **Raissian & Bullinger 2017** (*Children and Youth Services Review* 72:60–70): $1 ↑ state minimum wage → −9.6% in neglect reports overall; −10.8% for children ≤5; null for ages 13–17. Cleanest DID natural experiment in the field.
- **Berger, Font, Slack & Waldfogel 2017** (*Review of Economics of the Household* 15:1345–72): IV design using state+federal EITC generosity as exogenous income variation → reduced CPS involvement among unmarried families.
- **Klevens et al. 2017** (*Child Abuse & Neglect*): refundable state EITC → −3.1 AHT hospital admissions per 100k children <2 yr (P=.08, **marginal — accurately reported as marginal in the doc, not overstated**).
- **Pelton 1978** (*Am J Orthopsychiatry* 48:608–17): rebuts reporting-bias confound via child-fatality data — 80% Philadelphia / 70% NYC fatality-parents on public assistance.

**Three inserts made (parallel, single turn):**

1. **Problem table (line 87–90)** — added five rows: NIS-4 SES gradient, Raissian-Bullinger DID, Berger+Klevens DID, Pelton fatality data.

2. **Innovation & Impact paragraph (line 92)** — new paragraph **"Socioeconomic status is a known PFC-window amplifier — and PFC-Window is additive to economic-policy interventions, not a substitute."** Frames the mechanism as multiplicative (material stress compresses the PFC-regulation window further during PFC-consolidation years), positions PFC-Window as complementary to EITC/min-wage/SNAP/housing policy: material support reduces *load* on the PFC; this platform builds *capacity* and supplies *moment-of-stress reinforcement*. Pre-empts the "isn't this really just poverty?" reviewer critique that would otherwise be fatal.

3. **Technical Approach (line 142)** — risk-surrogate description now explicitly cites the DID income-maltreatment literature as a training-data source for SDOH amplifier features, enumerates the SDOH features (housing instability, food insecurity, income volatility, public-assistance receipt, parental-education proxy, sleep-gap as SES-correlated downstream stress indicator), and notes they're already shipped in the four-domain TCAF risk engine.

**Why this matters strategically (for Karlin/Shen):**
- DID natural experiments are causal-grade evidence — the top of the evidence pyramid for an implementation scientist. Adds a Karlin-respected evidence tier on top of our existing cross-sectional + longitudinal correlational base (Lebel, Putnam-Hornstein).
- Closes the "you're confusing PFC immaturity with poverty" attack vector before a reviewer can raise it.
- Strengthens empirical scaffolding for the Ethical-EI Iron Rule #3 default-population posture (Black/Latino/Indigenous/immigrant/justice-involved/foster/rural/**low-income** default).
