# TEKS §127.15 CTE Employability Skills — ThriveUp Academy Alignment Map

**Standard:** 19 TAC §127.15, Career and Technical Education Employability Skills, Adopted 2025
**Effective:** 2025-2026 school year
**Applicant:** The Collaborative Advocate (501(c)(3)), Austin TX
**Platform:** ThriveUp Academy — AI-Powered Workforce Development

---

## LEVELS 1 & 2 — Foundational Employability Skills

### (A) Professional Conduct — Dressing appropriately, speaking politely, conducting oneself in a manner appropriate for the profession and work site

| Status | Coverage |
|--------|----------|
| PARTIAL | Needs enhancement |

**Current ThriveUp Coverage:**
- "Polite Prompts" module teaches respectful communication habits using AI interactions as proxy for professional interpersonal skills (Module 1-2 Tools)
- SEL curriculum covers self-awareness and healthy relationships (Grades 6-12)
- Career Milestones include "Rising Professional" transition skills (Grades 11-12+)

**Gap:** No explicit module on workplace dress codes, professional appearance standards, or workplace conduct expectations. Communication is covered but physical presentation and workplace behavioral norms are not directly addressed.

**Mitigation:** Add "Professional Presence" module covering dress codes by industry, workplace etiquette, email/phone professionalism, and interview presentation skills. Can be built as a career milestone sub-module.

---

### (B) Teamwork — Describe teamwork, group dynamics, and conflict resolution and how they can impact collective outcome

| Status | Coverage |
|--------|----------|
| STRONG | Well covered |

**Current ThriveUp Coverage:**
- SEL Curriculum: "Healthy Relationships" and peer collaboration modules (Grades 6-8, 9-12)
- Community Partner Network with referral workflows teaches collaborative professional relationships
- PM Academy covers building RACI matrices, team communication plans, and stakeholder management
- Ecosystem "Team-of-Teams" architecture demonstrates cross-platform collaboration in practice
- CHW Core Competencies include Cultural Mediation and advocacy teamwork

**Gap:** Minimal — no specific "conflict resolution simulation" or group dynamics exercise for CTE-age students.

**Mitigation:** Add a conflict resolution scenario module (role-play exercises with AI-simulated workplace conflicts). Low effort — can extend existing SEL modules.

---

### (C) Communication — Present written and oral technical communication in a clear, concise, and effective manner for a variety of purposes and audiences

| Status | Coverage |
|--------|----------|
| STRONG | Well covered |

**Current ThriveUp Coverage:**
- AI Mastery Academy: Prompt Engineering as a core employability skill — teaches precise, clear communication
- 5 W's Prompt Builder (Who, What, When, Where, Why) — structured communication framework
- "Garbage or Gold" quality assessment — teaches students to evaluate communication effectiveness
- SpeechBridge Integration: Culturally Responsive Communication Training and Health Literacy coaching
- PM Academy: Communication plans, status reporting, stakeholder updates
- Polite Prompts: Professional tone and respectful communication habits

**Gap:** No explicit "technical writing" module (e.g., writing memos, reports, SOPs, or technical documentation specific to trades/industries).

**Mitigation:** Add "Workplace Writing" module — covers email etiquette, incident reports, shift handoff notes, basic technical documentation. Aligns with AI Creation Studio tools already built.

---

### (D) Time Management — Identify time-management skills such as prioritizing tasks, following schedules, and tending to goal-relevant activities

| Status | Coverage |
|--------|----------|
| MODERATE | Partially covered |

**Current ThriveUp Coverage:**
- Program Management Engine has milestone tracking, phase planning, and timeline management
- Career Milestones system is itself a time-bound progression (Grades 6-12+)
- PM Academy covers project scheduling and task prioritization
- Case Management dashboards track phase-based plans with deadlines

**Gap:** These tools exist for administrators/facilitators, not as direct student-facing lessons on personal time management. No explicit module teaching students how to prioritize their own tasks, use calendars, or manage competing deadlines.

**Mitigation:** Add "Time & Priority Management" student module — teach prioritization frameworks (urgent/important matrix), calendar skills, deadline tracking. Can integrate with the existing attendance/progress tracking system to give students hands-on practice.

### (E) Work Ethic — Define work ethic and discuss characteristics of a positive work ethic, including punctuality, dependability, reliability, and responsibility

| Status | Coverage |
|--------|----------|
| MODERATE | Partially covered |

**Current ThriveUp Coverage:**
- Attendance tracking system logs punctuality and participation

### (H) Discrimination and Harassment — Identify consequences relating to discrimination and harassment

| Status | Coverage |
|--------|----------|
| WEAK | Needs new content |

**Current ThriveUp Coverage:**
- SEL Curriculum touches on healthy relationships and conflict
- Equity-Focused Lens addresses systemic inequity broadly
- SafeReport platform in ecosystem handles safety reporting

**Gap:** No explicit module on workplace discrimination and harassment — definitions, types (sexual harassment, racial discrimination, age discrimination, disability discrimination), consequences (legal, professional, personal), reporting procedures, and bystander responsibilities.

**Mitigation:** Build "Workplace Rights & Responsibilities" module covering: Title VII basics, types of harassment, how to recognize and report, consequences for perpetrators, protection for reporters. This is a REQUIRED addition for TEKS compliance.

### (I) Workplace Safety — Discuss the importance of safety in the workplace and why it is critical for employees and employers to maintain a safe work environment

| Status | Coverage |
|--------|----------|
| WEAK | Needs new content |

**Current ThriveUp Coverage:**
- SafeReport platform exists in ecosystem for safety incident reporting
- OSHA-adjacent content not present in student-facing curriculum
- Emergency Management handles security/threat assessment but not workplace safety training

**Gap:** No student-facing workplace safety module. No coverage of OSHA basics, hazard identification, PPE, emergency procedures, or employer/employee safety responsibilities.

**Mitigation:** Build "Workplace Safety Essentials" module covering: OSHA basics, common workplace hazards by industry, PPE requirements, emergency procedures, right to refuse unsafe work, reporting procedures. Can reference SafeReport as the ecosystem's safety reporting tool.

### (J) Roles of Managers — Describe the roles and responsibilities of managers

| Status | Coverage |
|--------|----------|
| MODERATE | Partially covered |

**Current ThriveUp Coverage:**
- PM Academy covers project management leadership, RACI matrices, stakeholder management
- Program Execution Engine demonstrates management roles in practice (milestones, risk management, team coordination)
- Facilitator Hub models the instructor/manager role

**Mitigation:** Add "Understanding Management" module — covers organizational structures, manager vs. individual contributor roles, how to work with supervisors, what managers are responsible for. Brief module, can use real examples from the ecosystem.

## ENHANCEMENT PLAN

To achieve full TEKS §127.15 alignment, build 5 new curriculum modules:

1. **"Professional Presence"** — Workplace conduct, dress codes, interview skills, professional communication
2. **"Workplace Rights & Responsibilities"** — Discrimination, harassment, reporting, bystander duties, EEO basics
3. **"Workplace Safety Essentials"** — OSHA basics, hazards, PPE, emergency procedures, safety plans
4. **"Time & Priority Management"** — Personal productivity, calendar skills, deadline management, group coordination
5. **"Work Ethic & Career Leadership"** — Work ethic definition, punctuality, meritocracy, manager vs. leader roles, organizational structures

These 5 modules fill every gap identified above. Combined with ThriveUp's existing coverage (communication, teamwork, respect, critical thinking, financial literacy, career exploration), the platform would achieve **100% TEKS §127.15 alignment** across both Level 1-2 and Level 3-4 standards.
