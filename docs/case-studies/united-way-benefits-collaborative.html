The Story — Maria, Caldwell County

"Maria, a Caldwell County mother of two, was eligible for Medicaid, WIC, and the Earned Income Tax Credit for three years and received none of them. In 15 minutes on her phone, in Spanish, she enrolled in all three — capturing roughly $8,400 in annual benefits her family had been leaving on the table. Her story is the rule, not the exception."
— TCAF Case Documentation, 2026 · Caldwell County resident · Benefits screened: Medicaid, WIC, EITC

Maria's story is the data storytelling anchor for this initiative. Across the five-county region, tens of thousands of residents face the same invisible gap — eligible, but not enrolled. Not because they don't want help, but because the system was never designed to find them.

The Solution — One Intake, Full Network

A single 15-minute intake screens for all 9 benefits simultaneously and routes each applicant to the correct portal, with direct deep-links, receipt capture, and denial-to-appeal workflow built in. Any CHW, navigator, promotora, school liaison, pastor, FQHC front-desk staffer, or resident on a personal phone runs the same flow.

The Data Story — What Makes This Work

The Maria story leads because it converts a policy problem (benefits take-up gap) into a human moment (a mother, 15 minutes, $8,400/year). But the story only holds if the data behind it is auditable. That's what ChainWeb provides: every number that appears in a funder report, a community presentation, or a City Council meeting chains back to a primary federal source URL. We don't assert — we cite.

The United Way alignment is direct: the WAB2 initiative is structured as a United Way-style backbone, building shared infrastructure that every partner in the region uses — instead of each organization running its own fragmented intake and counting the same resident twice. The deduplication architecture exists specifically because United Way requires a single, honest count.

Fidelity and Evaluation

30-day MAP-GAP fidelity cycle with CFIR + RE-AIM checks. External evaluation by Dell Medical School / UT School of Social Work under an IRB-reviewed protocol with quarterly public dashboards. If yield drops in a tract, outreach shifts within the cycle. If denials spike on one program, navigator training is retooled. Active HHSC Community Partner Program Level 1.

Sustainability post-grant: HHSC CPP processing reimbursements, Medicaid 1115-waiver navigator billing, and ongoing TCAF infrastructure investment. Match: $120K in-kind partner clinical staff time + $80K TCAF platform infrastructure.
