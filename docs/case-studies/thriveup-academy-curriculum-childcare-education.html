What This Program Is

The ThriveUp Workforce Readiness Academy is an AI-powered employability skills program built for the students we actually serve: Black and Latino CTE students, first-generation career seekers, English Language Learners, students from low-income households, and students with disabilities in CTE pathways.

Design Principle

"This is not a worksheet program. It's built FOR students, not AT students." Every lesson features relatable student characters navigating real workplace scenarios. A progressive resume builder threads across all 5 modules so students graduate with a complete professional resume built from their real experiences.

Design Principles

📱 Phone-First (PWA)
Students install on Day 1 — full access from any device, anytime, no app store required
🎯 Culturally Responsive
Relatable scenarios, code-switching honored as a skill, "Real Talk" sections addressing real barriers
🤖 AI-Coached
Private mock interview practice, email feedback, resume review — available anytime, judgment-free
🌍 Inclusive
WCAG 2.1 AA, dyslexia-friendly fonts, English/Spanish bilingual, disability accommodations
🏘️ Community-Connected
Neighborhood champions, weekly live check-ins, cohort accountability structure
♿ Accessible
Screen reader optimized, high contrast options, no prerequisite technology skills required

The Five Modules

1

Professional Presence

Character: Jaylen — 16, just hired at H-E-B · Standard: (A) Professional Conduct, (C) Communication
  • 7-Second Rule — strong first impressions in professional settings
  • AI Mock Interview Lab (Starter, Standard, and Tough levels) — unlimited private practice
  • AI Email Coach — write 3 professional emails with real-time feedback
  • Reframe existing skills: babysitting = childcare experience; church volunteering = event coordination
  • Resume Builder Part 1: Header, Objective Statement, Education
Real Talk: "What if you can't afford interview clothes?" Resources for free professional clothing, thrift store tips, "clean and pressed matters more than brand names."
2

Workplace Rights & Responsibilities

Character: Aaliyah — 17, works at a clothing store · Standard: (H) Discrimination & Harassment
  • Workplace discrimination under Title VII, ADA, and ADEA — interactive 8-case scenarios
  • 5 D's of bystander intervention: Distract, Delegate, Document, Delay, Direct
  • Digital rights — employer monitoring, social media policies, NLRA wage discussion protections
  • Personal Digital Presence Audit — Google yourself, review social media, set up LinkedIn
  • Resume Builder Part 2: Skills Section (hard + soft skills with evidence)
Real Talk: "But I need this job" — documenting violations, anonymous EEOC reporting, free legal aid resources (Texas RioGrande Legal Aid, Lone Star Legal Aid).
3

Workplace Safety Essentials

Character: Marcus — 16, works at a warehouse · Standard: (I) Workplace Safety
  • OSHA rights including the right to refuse dangerous work
  • Hazard identification across retail, food service, warehouse, and construction
  • PPE Matching Challenge — scenario-based equipment selection
  • Emergency procedures — fire, medical, active threat (interactive AI decision trees)
  • Free certification guide: OSHA 10-Hour, Texas Food Handler, Google Digital Garage
  • Resume Builder Part 3: Certifications & Training Section
Real Talk: "But I'm new — I can't say no to my boss." Yes you can, and here's why it protects both of you.
4

Time & Priority Management

Character: Sofia — 17, AP classes + Chick-fil-A + caring for her little brother · Standard: (D) Time Management
  • Eisenhower Matrix with the student's own real priorities (school, work, family, social)
  • Digital Calendar Setup — color-coded, realistic, not idealized
  • SMART Goal Workshop with AI coaching and accountability partners
  • Google XYZ formula for writing accomplishments ("Accomplished X as measured by Y by doing Z")
  • Resume Builder Part 4: Projects & Accomplishments Section
Real Talk: "I don't have time" — acknowledging that not everyone has the same 24 hours. Finding 30–60 recoverable minutes per day without pretending family isn't a real obligation.
5

Work Ethic & Career Leadership

Character: DeAndre — 18, about to graduate, asked to train new employees · Standards: (E), (G), (J)
  • 4 pillars of work ethic: punctuality, dependability, reliability, responsibility
  • How promotions actually work — org chart navigation, not mythology
  • Leadership Style Quiz (AI-powered, 15 questions) — Servant, Transformational, Democratic, Coaching
  • Career Character Pledge — personal commitment statement
  • Final resume + tailored cover letter for a real job posting with AI review
  • Resume Builder Part 5: Final polish, Work/Volunteer Experience, References, Cover Letter
